## 原文

Memory capacity, bandwidth and connectivity are becoming as critical as compute in AI infrastructure. Larger models, longer context windows and growing KV caches are driving demands that traditional server architectures were not designed to meet. By moving data closer to compute and reducing latency, Marvell is helping customers improve utilization, boost token efficiency and scale AI infrastructure without compromising performance, power or cost.

Marvell will showcase its AI memory and storage portfolio at FMS 2026, August 4-6 in Santa Clara, at Booth 805. Demonstrations will span PCIe 6.0 enterprise SSD controllers, CXL near-memory acceleration, CXL memory expansion and compression, CXL switching for memory pooling, Octeon DPU-powered network storage, and Photonic Fabric technology for optical scale-up.

Marvell executives will also lead sessions throughout the conference, including a keynote on August 5 from Vice President of Technology Mark Kuemerle: "The Accidental Architecture of AI."
