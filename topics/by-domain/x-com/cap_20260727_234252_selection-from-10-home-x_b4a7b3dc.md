I saw a summary of the CXMT conference call from Goldman Sachs today, and there were just so many solid insights packed in.
我今天看到了高盛关于 CXMT 电话会议的摘要，其中包含了非常多扎实的见解。

Starting with production capacity.
从生产能力开始。

China's leading DRAM companies are expected to more than double their production capacity by 2030 compared to now.
中国领先的 DRAM 公司预计到 2030 年将比现在翻一番以上的产能。

They're continuously building factories in Hefei, Shanghai, and Beijing, with most of them up and running before 2028,
他们正在合肥、上海和北京持续建设工厂，其中大部分将在 2028 年之前投产运行，

and after that, even larger mega-factories are planned to come online. It really feels like they're just going to steamroll with sheer volume.
此后，还计划启用更大的超大型工厂。感觉他们就是要靠纯粹的规模碾压一切。

And the equipment side is interesting too.
设备端也同样引人关注。

For new lines, they're not just using overseas equipment—they're rapidly increasing the share of domestic equipment.
在新产线方面，他们不仅使用海外设备，还在迅速提高国产设备的占比。

Some factories are apparently running almost entirely on Chinese-made gear, except for lithography tools.
一些工厂除了光刻机之外，几乎全部采用中国制造的设备。

Goldman views this trend as irreversible, and they're saying Chinese equipment companies like NAURA, AMEC, and ACM Research will directly benefit.
高盛认为这一趋势不可逆转，并表示中国的设备公司如北方华创、中微公司和盛美半导体将直接受益。

On product tech, there's still a gap compared to Samsung or Hynix in read/write performance, but their goals are clear.
在产品技术方面，与三星或海力士相比，读写性能仍有差距，但他们的目标很明确。

They're targeting mass production of HBM3 and HBM3E by 2026,
他们的目标是到 2026 年实现 HBM3 和 HBM3E 的大规模量产，

and since they can't use EUV, they're developing high-performance products with DUV and other process tech.
而且由于无法使用 EUV，他们正在利用 DUV 等其他工艺技术开发高性能产品。

There's also a forecast that they could technically break through on 3D DRAM by 2027.
还有预测称，他们可能在 2027 年之前实现 3D DRAM 的技术突破。

It's a way to somewhat bypass EUV restrictions.
这是一种在一定程度上绕过 EUV 限制的方法。

They talked about pricing too,
他们也谈到了定价问题，

saying Chinese-made DRAM prices are already nearly on par with global players.
称中国制造的 DRAM 价格已经几乎与国际厂商持平。

But yields are still low.
但良品率仍然很低。

For Q3-Q4, smartphone makers don't want to raise prices, so the upward momentum might slow a bit compared to the first half,
对于第三至第四季度，智能手机制造商不愿提价，因此上涨势头与上半年相比可能略有放缓，

but with AI demand staying strong in China and abroad, they see memory prices likely rising even into 2027.
但随着国内外人工智能需求保持强劲，他们认为内存价格可能持续上涨至 2027 年。

The odds of global companies diverting HBM capacity to regular DRAM are low,
全球企业将高带宽内存（HBM）产能转用于常规 DRAM 的可能性较低，

and with the share of long-term supply contracts increasing, prices should gradually stabilize, per their view.
且随着长期供应合同占比的增加，他们认为价格应会逐步趋稳。

Finally, on institutional assessments:
最后，关于机构评估：

CXMT's listing isn't just an IPO—it's a symbolic event marking China's semiconductor self-reliance entering a true commercialization phase.
CXMT 的上市不仅仅是一次 IPO——它标志着中国半导体自主化进入真正的商业化阶段，是一个具有象征意义的事件。

Going forward, Chinese memory and advanced logic companies will meet domestic CSPs' demand for generative AI, benefiting the entire 'China for China' supply chain—from foundry, equipment, materials, components, IP, to fabless.
展望未来，中国存储和先进逻辑公司将满足国内 CSP（云服务提供商）对生成式 AI 的需求，从而惠及整个“中国为中国”供应链——从代工、设备、材料、元器件、IP 到无晶圆厂。

Overall, it feels like the picture is getting clearer: China ramping up DRAM volume,
总体而言，情况似乎越来越清晰：中国正在提升 DRAM 产能，

gradually catching up on tech,
逐步追赶技术，

and localizing equipment to keep it all in-house.
并实现设备本地化，以保持内部自主可控。
