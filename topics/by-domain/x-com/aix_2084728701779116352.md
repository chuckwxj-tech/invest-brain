# AI infrastructure vendors love framing architectural workarounds as noble breakthroughs, conveniently ignoring the underlying financial panic. The reality is far less glamorous:...

## 用户备注

来源: ai-x-discovery；机器初筛，仍需外部核查。

## 原文

AI infrastructure vendors love framing architectural workarounds as noble breakthroughs, conveniently ignoring the underlying financial panic. 

The reality is far less glamorous: long-context LLM inference is slamming headfirst into the Memory Wall.

Compute clusters waste valuable cycles starving for bus bandwidth, hopelessly shuffling terabytes of vector embeddings back and forth.

The SK hynix and Marvell team-up for CXL PNM (CMM-Ax) is an exercise in ruthless optimization. 

The fix? 

Embed a dedicated ASIC (Marvell Structera A) right onto the CXL memory module. 

Processing-Near-Memory. 

Data stays where it lives, with calculations executed locally instead of burning interconnect bandwidth to the main compute engine. 

Eliminating out-of-memory bottlenecks isn't magic—it’s just the logical result of killing redundant data transit.

Hardware without a pipeline is a liability.

SK hynix absorbing SK Telecom’s stake in Penguin Solutions ($PENG) right before launch exposes the real playbook. Selling bare silicon is a low-margin trap.

By securing rack-level deployment channels and full-stack system integration, they are quietly building a turn-key distribution moat for CXL at scale.

Will this integrated CXL architecture finally solve memory starvation, or are we simply shifting the structural bottleneck further down the system stack?
