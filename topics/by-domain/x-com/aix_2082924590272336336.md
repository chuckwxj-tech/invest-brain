## 原文

Samsung Electro-Mechanics is concentrating on AI data centers and automotive.

On its second-quarter call the company said it will direct resources toward these two high-growth markets and expand capacity ahead of demand. It expects sales and margins to improve further in the third quarter.

MLCC and high-end FCBGA supply remains tight. Long-term agreements with about ten hyperscalers and major chipmakers are already supporting results. The priority is ultra-high-capacity, high-temperature, and high-voltage MLCCs for AI servers and ADAS. 

Demand for the 1005-size 47μF part is projected to rise sevenfold by 2027, so next-generation 68μF and 150μF devices plus 1kV-plus parts are being prepared.
Glass substrate is the newest initiative. 

A joint venture with Dongwoo Fine-Chem in which Samsung Electro-Mechanics holds a 66.2% stake is targeting mass production in 2028.

The company is positioning itself for the next phase of AI infrastructure rather than relying only on the current cycle.
