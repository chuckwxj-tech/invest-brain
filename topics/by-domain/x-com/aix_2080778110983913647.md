Why KLIC deserves more attention:

• Fluxless Thermo-Compression Bonding (FTC/TCB): One of the company’s biggest opportunities. 

KLIC is among the leaders in production-proven fluxless TCB systems, enabling ultra-fine copper-to-copper connections used in AI accelerators, chiplets, HBM, and advanced heterogeneous packaging. Management expects TCB to become a major long-term growth driver.

• Vertical Wire Technology: A lesser-known technology targeting high-density DRAM and NAND stacking. It offers improved bandwidth, smaller form factors, and potentially lower manufacturing costs while gaining traction with memory manufacturers.

• Power Semiconductor Assembly: KLIC continues expanding its wedge-bonding leadership through its new ASTERION-TW platform, targeting high-current power modules used in AI data centers, power delivery, EVs, and industrial electrification.

• Hybrid Bonding Path: Through collaborations such as ROHM’s CuFirst process, KLIC is positioning its FTC platform as a lower-cost, higher-yield alternative for certain hybrid bonding applications.

The reason many investors overlook KLIC is simple.

Most still see a cyclical wire-bonding company.

Fiscal Q2 2026 showed one of the strongest growth rates across semiconductor equipment:

• Revenue: $242.6M (+49.8% YoY, +21.5% sequential)

• Gross Margin: 49.3%

• Non-GAAP EPS: $0.79, beating expectations.

• Fiscal Q3 guidance: ~$310M ± $20M, implying another major sequential increase.

• FY2026 CapEx guidance remains just $20-24M, highlighting an asset-light business with strong free cash flow potential.

Growth vs. the industry leaders

Compared with $AMAT, $LRCX, $KLAC, and $ASML, $KLIC is much smaller, but it’s currently growing considerably faster on a percentage basis.

• Revenue growth approaching 50% YoY

• Sequential growth above 20%

• Competitive gross margins around 49%

• Low capital requirements

Management expects:

• Thermo-Compression Bonding revenue to exceed $100M during FY2026.

• Approximately 70% sequential growth within the TCB business.

• Capacity investments underway supporting roughly $400M of annual TCB revenue potential by the first half of FY2027.

The stock isn’t the deep value opportunity it was earlier in the recovery after its strong 2026 rally, but valuation remains reasonable if management continues executing on advanced packaging growth.

Technically, the stock experienced a massive rally from roughly $35 into the $135-137 range before entering a sharp correction.

Current observations:

• MACD remains bearish with no confirmed bullish crossover.

• Volume expanded during the decline, confirming recent distribution.

• Price has fallen below the faster moving averages while the longer-term trend remains intact.

The $92 area stands out as the most important support.

If buyers defend that level, the broader uptrend remains intact and a recovery toward $110-120 becomes increasingly realistic before eventually challenging prior highs.

If $92 fails with heavy volume, the next meaningful support zones become the rising long-term moving averages in the upper $70s, followed by the $60-70 prior breakout region.

If advanced packaging adoption continues accelerating into 2027, today’s business could look meaningfully different than what most investors still assume.

For now, $92 remains the level I’m watching. If that support holds, I think this pullback could prove to be a solid long-term accumulation opportunity heading into the next earnings report in early August.
