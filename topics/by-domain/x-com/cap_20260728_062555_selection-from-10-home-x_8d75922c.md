TFHK Commentary: How Should We Understand the Current Correction in AI Hardware?
TFHK 评论：我们应如何理解当前 AI 硬件的回调？

The market is always right. Changes in stock prices inevitably reflect the new variables the market is currently pricing in. Even as long-term bulls on the AI industry, we need to understand the core concerns driving this correction in AI semiconductor stocks.
市场永远正确。股价变化必然反映市场当前正在定价的新变量。即使作为 AI 行业的长期看多者，我们也需要理解推动 AI 半导体股票本轮回调的核心担忧。

The current market bears a striking resemblance to last autumn and winter. Following OpenAI’s large fundraising round, industry conditions were very strong, yet stocks continued to trade sideways. Market participants spent every day debating CapEx, ROI, valuations, and financing—much like they are doing now.
当前市场与去年秋冬惊人相似。OpenAI 完成大规模融资后，行业基本面非常强劲，但股票却持续横盘交易。市场参与者每天都在争论资本支出、投资回报率、估值和融资——正如现在一样。

The conclusions from this quarter’s earnings reports remain overwhelmingly positive. GCP grew by 80%, the ROI of cloud investment was validated, Intel delivered a significant beat, and ASML, TSMC, and Intel raised their order or CapEx outlooks. Presumably, these companies also saw extremely strong downstream forecasts, giving even the most conservative players in the supply chain the confidence to make aggressive bets. Had this information emerged in May or June, semiconductor stocks would almost certainly have surged.
本季度财报的结论仍然非常积极。GCP 增长了 80%，云投资的投资回报率得到验证，英特尔业绩大幅超预期，ASML、台积电和英特尔上调了订单或资本支出展望。可以推测，这些公司还看到了极其强劲的下游预测，即使是供应链中最保守的参与者也有信心进行激进押注。如果这些信息在 5 月或 6 月出现，半导体股票几乎肯定会飙升。

Now, however, every earnings release has instead become an opportunity for bears to reassess valuations and the long-term investment thesis. What we may be seeing is that the AI market is no longer in the “AI Summer” of May and June. The same positive developments now provide less support to share prices. Take GCP’s 80% growth as an example. Previously, the market’s first reaction would have been: “AI demand has exceeded expectations—the catalyst has arrived.” Now, the first response is: “So what? What about 2028? Can OpenAI become profitable? For how many more years can GPU prices keep rising? Margins are rising again, financing costs are increasing, and the entire CapEx thesis needs to be repriced.” In essence, the market has shifted from trading the growth of AI CapEx to trading its sustainability and ROI.
然而现在，每一次财报发布反而成了空头重新评估估值和长期投资逻辑的契机。我们看到的或许是，AI 市场已不再处于五六月的“AI 盛夏”。同样的积极进展如今对股价的支撑力度明显减弱。以 GCP 80%的增速为例，此前市场的第一反应会是：“AI 需求超出预期——催化剂来了。”如今的第一反应却是：“那又怎样？2028 年呢？OpenAI 能盈利吗？GPU 价格还能涨几年？利润率又在攀升，融资成本持续增加，整个资本支出逻辑都需要重新定价。”本质上，市场已从交易 AI 资本支出的增长，转向交易其可持续性和投资回报率。

Market sentiment, as we perceive it, has already become extremely bearish. Even long-term bulls are beginning to question whether AI semiconductor stocks can continue to rise, and we are hearing almost no calls for new highs. When the market shifts from looking for further upside to searching for additional downside risks, it usually means that pessimistic expectations have already been largely priced in.
据我们所感知，市场情绪已极度看跌。即便是长期看涨者也开始质疑 AI 半导体股票能否持续上涨，而我们几乎听不到任何关于创新高的呼声。当市场从寻找进一步上行空间转向搜寻更多下行风险时，通常意味着悲观预期已大部分被消化。

Nevertheless, we have no doubts about the fundamentals. We also believe that, ultimately, facts determine stock prices. So what would send these stocks higher again? Under the framework outlined above, additional capital-spending plans alone will no longer be enough to convince the bears. What is needed is validation of a new demand curve. The most powerful and direct catalyst would be the emergence of a blockbuster product. If “Coding 1.0” proved that AI can improve developer productivity, then “Coding 2.0” must demonstrate that AI agents can genuinely replace part of the software development process. Once new productivity use cases are validated, the market’s concerns about AI ROI may be redefined, and AI infrastructure spending will once again be viewed as “productivity investment” rather than a “cost.”
尽管如此，我们对基本面并无怀疑。我们也相信，最终决定股价的是事实。那么，什么因素能再度推高这些股票？在上述框架下，仅凭额外的资本支出计划已不足以说服空头。真正需要的是新需求曲线的验证。最有力且最直接的催化剂，当属一款爆款产品的诞生。如果说“编程 1.0”证明了 AI 能提升开发者效率，那么“编程 2.0”必须证明：AI 代理能够真正替代部分软件开发流程。一旦新的生产力应用场景得到验证，市场对 AI 投资回报率的疑虑将可能被重新定义，AI 基础设施支出也会再度被视为“生产力投资”，而非“成本”。
