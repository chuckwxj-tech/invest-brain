Street Signal | ASMPT Beats Expectations as Advanced Packaging and Photonics Drive Growth, J.P. Morgan Says

ASMPT's second-quarter results and third-quarter guidance both came in far above market expectations, helped by operating leverage, a recovery in its core SEMI and SMT businesses, and continued strong growth in TCB (thermocompression bonding) and photonics.

Photonics revenue in the first half rose threefold year over year to USD 75 million, and the company already has a leading position in CPO (co-packaged optics).

J.P. Morgan raised its 2026-2028 EPS estimates by 7% to 12%. The note says ASMPT sits at the intersection of advanced packaging and silicon photonics, with TCB and photonics set to be core earnings drivers over the next few years. It argues that the outperformance points to a new growth cycle.

The note also says the stock and the broader advanced packaging and optical interconnect equipment sector could benefit, and that the market may still be underpricing ASMPT's position in AI and CPO.

Key catalysts include faster memory TCB order conversion, especially from HBM customers; higher penetration of logic-chip C2W (chip-to-wafer) bonding; and continued growth in CPO-related equipment orders.
