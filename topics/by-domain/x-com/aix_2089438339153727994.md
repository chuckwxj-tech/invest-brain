$PENG Rosenblatt Conference Key Takeaways. Looks highly likely they will win opportunities with SK Telecom's expanded AI announcements:

- Have tremendous depth specific to $NVDA clusters. Have accumulated 4B+ hours of managing GPU clusters at scale. Also completely open to all architectures. Delivered a Spectra cluster based on NextSilicon advanced accelerators. 

- MemoryAI and KV Cache Server. The KV Cache CXL Server adds 11 TB that can store tokens at low latency. Within the KV Cache area, there are approaches people take using 100x slower flash based storage, but because of Penguin's experience and awareness that latency can be an issue is their solution is 100% based on the fastest DDR5 memory for lowest time to first token and offloading lots of work. 

- Does Penguin's differentiation reside in Hardware or Software/Services in the future: It will be a combo of our hardware expertise and software/services. We can work side by side with our customer and be able to create a tailored configuration for customers' workloads. The things we do at the intersection of AI and Memory, we do better than those that our out there.

- With neo-cloud and sovereign, there is overlap there. When we talk about Sovereign, it is typically in an isolated way. The good example is the SK Telecom Haein cluster that we designed and manage today. We are seeing Enterprise sovereign also where there are secure internal needs. The characteristics of neo-clouds are scale, where they understand in most cases what they want. We work regularly with neo-clouds who have access to power, data center, customer, but need our help with the technology aspect to bring capacity online. Sovereign tends to be more hand holding where our design, build, deploy, manage playbook comes into play. With Enterprise, we can clearly see that Agentic AI and AI Inference is moving from experimentation to production. Neo-clouds are looking for help in the application layer helping them with both hardware and software infrastructure. It is a flywheel, as use case 1 comes online then there are 50 use cases behind that that will be deployed over time.

- $SKM Discussion, Following Haein, how should investors think about SK Telecom and a repeatable blueprint for Sovereign AI projects? Do you see Penguin benefiting in Korea and APAC through the SK Telecom partnership? 

SK Telecom is an investor in Penguin Solutions and our collaborations have transcended that investment. The Haein cluster is award winning for its rapid deployment time and uptime. ClusterWare is fully deployed. There are tremendous opportunities for us to work with SK Telecom on other opportunities. People can search and see that SK Telecom has announced intents to expand their business meaningfully all over APAC specific to AI deployments. Also SK Hynix is a company who we have had a long term relationship and partnership and as they look to get further into the AI deployment space, it will be a large opportunity where we can add value. 

- Talk about the $DELL partnership. Where does Penguin contribute where Dell does not want to or can? Is Penguin becoming the software of standardized DELL hardware.

The partnership with DELL is tremendous. We have great partnerships with CDW and WWT also. As we engage with these partners, the focus is to create wins for them and how we can help. We can offer our MemoryAI platform and our end-to-end services not just to build a system, but also to provide a private cloud experience. Guaranteeing uptime and adhering to an SLA.

- Is the Software offering licensed or hardware attached? Discuss the landscape? Discuss the land and expand model in practice?

Software can run alongside managed services. Someone might manage an open source platform and set up a base cluster, but that gets them to step 1 in a multi step journey. We constantly add value back, tuning, guaranteeing uptime, etc. We are building in constantly the experience we are seeing from being in the market. The deployments are not dissimilar in AI from Penguin's history over time. Customers go with Penguin because they won't let them fail. Customers trust Penguin will operate with their best interest in mind. Penguin delivers and then gets to be in those conversations to expand business as a true partner. Penguin's CEO has been talking more openly about their land and expand strategy with new logos onboarded over the 12-month period and showing what % of those have expanded the offering meaningfully. The numbers are quite significant.

- As we think about Enterprises adopting AI, is it data readiness, use case?

Customers are at every phase in this. For many, they are just getting into this at scale. Agentic AI is AI doing work. The holy grail for Enterprise is deploying systems with agency and are doing work 24/7 on their behalf. Customers make a decision they want to do AI, then we do ideation for which workloads provide the highest ROI, then putting together the agent flow and data (Tapping into CRM, ERP, Healthcare info system, etc). The rationale for enterprises wanting to bring it on-prem is because of security. Having a secure deployment gives Enterprises the most power and also cost predictability. If I knew I needed to drive every day to my job, renting every day from a car agency is more expensive than me buying a car. Once I decide this is something I am doing, owning is the correct move, as Enterprises are committed to doing AI at scale forever.
