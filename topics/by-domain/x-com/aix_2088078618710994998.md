## 原文

$NVDA Feynman is expected to move onto TSMC’s A16 process (an upgraded 2nm node), increase SoIC 3D stacking, use custom HBM, and go straight to co-packaged optics (CPO).

For TSMC, NVIDIA shortens product iteration cycles and simultaneously pushes up advanced process and package expansion pressures. 

The supply chain pointed out that TSMC is currently accelerating the construction of CAP7 and Nanke AP8; SoIC, CoWoS-L, and the next generation of CoPoS cleanrooms are all laid out in advance, and the factory, cleanroom, and equipment entry operations continue to "rush the progress".

TSMC's monthly production capacity has continued to increase since 2023, originally planned to establish about 10,000 to 15,000 monthly  production capacity in 2026, reaching 20,000 by the end of 2026; driven  by NVIDIA, ultra-micro (AMD) and other demands and Feynman mass  production schedule.
