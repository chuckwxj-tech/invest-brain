Just some TLDRs of stuff I found interesting this week:

- $SNDK projecting 80% adjusted gross margins through 2030, ~75% operating margins and ~50% FCF margins. LTAs already cover 2/3 of 2028 output with $93B minimum contracted revenue. Hard to stay cyclical when demand is locked in for years.

- Conventional DRAM gross margins (e.g. $MU) estimated to hit unprecedented ~95% by 2027, surpassing HBM. Legacy players like Nanya/Winbond should benefit if this plays out.

- Anthropic reportedly hit 14x+ YoY growth and 2.4x sequential, with Q2 revenue >$11.5B. Trajectory pointing toward $190–200B by 2028. Frontier labs keep growing at absurd rates.

- $NVDA in talks for $3B investment into SB Energy + major compute financing push with big PE names. Feynman moving to TSMC A16 + SoIC + custom HBM + CPO in H2 2028.

- $MSFT Maia 300 targeting >300k units in 2027 (expanding to 1M+). Potential beneficiaries in the ASIC supply chain ($MRVL, Alchip, GUC etc.).

- $TSM Advanced Packaging VP: industry facing multi-year shortages not just in memory but also ABF substrates. Upstream equipment makers already seeing full capacity + price hikes.

- $AMAT raising advanced packaging revenue growth outlook to >70% in 2026 (from >50%), with customer discussions now extending to 2030.

- Power shift accelerating: Google notes 48V running out of headroom. $NVDA detailing 800VDC MGX-compatible racks for H2 2026.

- Memory industry fully adopting LTA playbook (Nanya 50% covered, CXMT multi-year deals). Probe cards and MLCCs also tight (lead times up to 36 weeks).

TLDR: AI supply chains are locking in multi-year contracted demand across memory, packaging, substrates, power, and components. Bottlenecks are structural, not temporary.
