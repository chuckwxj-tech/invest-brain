Huawei AI Chips Adopt Glass Substrates 2027

Huawei dominates China's AI chip market because NVIDIA is absent not due to superior technology. Huawei plans to adopt glass substrates for AI chips to become a global competitor. 

Huawei works with domestic suppliers BOE and Visionox for glass substrate production. Huawei aims for glass substrate AI chip mass production in 2027. 

This timeline would give Huawei a one year advantage over Samsung and rivals planning 2028 production. 

Glass substrates enable denser 3D chip stacking and reduce manufacturing warpage. 

Glass substrates also reduce current leakage improving power efficiency and data rates. 

This technology is essential for training large language models.
