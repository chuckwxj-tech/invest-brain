Street Signal | Lens Technology cooperates with Intel to make significant progress in the glass substrate technology route, but the short-term contribution is limited (Morgan Stanley)

Lens Technology announced that it is cooperating with Intel to develop glass core substrates. This news directly drives display supply chain manufacturers such as Innoux, BOE, and AUO to actively deploy advanced packaging glass substrate technology.

Morgan Stanley believes that this cooperation will help improve market sentiment, but it will still take time for substantial revenue contribution. Although glass substrates have cost and electrical performance advantages, they currently face accuracy and yield challenges.

The market's hype for the "concept" may have been sufficient, but the mass production schedule and yield improvement are key. One-sentence conclusion: Glass substrates are the long-term trend for advanced packaging, but they are still thematic investments in the short term. Real performance needs to wait for yield breakthroughs and mass production.

Positive/negative: Positive for display and glass processing companies such as Lens Technology (https://t.co/zz6Lbra4gU), Innoux, BOE, and AUO. The current stock price has been partially priced in the cooperation news, and whether it can continue to rise in the future depends on the progress of technological breakthroughs. Catalysts:

1) Quarterly data on each manufacturer's glass substrate yield improvement;

2) Intel or other major manufacturers' specific mass production schedule for advanced packaging of glass substrates;

3) Supply chain manufacturers' progress in obtaining value-added shares in the mass production stage.
