## 原文

Taiwan’s IC design houses are now treating R&D compute as a strategic asset.

MediaTek, Global Unichip and Novatek are all building or expanding private data centers to handle the exploding complexity of AI chips and advanced nodes.

MediaTek switched on its Tongluo R&D data center in May. It runs Taiwan’s first NVIDIA DGX B200 SuperPOD, processing 138 billion tokens a month and completing more than 24,000 training iterations. Single-phase immersion cooling handles the density.

Global Unichip’s Zhunan Turing Center entered trial operation in Q2 and moves to full use in the second half. It is built for 3nm/2nm designs and high-end packaging so the company can run more projects in parallel and chase larger AI ASIC deals.

Novatek broke ground on its Tongluo Phase 1 computing center at the end of July. The civil and mechanical contracts already total nearly NT$2.9 billion. The goal is high-end display SoCs, edge AI and custom ASICs.

At 3nm and below, circuit scale, HBM and advanced packaging multiply the compute needed for layout, timing, power and verification. Keeping that work in-house also reduces the security risk of putting sensitive designs on public cloud.

Short-term costs rise. Longer term, faster iteration and more concurrent projects become a competitive edge. Compute capacity is turning into the next battleground for Taiwan’s design firms.
