## 原文

UTI has cleared final mass production approval for foldable UTG with its North American customer.

The company said it has completed quality verification and process stabilization. 

Supply of the ultra-thin glass for foldable smart devices has begun, centered on its Vietnam production line. Volume is set to expand from the second half of the year.

This effectively ends earlier market concerns about delays in the customer’s production schedule.

UTI plans to use the milestone to push capital structure improvements and attract new investment. At the same time it will continue developing next-generation UTG, through-glass via (TGV), and co-packaged optics (CPO).

The move strengthens its position in the foldable display supply chain as demand for thinner, more durable cover glass grows with new device launches.

How quickly can UTI scale this Vietnam line to meet rising foldable volumes, and will the TGV and CPO efforts open meaningful new revenue streams beyond UTG?
