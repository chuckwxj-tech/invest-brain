Meta and Micron just published a paper about a memory chip most investors have never heard of and it explains why the AI memory shortage isn't ending anytime soon (Save this).
Meta 和美光刚刚发布了一篇关于大多数投资者从未听说过的内存芯片的论文，它解释了为什么 AI 内存短缺不会很快结束（收藏此文）。

The chip is called LPDDR5X, the same type of memory that's been sitting in phones for years, now being repurposed for data center servers.
这种芯片名为 LPDDR5X，是多年来一直用于手机的同类型内存，现在被重新用于数据中心服务器。

The reason it matters is power because LPDDR5X uses roughly a third of the electricity of the DDR5 memory data centers have relied on for decades while accounting for less than 7% of total system power draw.
它之所以重要是因为功耗，因为 LPDDR5X 的耗电量大约只有数据中心几十年来一直依赖的 DDR5 内存的三分之一，同时占系统总功耗不到 7%。

That efficiency gain compounds fast at scale, since power and cooling are the two hardest constraints hyperscalers are hitting as they build out more AI capacity.
这种能效提升在大规模部署时会迅速叠加，因为电力和冷却是超大规模企业在建设更多 AI 能力时面临的两个最严峻限制。

Meta's own benchmarks found doubling LPDDR5X capacity eliminated disk spill entirely in Spark SQL data workloads, delivering two to three times the throughput without touching the CPU.
Meta 自己的基准测试发现，将 LPDDR5X 容量翻倍可以完全消除 Spark SQL 数据工作负载中的磁盘溢出，在不接触 CPU 的情况下实现两到三倍的吞吐量。

Faster configurations also cut tail latency by about 9%, which matters directly for services running under strict response time requirements.
更快的配置还将尾部延迟降低了约 9%，这对于在严格响应时间要求下运行的服务而言意义重大。

That's the part that explains why demand keeps climbing instead of leveling off.
这部分解释了为什么需求持续攀升而非趋于平稳。

Once hyperscalers see more capacity and faster speeds translating directly into 2-3x throughput gains and lower latency, the logical move isn't to hold memory footprints flat, it's to keep buying more of it, since every additional gigabyte and every speed bump now pays for itself in performance.
一旦超大规模云服务商发现更高的容量和更快的速度直接转化为 2-3 倍的吞吐量提升和更低的延迟，合乎逻辑的做法就不是保持内存占用不变，而是继续购买更多内存——因为每增加一个吉字节、每一次速度提升，现在都能在性能上收回成本。

The real unlock is a new modular format called SOCAMM2, which lets data centers install and swap this mobile style memory the same way they'd service any other server component, something the old soldered down mobile memory design never allowed.
真正的突破口在于一种名为 SOCAMM2 的新型模块化设计。它使得数据中心能够像维护任何其他服务器组件一样，安装和更换这种移动式内存——这是以往焊接固定的移动内存设计所无法实现的。

That alone removes a structural barrier that used to cap how much LPDDR memory a data center could realistically deploy, which means demand can now scale in a way it physically couldn't before.
单凭这一点就移除了一项结构性障碍，该障碍过去限制了数据中心实际部署 LPDDR 内存的容量，这意味着需求现在可以以以前物理上不可能的方式扩展。

This lands right in the middle of what's already the tightest memory market in over a decade.
这正赶上十多年来最紧张的内存市场之中。

DRAM contract prices jumped as much as 95% in the first quarter of 2026 alone, driven almost entirely by AI data centers eating up supply faster than manufacturers can produce it.
仅 2026 年第一季度，DRAM 合同价格就飙升了高达 95%，这几乎完全是由于 AI 数据中心吞噬供应的速度超过了制造商的生产能力。

Server memory, including SOCAMM and HBM, now makes up more than half of all global DRAM demand and Goldman Sachs expects that undersupply to persist through 2027.
服务器内存（包括 SOCAMM 和 HBM）如今占全球 DRAM 需求的一半以上，高盛预计这种供应短缺将持续到 2027 年。

With that being said, Micron is the direct winner here because its data center revenue hit an annualized run rate over $100 billion last quarter, up sevenfold year over year, with DRAM prices alone climbing roughly 60% sequentially.
话虽如此，美光无疑是直接赢家，因为其数据中心营收在上季度达到了超过 1000 亿美元的年化运行率，同比增长七倍，仅 DRAM 价格就环比上涨约 60%。

That data center segment includes SOCAMM shipments, HBM, and now the LPDDR5X server pipeline this paper is built around, meaning Micron isn't just riding the shortage, it's actively engineering the next format hyperscalers will need to keep buying.
该数据中心部门包括 SOCAMM 出货、HBM，以及这份白皮书所围绕的 LPDDR5X 服务器管线，这意味着美光不仅在利用供应短缺，还在积极设计超大规模云服务商未来需要持续采购的下一种规格。

Micron's data center SSD business alone crossed $5 billion in quarterly revenue on top of its core memory sales, showing the shortage is lifting more than one product line at once.
仅美光的数据中心 SSD 业务季度营收就超过 50 亿美元，加上其核心内存销售，表明供应短缺正在同时提振多个产品线。
