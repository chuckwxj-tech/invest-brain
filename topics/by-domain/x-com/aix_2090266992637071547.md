## 原文

The AI race just moved beyond chips.
Compute is scaling 3× every two years. 

Interconnect bandwidth? Only ~1.4×. 

That’s the ‘Copper Wall’ as hyperscale AI clusters span racks and pods.

@SKhynix is doubling down on Interconnects to make memory available in disaggregated form at high speed, low latency and low power.

The company just co-authored a major roadmap on co-packaged optics (CPO) in Nature Electronics with researchers from UVA, MIT, UIUC, NTU, and Yonsei. Seunghoon Hong (SK hynix AI Infra Team Lead) is a corresponding author.

The paper goes beyond HBM. 

It lays out how optical engines co-packaged with processors replace long copper links with light, thus, targeting >100 Tb/s per node, <1 pJ/bit, and <10 ns chip-to-chip latency.

The recent $SKHY $MRVL partnership with CXL-Memory also will extend to Optical CXL.

Why SK hynix is driving this:

▪️HBM is already solving the ‘memory wall’ inside the package.

▪️The next bottleneck is data movement between the disaggregated compute.

▪️An optics-centric architecture with photonic interposers could eventually link memory pools directly to compute, letting accelerators share large, flexible memory resources more efficiently.

SK hynix trying to go from “best HBM supplier” to contribute in shaping the system architecture of next-gen optically connected AI infrastructure.
