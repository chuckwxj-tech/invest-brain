## 原文

Memory took center stage at FMS 2026.

Five months after GTC focused attention on GPUs, the Future of Memory and Storage conference in Santa Clara shifted the spotlight to bandwidth and capacity. Samsung and SK Hynix delivered the opening keynotes and drew the largest crowds on the show floor.

Samsung highlighted its zHBM concept that stacks memory directly above AI accelerators, along with the industry’s first V10 BV-NAND exceeding 400 layers. A compact 256 TB SSD using V9 QLC and a 5 nm controller was shown as an example of how expensive high-capacity storage has become. Heat management through structures described as chimney-like paths was a recurring technical theme.

SK Hynix emphasized CXL memory modules for pooling and its newly announced High Bandwidth Flash standard developed with SanDisk.

Engineers clustered around the CMM displays to ask about performance and cost. HBF hardware itself was not yet on the floor, as the standard has only just been released.

More than 100 companies participated, up from about 70 previously, and attendance rose above 3,500. 

The common message was that AI’s bottleneck is moving from pure compute to memory hierarchy. SSDs are increasingly positioned as part of an extended AI memory stack rather than simple storage.
