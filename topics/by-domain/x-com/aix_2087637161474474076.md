$COHR Q4 2026 earnings: Explosive AI Demand Powers Record Results, Masking Industrial Weakness

Coherent delivered an exceptional Q4, driven entirely by the AI datacenter boom. Revenue surged 34% YoY to $2.05B, while Non-GAAP EPS skyrocketed 74% YoY to $1.74, crushing expectations. Operational leverage is shining through, with Non-GAAP gross margins expanding 215 bps YoY to 40.2%. The AI supercycle continues to accelerate, prompting a blowout Q1 FY27 guidance of $2.2B-$2.4B in revenue. However, beneath the headline beats, a deep divergence exists: the Datacenter & Communications segment is accelerating rapidly, while the Industrial segment is steadily decelerating, down 16% YoY.

⚖️ 𝐕𝐞𝐫𝐝𝐢𝐜𝐭: 🟢🟢
Strong Bullish. The sheer velocity of AI-driven datacenter demand is overpowering the weakness in the Industrial segment. With robust pricing power, scaling 6-inch wafer margins, and massive forward guidance, Coherent's execution is firing on all critical cylinders.

𝐊𝐞𝐲 𝐓𝐡𝐞𝐦𝐞𝐬

🟢🟢 𝐓𝐫𝐚𝐧𝐬𝐜𝐞𝐢𝐯𝐞𝐫 𝐒𝐮𝐩𝐞𝐫𝐜𝐲𝐜𝐥𝐞 𝐀𝐜𝐜𝐞𝐥𝐞𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐠
The core growth engine remains the insatiable demand for high-speed datacom infrastructure. Driven by scale-out AI clusters, 800G shipments are surging, while 1.6T transceivers are ramping faster than historically anticipated. This demand fueled a 59% YoY explosion in Datacenter & Communications revenue to $1.61B.

🟢🟢 𝟔-𝐢𝐧𝐜𝐡 𝐈𝐧𝐝𝐢𝐮𝐦 𝐏𝐡𝐨𝐬𝐩𝐡𝐢𝐝𝐞 (𝐈𝐧𝐏) 𝐒𝐭𝐫𝐮𝐜𝐭𝐮𝐫𝐚𝐥 𝐌𝐨𝐚𝐭
The rapid internal transition from 3-inch to 6-inch InP wafer production is Coherent's primary margin lever. The 6-inch platform produces over 4x the devices at less than half the cost per wafer, and yields are already exceeding legacy lines. This transition is actively expanding gross margins and provides a critical supply advantage in a capacity-constrained industry.

🟢 𝐍𝐞𝐰 𝐇𝐢𝐠𝐡-𝐕𝐚𝐥𝐮𝐞 𝐎𝐩𝐭𝐢𝐜𝐚𝐥 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐓𝐡𝐞𝐫𝐦𝐚𝐥 𝐓𝐞𝐜𝐡
Coherent is actively commercializing multiple adjacent product vectors. Optical Circuit Switches (OCS), Liquid-crystal technology, Co-Packaged Optics (CPO), and advanced data center cooling materials (Thermodite) significantly expand the TAM. A recent $50M CHIPS Act Letter of Intent and a massive NVIDIA supply agreement strongly validate this pipeline.

🔴 𝐈𝐧𝐝𝐮𝐬𝐭𝐫𝐢𝐚𝐥 𝐒𝐞𝐠𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐌𝐚𝐜𝐫𝐨 𝐖𝐞𝐚𝐤𝐧𝐞𝐬𝐬
The bullish AI narrative contradicts the reality of the Industrial segment, which is steadily reversing. Q4 Industrial revenue fell 16% YoY and 3% sequentially to $430.5M. The macro environment, characterized by sluggish semiconductor capital equipment and automotive markets, is pulling down the company's otherwise meteoric total growth rate.

🔴 𝐂𝐚𝐩𝐄𝐱 𝐁𝐮𝐫𝐝𝐞𝐧 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐂𝐚𝐬𝐡 𝐂𝐨𝐧𝐯𝐞𝐫𝐬𝐢𝐨𝐧 𝐋𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐧𝐜𝐲
Meeting the extraordinary datacenter demand requires immense capital expenditures. Building out 6-inch fabs in Texas, Sweden, and Zurich, alongside module assembly in Asia, represents an enormous cash burn. There is a multi-month latency between wafer production and revenue recognition, heightening the financial stakes if demand normalizes.

⚪ 𝐆𝐞𝐨𝐩𝐨𝐥𝐢𝐭𝐢𝐜𝐚𝐥 𝐚𝐧𝐝 𝐓𝐚𝐫𝐢𝐟𝐟 𝐕𝐮𝐥𝐧𝐞𝐫𝐚𝐛𝐢𝐥𝐢𝐭𝐢𝐞𝐬
While unmentioned in the Q4 press release, prior quarters highlighted the persistent threat of tariffs, global trade protection measures, and intense competition from rapidly expanding Chinese optical networking peers. Despite a strong U. S. manufacturing footprint, a volatile global trade environment remains an exogenous risk to global transceiver supply chains.

𝐎𝐭𝐡𝐞𝐫 𝐊𝐏𝐈𝐬

𝐍𝐨𝐧-𝐆𝐀𝐀𝐏 𝐆𝐫𝐨𝐬𝐬 𝐌𝐚𝐫𝐠𝐢𝐧: 40.2%
Accelerating sequentially and YoY. Up from 38.1% in 25Q4 and 39.6% in 26Q3. This proves the company is successfully optimizing pricing and harvesting the structural cost benefits of its 6-inch InP manufacturing ramp, steadily approaching management's long-term goal of >42%.

𝐍𝐨𝐧-𝐆𝐀𝐀𝐏 𝐎𝐩𝐞𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐄𝐱𝐩𝐞𝐧𝐬𝐞𝐬: $377 million
Up 22.7% YoY, but growing slower than revenue (33.8% YoY), demonstrating operating leverage. SG&A expenses specifically fell to 8.2% of revenue on a Non-GAAP basis (down from 10.3% a year ago), showcasing the benefits of recent portfolio optimization and site consolidation.

𝐎𝐩𝐞𝐫𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐧𝐠 𝐈𝐧𝐜𝐨𝐦𝐞 (𝐆𝐀𝐀𝐏): $254 million
A massive reversal from the nominal $6M reported in Q4 FY25. Operating margin surged to 12.4% on a GAAP basis, proving the company's ability to drop top-line datacenter growth directly to the bottom line.

𝐆𝐮𝐢𝐝𝐚𝐧𝐜𝐞

Accelerating. The $2.3B midpoint implies an astonishing 45% YoY growth rate, a stark acceleration from Q4's 34% YoY growth. This signals that supply constraints are easing while customer deployments for 800G/1.6T networking remain insatiable.

Accelerating. The $1.95 midpoint implies roughly 68% YoY growth over Q1 FY26 ($1.16). While the percentage growth rate looks slightly lower than Q4's 74% YoY print due to tougher base effects, the absolute sequential jump of $0.21 confirms immense profitability momentum.

Stable to Accelerating. The 40.5% midpoint represents further sequential expansion from 40.2% in Q4, maintaining the trajectory toward the >42% target driven by 6-inch wafer adoption.
