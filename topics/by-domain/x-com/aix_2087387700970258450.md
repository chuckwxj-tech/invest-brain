For those new to these name – 

MEC was founded in 1969. Current core business is in developing/manufacturing/selling surface treatment chemical/etching solutions for semiconductor/electronic PKG substrates

Their production bases are in Japan (Amagasaki & Nagaoka, with Kitakyushu plant under construction), China (Suzhou, Zhuhai)/Taiwan, Europe and Thailand

They hold a near-monopoly (almost 100% global share) in copper surface treatment chemical solutions for advanced PKG substrates used in AI chips (GPUs, CPUs, ASICs). These chemicals basically enhance adhesiveness between different material types (copper + organic resin/ABF) by roughening the copper surface

Products are broadly categorized into two: 

1) Adhesion Enhancers (CZ Series, V-Bond Series)

Their first-gen CZ product (CZ-8100, with 1.5µm coarseness) had been in use since launch in 1995 (Intel/Pentium era). Current demand driven by automotive PKG (EV/ADAS)

Their most popular product now is CZ-8101 (with 1µm coarseness) that are used in thin line/space (L/S) PKG substrate used in end-applications such as smartphones/tablets/PCs/servers

CZ-8201 (0.5µm) and CZ-8401 (0.1µm) are now advanced CZ product iterations which are increasingly used/explored to be used in advanced packaging (CoWoS, EMIB/EMIB-T, 3D SoIC) with wiring miniaturisation – and these products are expected to drive FY26 earnings growth, while supported by CZ-8101 sales

Sales split for 1H FY26 (Dec): CZ-8100 (~11%), CZ-8101 (~52%), other CZ products (CZ-8201, CZ-8401 and chemicals used in the pre-/post-treatment processes) (~37%)

For V-Bond Series, they are used in multilayer substrates for automotive/smartphone/satellite comms. Demand is now driven primarily by sales of satellite comms-related substrates (Meiko is one of their key customers, which sells multilayer PCBs & ground-based antenna components to SpaceX)

2) Etching Chemicals (EXE, SF Series)

EXE Series: fine wiring/anisotropic etching used for wire patterning of chip-on-film (COF) substrates used in FPD. Been used in mobile device displays since 2018

SF Series: key material used in touch panel sensors, with two processes (selective etching – copper) and direct resin-to-metal bonding (bonding two materials without adhesive)

New products currently under development is the AP series, which is a flat organic adhesion promoter with no roughening (0µm) = low signal loss. They expect this new series to expand the use of their product range beyond PKG substrates into PCBs/high-freq substrates

For e.g., while fine roughening is good for 800G applications, 1.6T onwards would require/drive demand for non-roughening technology

The AP series can also be used with the CZ series (CZ-8401) to reduce etching volume and signal loss

Their customers include Ibiden, Shinko Electric, Meiko, CMK Corp in Japan, Nanya Tech in Taiwan, Samsung Electronics, SEMCO, LG Electronics (and possibly Hynix) in Korea 

Sales split by geography (2Q FY26); 
Japan 36.2%
Taiwan 18.5%
China 32.8%
Europe 6.1%
Thailand 6.4%

Japan net sales include direct sales (for advanced PKG substrate) and sales to Korea (for memory PKG substrate) through a Japanese distributor

Thailand net sales are mostly from satellite comms-related products. China sales are mainly smartphones/PCs/displays; whereas EU sales are mostly general-purpose PKG substrates
