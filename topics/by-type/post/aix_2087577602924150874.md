Loving that report from $LITE. Blowout numbers. No sign of "peak spending." Lots of signs of accelerating growth everywhere.

Revenue surged 109% year over year to $1.01 billion, adjusted EPS beat estimates at $3.23, and gross margin crossed 50% far earlier than management's original model anticipated. And contrary to slowdown fears, the momentum is accelerating: Lumentum guided fiscal Q1 revenue to a $1.25 billion midpoint, up roughly 24% sequentially and more than 130% year over year, as record 800G shipments give way to a powerful 1.6T ramp, OCS revenue moves above $100 million per quarter, and demand for pump lasers, EMLs and ultra-high-power lasers continues to exceed supply.

Management expects 1.6T strength to persist throughout 2027, described the 2027 OCS demand signal as "incredibly strong," and reported increasing customer interest in next-generation near- and co-packaged optics for 2027-2028 deployments. AI networking demand is broadening from scale-out into scale-across and scale-up connectivity, while rising optical intensity and Lumentum's technology leadership drive extraordinary operating leverage. This report strongly suggests the recent selloff was a sentiment-driven reset and materially strengthens the case for LITE as a premier long-term beneficiary of the AI infrastructure supercycle.

Excellent technical setup, too, with LITE stock bouncing off its 200-day moving average back in July and then retaking its 50- and 100-day in early August, only to briefly lose both, and then bullishly retake both today. Resistance turning back into support. The rally is back.
