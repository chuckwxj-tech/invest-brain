## 原文

A familiar face at Primemas makes this news hit differently.

A colleague I’ve known for several years through various projects moved to Primemas’ U.S. HQ in Santa Clara, and we’ve stayed in touch ever since. So when I saw today’s update about the company, it felt especially personal — and pretty exciting. I am giving my big 👏 to him and his company. 

Primemas is teaming up more closely with Micron $MU on next-generation CXL-based pooled memory for AI data centers. They’re gearing up for mass production of JBOM ("Just a Bunch of Memory”) in the second half of this year. Each JBOM unit packs around 40TB by combining multiple CXL memory expansion cards into a shared pool that servers can draw from dynamically — no need to load every machine with expensive local DRAM.

Even bigger is the Abaco project: a large-scale shared-memory system headed to the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL) under the U.S. Department of Energy.  

- Primemas is supplying the CXL controllers, PMA14/PMA16 cards, and the 40TB-class JBOM units  
- Micron is providing the high-capacity DDR5 DRAM  
- Goal: more than 100TB (potentially up to ~160TB) of CXL shared/pooled memory in a single rack  

Cards ship to Micron in September for testing and benchmarking. Volume production revenue is expected to kick in during Q4, with a cumulative sales target of at least $200 million by the end of 2027.

CXL is quietly turning external memory into something CPUs and GPUs can treat almost like local DRAM. This kind of pooling could carve out a whole new tier between traditional DRAM and storage in AI infrastructure.

Knowing someone on the inside makes watching this progress land a little differently. Looking forward to seeing how it all unfolds.
