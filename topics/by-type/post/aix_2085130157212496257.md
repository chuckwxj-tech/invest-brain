$NLST
Netlist just changed the game.

$Samsung signed an $897M five-year strategic alliance — one of the largest patent licensing deals in memory industry history. $239M upfront, up to $32.9M per quarter on a revenue-based formula through 2031. Net to Netlist after Korean taxes: up to $750M. This creates a recurring, high-margin revenue stream that didn't exist yesterday.

But it's more than a licensing deal. Samsung will supply Netlist with up to $1.5B of DRAM and NAND over five years — "critical sourcing assurance at a time of historically high demand and significantly constrained memory supply amid the build out of AI infrastructure." Management says supply constraints may not ease until 2029. Samsung is also becoming a shareholder with a five-year lockup, and the two companies will collaborate on next-gen memory technology — Samsung brings ASIC design and foundry, Netlist brings CXL, hybrid memory, and low-power MRDIMM architectures.

The part most people will miss: Samsung agreed to an ITC cooperation deal — providing information and declarations Netlist can use in future actions against other memory companies. Their VP of IP Strategy said it on the call: "We believe the unauthorized use of Netlist patented technologies remains widespread across the memory industry. These agreements will allow Netlist to now focus its full attention on other infringing parties."

200+ patents covering DDR5, HBM, LRDIMM, RDIMM, and rank multiplication — fundamental architectures every modern server memory module and every AI GPU's high-bandwidth memory must use. Two of three major memory companies now license this portfolio.

SK Hynix's prior deal — signed in 2021 for $40M — expired in April. No renewal announced. SK Hynix holds 56-62% of the global HBM market, 3x Samsung's share, and is the dominant HBM supplier to NVIDIA. Samsung just paid $897M. That $40M deal was a different era — before HBM became the chokepoint of AI computing.

Meanwhile the product business is accelerating. Q2 revenue $109.8M, up 163% YoY. Gross profit up 1,544%. The company is now profitable. One analyst covers it — Roth MKM, Buy, $5 target.
