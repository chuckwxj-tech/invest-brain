## 原文

Samsung Electro-Mechanics is doubling down on AI data centers and automotive.

On its Q2 conference call the company said it will concentrate resources on these two high-growth markets and expand capacity ahead of demand. 

It expects further sales growth and margin improvement in the third quarter, supported by continued AI data-center investment and rising automotive demand.

MLCC and high-end FCBGA supply remains tight. Long-term contracts and higher average selling prices are already helping results. 

In MLCCs the focus is on ultra-high-capacity, high-temperature, and high-voltage products for AI servers, networks, and ADAS. It already has long-term agreements with about ten top hyperscalers and major chipmakers. 

Demand for the 1005-size 47μF part is expected to rise more than seven times by 2027, so next-generation 68μF and 150μF devices are being prepared along with 1kV-plus and 125–150°C ultra-high-temperature lines. 

Silicon capacitors are also being expanded for additional big-tech design wins.

On the package side, high-end FCBGA production for AI accelerators and server CPUs is increasing. Capacity for large-area, high-layer substrates is being added under existing long-term contracts.

Glass substrate is the newest push. Samsung Electro-Mechanics signed a final agreement with Dongwoo Fine-Chem (a Sumitomo Chemical Korea subsidiary) to produce glass cores. 

It will hold a 66.2% stake in the joint venture, which is scheduled to be established this year. Mass production is targeted for 2028. Some data-center customers are already in technical approval and sample evaluation stages.

The company is positioning itself for the next phase of AI infrastructure and automotive electronics rather than relying only on the current cycle.
