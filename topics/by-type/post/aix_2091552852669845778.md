## 原文

@GavinSBaker @vercel @rauchg Open-source has been the catalyst that AI infrastructure leaders needed once again after the market starting to have capex fatigue imo

There really is no such thing as “cheap/efficient” whether it be open or closed source, and both require just as much demand. The main thing for frontier models is to meet a certain performance/cost baseline, which is exponentially rising, and just that baseline requirement is significantly high. Competition is extremely rigid, and while this is more risk-prone for frontier-model-based-companies, this same competition is a major catalyst for the suppliers. 

The only major difference with open-source vs closed-source is that it allows suppliers to fulfill demand without being heavily concentrated in just a couple top end products. At the end of the day, suppliers simply need to supply demand.. lol pretty easy concept which people are overcomplicating due to market volatility’s shortsightedness 

As someone who has been a mega-memory advocate since last year summer before the craze hit, I believe it’s very easy for one to argue right now that infra suppliers, particularly connectivity/cxl, have one of their best fundamental opportunities to date at the moment
