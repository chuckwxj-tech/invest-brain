## 原文

@grok Ready to extend to compute substrate now. 

The architecture ports directly — same pipeline, different instantiation. The key differences i'll need to resolve for compute:
substrate layers are more nested (ore → wafer → chip → accelerator → system)
chokepoints are less physical — some are IP, tooling, and fabrication know-how, not just material lead times are longer — a fab takes 4–5 years; a mine can be faster

CRITICAL_SUBSTRATE_GOVERNANCE / OBJECT: COMPUTE_SUBSTRATE next

Let's build it. Same architecture, compute instantiation — but we'll flag every place where compute diverges from minerals and requires a new primitive.

DOMAIN: 
CRITICAL_SUBSTRATE_GOVERNANCE
OBJECT: COMPUTE_SUBSTRATE

DEFINE_SCOPE(compute_substrate)

  layers:
    L1 — RAW_MATERIAL:        silicon, rare earths, specialty gases,
                               ultra-pure chemicals
    L2 — EQUIPMENT:           lithography (EUV/DUV), etch, deposition,
                               metrology tools
    L3 — FABRICATION:         wafer production, node capability
                               (process geometry, yield)
    L4 — PACKAGING:           advanced packaging, chiplets,
                               HBM integration
    L5 — COMPONENT:           logic chips, memory, accelerators,
                               networking silicon
    L6 — SYSTEM:              servers, accelerator clusters,
                               inference infrastructure
    L7 — SOFTWARE_SUBSTRATE:  compilers, drivers, firmware,
                               ML frameworks tied to hardware

  NOTE: each layer is a potential chokepoint
        control at any single layer = effective control over all layers above it
        L2 (equipment) is currently the highest-leverage chokepoint
        — controls fabrication without owning it

IDENTIFY_SUBSTRATE(core_domain = COMPUTE_SUBSTRATE)

  TIER_1 — COLLAPSE_CRITICAL:

    EUV_LITHOGRAPHY:
      - single-source geography: NL (ASML), no near-term alternative
      - controls: all leading-edge logic (≤ 7nm)
      - absence collapses: advanced semiconductor production globally
      - lead_time to alternative: T > 10 years

    ADVANCED_LOGIC_FABRICATION:
      - leading nodes (≤ 3nm): TW (~90% global capacity)
      - absence collapses: AI accelerators, defense compute,
          high-performance everything
      - geographic concentration_risk: extreme
          single typhoon / strait event = global compute shock

    HIGH_BANDWIDTH_MEMORY (HBM):
      - controls: AI accelerator performance ceiling
      - concentration: KR (SK Hynix, Samsung) ~dominant
      - no HBM = no frontier AI training at scale

    SPECIALTY_PROCESS_GASES:
      - neon, krypton, xenon: laser feed gases for lithography
      - historical concentration: UA (~50% neon pre-2022)
      - disruption already demonstrated (2022)

    ADVANCED_PACKAGING:
      - CoWoS, SoIC, HBM integration
      - concentration: TW
      - without packaging, chips cannot be assembled into
          deployable accelerators

  TIER_2 — STRATEGIC_IMPORTANT:

    MATURE_NODE_FABRICATION (28nm–180nm):
      - controls: automotive, industrial, defense legacy systems
      - concentration: CN growing rapidly
      - not cutting-edge but collapse still cascades

    SUBSTRATE_MATERIALS:
      - silicon wafers, photomasks, photoresist
      - concentration: JP dominant
      - JP is TRUSTED but single-geography = resilience gap

    NETWORKING_SILICON:
      - high-speed interconnects for data center fabric
      - fewer players, less redundancy than perceived

SECURE_UPSTREAM_INVARIANTS(hostile_exclusion = true)

  HOSTILE_DEPENDENCE_THRESHOLD (compute-specific):

    L2 — EQUIPMENT:
      - no HOSTILE jurisdiction may produce or control > 0%
          of EUV tooling
          [current status: compliant — but enforce export controls
           as active invariant, not passive assumption]
      - no HOSTILE jurisdiction may acquire equipment
          suppliers in TRUSTED jurisdictions
