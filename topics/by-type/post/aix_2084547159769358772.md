TAIWAN’S PANEL MAKERS ARE LEAVING THE LCD VOLUME GAME: AUO AND INNOLUX SHIFT TO HIGHER-VALUE TECH

TIL: By 2028, AUO and Innolux are expected to fall below 10% of global LCD monitor-panel supply and near 20% in notebook panels, while keeping roughly 22% of the TV-panel market.

Innolux plans to shut its Fab 3 notebook-panel line by late 2027 or 2028 as OLED takes more share. The site could be repurposed for FOPLP, CPO and TGV packaging instead of staying trapped in another LCD price war.

AUO is also selling older fabs and moving capital toward Micro LED, AI and CPO. With little new LCD capacity coming online, further closures could tighten TV and monitor supply more than people expect.
