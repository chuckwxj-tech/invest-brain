Some memory related insights I heard today from a Samsung VP

1. The zHBM era may arrive sooner than expected. Thermal issues may be less problematic than many assume, and it will be based on hybrid bonding.

2. Even with the arrival of zHBM, HBM5 development will continue in parallel. The two are expected to coexist depending on AI demand and workload requirements.

3. Samsung is also working on CPO research together with its foundry business, but meaningful adoption is still viewed as a post 2030 story.

4. The fact that “Optics: MicroLED” was explicitly included in the roadmap suggests that Samsung is taking this area quite seriously.

5. Samsung is developing its own CXL solution, CMM D, and expects meaningful synergy with custom HBM.

6. Samsung expects that the profit generated this year alone could be 10 to 20% higher than the cumulative profit it generated over the previous 20 years.

7. On the NAND side, Samsung appears to be focusing more on zNAND for on device AI rather than HBF for data centers.

8. I really wanted to ask about the possibility of reducing the HBM stack from 12 high to 8 high, but I held back 😅
