Glass substrates 🚨 

 the next Moore's Law unlock hiding in chip packaging

🎯 The problem: AI chips (Nvidia H100/H200/B200, Apple M-Ultra) rely on organic ABF substrates to package dies — but ABF is hitting a wall: thermal warpage, limited interconnect density, rough surfaces = defects

🛠️ The fix: glass. Matches the die's thermal expansion (less warpage), flatter surface enables sub-2μm interconnect etching = far denser packaging, better signal integrity, higher yields.

 Trade-off: brittle, poor heat conductor, needs liquid cooling

📈 Who's building it:

- Intel — dedicated glass-substrate line, commercial deployment later this decade

- Samsung — pilot lines + dedicated task force

- Applied Materials x SKC (Absolics JV) — first high-volume glass substrate fab live in Georgia, already sampling to AI/data center clients

- Corning, SCHOTT, AGC

 — glass material suppliers
- LPKF — laser systems for etching/cutting

As AI compute scales, packaging (not just the die) is becoming the bottleneck — and glass may be the next structural shift in semiconductors.
