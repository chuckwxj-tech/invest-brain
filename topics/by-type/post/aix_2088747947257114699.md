## 原文

Thoughts on the SemiAnalysis rumor of an AMD-Google TPU partnership:

1/ Broadcom and Google signed a disclosed 5-year cooperation agreement in March 2026 covering four TPU generations (v8 through v11), extending revenue visibility through 2031. Broadcom is a solid, proven partner. Google isn't ditching them.

2/ Supply chain diversification is real, and it's not new. Hock Tan said it himself on the June call: Google will use multiple suppliers going forward. MediaTek is already there on v9 (Icefish/Zebrafish). AMD potentially joining for v10 fits the same pattern, not a departure from it.

3/ Technically, AMD makes sense. Google reportedly wants on-package CPU cores for RL workloads:  today's off-package host CPU adds latency in tight training loops. AMD already ships this exact architecture in MI300A: Zen CPU cores and GPU chiplets stacked in one package, unified memory, proven SoIC hybrid bonding. It also has its own SerDes/PHY IP (Infinity Fabric), so it's not leaning on anyone else's stack.

4/ When the pie is growing this fast, there's no need to fight over slices. Broadcom's FY27 AI revenue target alone is $100B+. Room for Broadcom, MediaTek, and potentially AMD to all grow in absolute dollars even as any one supplier's % share of Google's wallet declines.
