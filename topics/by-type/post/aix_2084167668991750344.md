🔥 AECs — THE PRODUCT THAT TRANSFORMED CREDO

Credo became a major AI infrastructure beneficiary through:

Active Electrical Cables — AECs.

At extremely high signaling speeds, ordinary copper connections become increasingly difficult.

Electrical signals degrade.

One solution is optics.

But optics can introduce:
**Higher Cost
Higher Power
Greater Complexity**
for shorter connections.
AECs provide another solution.
They combine:

Copper Cable

Credo DSP Technology

to maintain reliable high-speed signal transmission.

For short-reach AI data-center connections, this can provide attractive:

Cost + Power + Reliability + Latency.

⚡ POWER EFFICIENCY IS A MAJOR COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE

AI data centers increasingly face another bottleneck:

POWER.

Every watt consumed by connectivity is one watt unavailable to compute.

At small scale, that difference looks insignificant.

Across:

Thousands → Tens of Thousands → Hundreds of Thousands of Connections

it becomes enormous.

Therefore connectivity is increasingly evaluated through:

Bandwidth per Watt.

Credo has built much of its architecture around this problem.
