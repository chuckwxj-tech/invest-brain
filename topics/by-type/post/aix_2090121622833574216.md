## 原文

$MRVL is rapidly evolving from a traditional networking semiconductor company into one of the most strategically important infrastructure suppliers of the AI era.

The recently disclosed $GOOG partnership represents one of the clearest validations of this strategy.

$MRVL will develop a comprehensive range of custom semiconductor products for Google’s TPU ecosystem, including AI inference accelerators, storage controllers, network interface controllers, memory interface controllers and near-memory compute solutions.

$GOOG also received warrants for up to approximately 59 million $MRVL shares at an exercise price of $206.58. Most of those warrants vest in proportion to Google’s purchases, with full vesting requiring as much as $120 billion in cumulative custom-product revenue through Marvell’s fiscal 2033.

$GOOG joins an already powerful customer ecosystem. $MRVL has a five-year, multi-generational agreement with $AMZN AWS covering custom AI silicon, optical DSPs, active electrical cable DSPs, PCIe retimers, data-center interconnect modules and Ethernet switching.

$MSFT has also been widely reported as a $MRVL custom-silicon customer, while Marvell has officially confirmed that it is working with all four leading U.S. hyperscalers on custom XPUs, CPUs, network interfaces, CXL controllers and related devices.

The company previously disclosed 18 custom-compute sockets across major and emerging hyperscalers, more than 50 pipeline opportunities and over ten customers. These relationships create meaningful cross-selling potential because every successful custom accelerator can generate additional demand for Marvell’s connectivity, switching, memory and packaging technologies.

$NVDA invested $2 billion in $MRVL and is working with the company on NVLink Fusion, custom XPUs, scale-up networking, silicon photonics and AI-RAN.

At the same time, $MRVL supports the open UALink and Ultra Ethernet ecosystems.

$MRVL already has strong positions in 800G and 1.6T optical DSPs, transimpedance amplifiers, laser drivers, active electrical cables and data-center interconnect modules.

The acquisitions of Inphi, Celestial AI, XConn and Polariton add coherent optics, Photonic Fabric, PCIe/CXL switching and next-generation plasmonic modulation.

Together, these technologies position $MRVL across scale-out, scale-up and scale-across connectivity.

The financial momentum already reflects this transformation.

$MRVL generated $8.2 billion of revenue in fiscal 2026, up 42%, with data-center revenue rising 46% to $6.1 billion and reaching 74% of total sales.

In the first quarter of fiscal 2027, revenue increased another 28% to a record $2.42 billion, data-center revenue reached $1.83 billion and operating cash flow hit a record $639 million.

Management expects approximately 40% total revenue growth and roughly 50% data-center growth in fiscal 2027, followed by another approximately 45% increase to around $16.5 billion in fiscal 2028.

Custom-silicon revenue is expected to grow more than 20% in fiscal 2027 and more than double in fiscal 2028, supported by several program ramps already secured.

$MRVL is building an end-to-end AI infrastructure platform capable of monetizing custom compute, XPU-attached silicon, high-speed switching, copper and optical interconnects, CXL memory, AI storage and advanced packaging simultaneously.

Its relationships with Google, AWS, NVIDIA and the broader hyperscaler ecosystem provide powerful external validation, while partnerships with $TSM, $MU, Samsung, SK hynix, ASE, $AMKR. $LITE, $COHR and leading cable manufacturers give it the supply-chain depth required to scale.

If $MRVL converts its expanding design-win pipeline into the production volumes management anticipates, the company could emerge as one of the largest and most diversified beneficiaries of the global AI infrastructure buildout.
