🇺🇸 Stock to Watch | Astera Labs, Inc. ($ALAB)
Astera Labs is a fast-growing AI infrastructure connectivity semiconductor company developing solutions that move data between GPUs, CPUs, memory and networking components inside hyperscale AI systems. Its PCIe, CXL and Ethernet connectivity portfolio addresses one of the biggest bottlenecks in increasingly complex AI data-center architectures.

Key Platforms: Aries • Leo • Taurus • Scorpio
End Markets: AI Servers • Hyperscale Data Centers • Cloud Infrastructure • Accelerated Computing
Key Growth Drivers: GPU cluster expansion, PCIe 6 adoption, rack-scale AI architectures, CXL deployment, 800G Ethernet, hyperscaler capex and increasing bandwidth requirements.
Next Major Catalyst: Q2 2026 Earnings — 4 August 2026
Investment Thesis:

Astera Labs provides the connectivity infrastructure required to keep increasingly powerful AI processors supplied with data. As AI systems scale from individual servers toward massive rack-scale architectures, connectivity bandwidth and memory access become critical performance constraints.

That creates a potentially powerful structural opportunity for Astera's PCIe, CXL and Ethernet portfolio. The company is delivering exceptional growth and high gross margins, but after the stock's substantial rerating, valuation and execution risk are equally important to monitor.
