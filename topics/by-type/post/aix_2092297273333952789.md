# $PENG if you give a conservative 12x earnings multiple for Integrated Memory and LED and apply a 20x earnings multiple for the Advanced Compute business, you get to a 17x blende...

## 用户备注

来源: ai-x-discovery；机器初筛，仍需外部核查。

## 原文

$PENG if you give a conservative 12x earnings multiple for Integrated Memory and LED and apply a 20x earnings multiple for the Advanced Compute business, you get to a 17x blended multiple. Integrated Memory deserves a higher multiple imho, as we haven't even seen higher margin CXL KV Cache Server or Photonic Memory Appliance revenues show up. 

Still, gets you to $85 - $107 per share target. 

Fighting the tide because AI infra names are under pressure, but have always believed that the Penguin Solutions moat and earnings power is more durable due to the service of the Enterprise and Sovereign segments of the market, which will have capex cycles well beyond the markets fears of a 2027/2028 capex peak for Hyperscalers.

Their Integrated Memory segment also is much more insulated to memory pricing normalization due to their gross profit $ being largely consistent if memory prices rise or fall (within reason). I think the Integrated Memory segment deserves a higher multiple than 12x given CXL and the Photonic Memory Appliance market entries.

Market is beating up all AI names the same, but not every AI name has an inflated valuation predicated on Hyperscaler capex continuing at a historic pace.
