# Every earnings call this season is basically saying the same thing: the AI buildout is nowhere close to being over. $MSFT says customer demand still exceeds available capacity. ...

## 用户备注

来源: ai-x-discovery；机器初筛，仍需外部核查。

## 原文

Every earnings call this season is basically saying the same thing: the AI buildout is nowhere close to being over.

$MSFT says customer demand still exceeds available capacity.

$GOOG says it remains supply constrained even after Cloud backlog reached $514B.

$AMZN is planning roughly $220B of 2026 capex and still says it won’t have enough capacity to meet demand. Most of its 2027 compute capacity is already reserved.

$META says the industry has historically underbuilt for the wave of AI adoption and is planning infrastructure around 2027, 2028 and beyond.

$AMD data center revenue grew 107% and Lisa Su says AI is driving a significant expansion in compute demand.

$ANET just had its first $3B quarter as AI networking keeps scaling into 1.6T.
$LITE revenue grew 109% as 1.6T, OCS and CPO demand ramp.

$COHR datacenter and communications business grew roughly 59% and management is expanding capacity for what it calls a multi-year AI opportunity.

$VRT says AI infrastructure is a “structural, long-duration shift” and is seeing huge growth in power and cooling demand.

Compute is constrained. Networking is accelerating. Optics are ramping. Power and cooling are being built out. Hyperscalers are already planning years ahead.
