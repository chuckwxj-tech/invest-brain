🇺🇸 Astera Labs Inc. | Q2 FY2026 Earnings Update

Date: 16 August 2026

Astera Labs delivered an exceptionally strong Q2 FY2026, with revenue surging 104% YoY to $392.4 million, significantly above the company's initial outlook of $355–365 million. Non-GAAP EPS reached $0.80, up sharply year over year, while demand for its PCIe 6, Scorpio AI fabric and other connectivity products continued to accelerate. The company also provided an unusually strong Q3 outlook of approximately $550 million revenue, implying another major sequential increase. The broader opportunity is being driven by the rapid expansion of rack-scale AI infrastructure, GPU clusters and high-speed connectivity requirements.
