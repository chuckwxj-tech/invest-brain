## 原文

Lumentum $LITE — Q4 was a major beat and the AI optical story is getting stronger.

Lumentum reported $1.006B revenue, up 109% YoY and 24.5% QoQ, showing that AI-driven optical demand is still accelerating. Non-GAAP EPS came in at $3.23, about 8% above expectations.

The huge $7.8B net loss looks frightening, but it was primarily caused by a one-time, non-cash accounting charge related to convertible senior notes. It does not represent a comparable deterioration in the underlying operating business, so investors should focus more on adjusted earnings and cash generation.

The biggest positive is the FY27 Q1 guidance: revenue of $1.225B–$1.275B, midpoint $1.25B, adjusted operating margin of 39.5%–40.5%, and adjusted EPS of $4.05–$4.35 versus roughly $3.63 expected. Management said it is reaching its target model more than a quarter ahead of schedule because of accelerating AI demand.

The business is firing on both cylinders. Components revenue reached $650M, up about 23% QoQ, while Systems revenue reached $360M, up about 29% QoQ. Lumentum achieved record 800G cloud transceiver shipments and has started production of 1.6T modules, with management expecting 1.6T adoption to accelerate through FY27 and calendar 2027.

Another huge catalyst is optical circuit switching OCS. Shipments doubled QoQ, and management expects its first $100M+ OCS revenue quarter in FY27 Q1.

Margins are also moving in the right direction. Gross margin reached 47.4%, up from 44.2% the previous quarter and marking another quarter of improvement. R&D and SG&A expenses are growing much slower than revenue, showing strong operating leverage.

What I like most:
• Revenue +109% YoY
• EPS beat expectations
• $1.25B midpoint Q1 guidance
• 1.6T production underway
• 800G shipments at record levels
• OCS approaching $100M+ quarterly revenue
• Gross margin expanding
• Strong operating leverage
• AI demand from hyperscalers/custom AI clusters

And this fits with the other LITE update: NPO and CPO can grow together, non-NVIDIA customers could create additional optical intensity, and CW lasers provide another growth avenue as silicon photonics adoption expands.

My takeaway: LITE is becoming one of the strongest pure-play beneficiaries of the AI optical infrastructure buildout. The numbers are no longer just about future potential — revenue, margins, 800G, 1.6T and OCS are already showing real acceleration.

The biggest things to watch now are 1.6T ramp, OCS exceeding $100M, sustained ~40% operating margins and whether AI optical demand remains this strong through 2027.
