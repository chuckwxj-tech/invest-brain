# The next bottleneck in AI infrastructure is not compute chips but the lines connecting them. GPU count stays at 72, but networking cost per rack jumps from $315,000 to $1,169,00...

## 用户备注

来源: ai-x-discovery；机器初筛，仍需外部核查。

## 原文

The next bottleneck in AI infrastructure is not compute chips but the lines connecting them. GPU count stays at 72, but networking cost per rack jumps from $315,000 to $1,169,000, a 3.7x increase.

This change opens a market that expands from $15.1B to $154.3B. Scale-up makes up 69% of the end market, and CPO-related components alone account for $91B. Optical modules take a smaller share, but their absolute dollar value grows 4x.

There is no single technology path. Silicon photonics penetration rises from 6% in 1Q 2024 to about 45% in 4Q 2028, but incumbent EML remains. The 800G to 1.6T speed transition and CPO adoption overlap.

The biggest disconfirming condition is adoption time. All 7 operators use 800G, but only Nvidia and Google have completed 1.6T, and Google is the only one with OCS adoption. So my conclusion is to prioritize actual shipments, early failure rates, and customer-by-customer adoption schedules over CPO expectations.
