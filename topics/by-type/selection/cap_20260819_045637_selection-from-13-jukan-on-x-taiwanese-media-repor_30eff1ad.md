Taiwanese media reported that the PCB industry has continued to raise prices as supplies of raw materials such as CCL and prepreg (PP) tighten and costs increase.
台湾媒体报道称，随着覆铜板（CCL）和半固化片（PP）等原材料供应趋紧、成本上涨，PCB 行业持续上调价格。

PCB makers have already raised quoted prices by approximately 5–30% this year, with the benefits of those increases gradually being reflected from the third quarter onward.
PCB 厂商今年已将报价提高约 5–30%，这些涨幅的效益正从第三季度起逐步显现。

With upstream material prices remaining elevated, PCB manufacturers are preparing further price increases in the second half through dynamic pricing adjustments to mitigate the erosion of gross margins caused by rising raw material costs.
在上游材料价格持续高企的情况下，PCB 制造商正计划通过动态定价调整在下半年进一步提价，以缓解原材料成本上涨对毛利率的侵蚀。

The main driver of PCB price increases is the broad-based rise in upstream material prices.
PCB 价格上涨的主要驱动因素是上游材料价格的全面上涨。

Major CCL manufacturers raised product prices by approximately 20–30% in the first half of this year, and quarter-by-quarter pricing pressure is expected to persist from the second half through 2027.
主要覆铜板（CCL）厂商今年上半年已将产品价格提高约 20–30%，预计从下半年至 2027 年，逐季的定价压力将持续存在。

Supplies of high-end electronic-grade glass fiber fabric are also tight. The supply-demand shortfall for second-generation Low Dk fabric is estimated to exceed 60%, while the shortage of T-Glass continues to widen. Prices of high-end glass fiber fabric could continue rising through the first half of 2027.
高端电子级玻璃纤维布的供应同样紧张。第二代 Low Dk 布的供需缺口预计超过 60%，而 T-Glass 的短缺状况持续扩大。高端玻璃纤维布价格可能延续涨势至 2027 年上半年。

Meanwhile, the price-increase trend is also continuing in the IC substrate market in the second half. As demand for high-performance computing applications such as AI GPUs and ASICs grows—and supplies of advanced materials, including T-Glass, remain tight—substrate manufacturers are continuing to adjust prices on a rolling basis. Quoted prices are reportedly considered to have room for double-digit increases in each of the third and fourth quarters.
与此同时，IC 载板市场的涨价趋势在下半年也在持续。随着 AI GPU 和 ASIC 等高性能计算应用的需求增长——以及包括 T-Glass 在内的先进材料供应持续紧张——载板制造商正在持续滚动调整价格。据悉，市场报价在第三季度和第四季度均有两位数的上涨空间。
