## 原文

Morgan Stanley: Memory
Morgan Stanley：存储器

> Divergence in Memory: A continuous divergence remains between old and mainstream memory, with pricing power for legacy products expected to sustain robust momentum into 4Q26 and likely 1Q27.  

> The Role of LTAs: The absence of fixed-pricing Long-Term Agreements (LTAs) is not necessarily a negative factor; meanwhile, global mainstream vendors continue to phase out DDR4 supply, causing supply availability to tighten.  

> DDR4 Pricing Surprise: DDR4 pricing continues to surprise on the upside into 2H26. While largely associated with server demand, significant demand is also emerging from consumer-related applications. Analysts project a 50% pricing hike in 3Q and another double-digit hike in 4Q.  

> SLC NAND Strength: SLC NAND remains a highest-conviction pricing call, driven by ongoing capacity constraints, limited supplier willingness to allocate wafer starts to legacy products, and MLC demand migration. Tighter supply conditions are projected to extend into 2027.  
SLC NAND 强势：受持续产能限制、供应商将晶圆投片分配给传统产品的意愿有限，以及 MLC 需求迁移的推动，SLC NAND 仍是市场最具信心的价格判断。预计供应紧张状况将延续至 2027 年。

> NOR Flash Fundamentals: Fundamentals continue to improve, supported by industrial, automotive, networking, edge-AI, and AI server applications. Another round of price hikes is anticipated in 4Q26, potentially extending into 1H27.
NOR Flash 基本面：基本面持续改善，受工业、汽车、网络、边缘 AI 及 AI 服务器应用的支撑。预计 2026 年第四季度将迎来新一轮涨价，并可能延续至 2027 年上半年。
