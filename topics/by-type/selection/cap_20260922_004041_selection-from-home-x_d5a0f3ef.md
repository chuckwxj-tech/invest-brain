## 原文

According to a report from Taiwan's DigiTimes, the arrangement in which CXMT and YMTC each handled DRAM and NAND respectively is changing, and the relationship between the two companies is turning hostile.
根据台湾 DigiTimes 的报道，CXMT 和 YMTC 各自分别处理 DRAM 和 NAND 的格局正在发生变化，两家公司之间的关系正变得敌对。

YMTC is moving into LPDDR5 DRAM and aims to begin trial production by the end of 2026, while CXMT is also looking to expand beyond DRAM into NAND flash. The industry expects the two Chinese memory makers to clash head to head as early as 2028.
YMTC 正在进军 LPDDR5 DRAM，并计划在 2026 年底前开始试产，而 CXMT 也寻求在 DRAM 之外扩展到 NAND 闪存。业界预计，这两家中国存储制造商最早将在 2028 年正面交锋。

With YMTC having already advanced to 270 layers through its Xtacking technology, patent disputes are expected to become the key battleground in this contest for the top position in Chinese memory.
随着 YMTC 通过其 Xtacking 技术已推进至 270 层，专利纠纷预计将成为这场争夺中国存储头把交椅的竞争中的关键战场。

As the memory industry enters a supercycle, YMTC plans to start production at its Phase 3 project by the end of 2026. Initially it is planning DRAM capacity of roughly 25,000 wafers, and it is expected to complete capacity expansion and stabilization in 2027. It is pursuing LPDDR5 as its first mass production target, while also securing space for further expansion.
随着存储行业进入超级周期，YMTC 计划在 2026 年底前让其三期项目开始投产。初期它计划约 25,000 片晶圆的 DRAM 产能，并预计在 2027 年完成产能扩张和稳定。它正将 LPDDR5 作为其首个量产目标，同时也在为进一步扩张预留空间。

CXMT does not appear willing to fall behind. Recent reports indicate it will build a NAND research and development and production line in Beijing.
CXMT 似乎不愿落后。近期报道显示，它将在北京建设一条 NAND 研发与生产线。

There is also talk in the market that CXMT has been recruiting personnel from inside YMTC. lol
市场上也有传言称，CXMT 一直在从 YMTC 内部挖人。哈哈

Originally, under the Chinese government's supply chain localization policy, the two companies developed technology and capacity in their own respective domains, in the spirit of "even brothers climb the mountain on their own." But as Chinese memory makers have grown rapidly, the competitive dynamic has become increasingly clear, and both are now shifting toward building their own cooperative ecosystems. DigiTimes points out that supply chain vendors may in turn find themselves having to pick a side.
原本，在中国政府的供应链本土化政策下，两家公司本着“兄弟登山，各自努力”的精神，在各自领域发展技术与产能。但随着中国存储制造商迅速成长，竞争态势日益明显，双方如今都在转向构建各自的合作生态。DigiTimes 指出，供应链厂商可能反过来发现自己不得不选边站队。

According to industry sources, it remains unclear when CXMT's production line will come online, with mass production expected in 2028 to 2029 at the earliest. The key question, however, is how CXMT will work around YMTC's technology and patents.
据行业消息人士称，CXMT 的生产线何时投产尚不清楚，预计最早也要到 2028 年至 2029 年才能量产。然而，关键问题在于 CXMT 将如何绕开 YMTC 的技术和专利。
