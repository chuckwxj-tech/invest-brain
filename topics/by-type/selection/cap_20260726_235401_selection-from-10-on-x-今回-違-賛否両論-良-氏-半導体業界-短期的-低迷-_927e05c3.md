This time it's different—it's good to have both praise and criticism, right?
这次不一样——既有表扬也有批评，不是很好吗？

Jensen Huang: The semiconductor industry won't slump in the short term. The "godfather of AI" confidently declared, "This time it's different."
黄仁勋：半导体行业短期内不会低迷。"AI 教父"自信宣称："这次不一样。"

Amid swirling debates on the "AI bubble," Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang emphasized "this time it's different" in an interview with overseas media. The main reason is that demand is being driven not by seasonal fluctuations, but by needs across the entire industry. He expressed optimism, stating that the AI infrastructure boom is still in its early stages, the semiconductor sector won't face a downturn anytime soon, and it is projected to grow 5 to 10 times over the next decade.
在关于"AI 泡沫"的争论甚嚣尘上之际，英伟达 CEO 黄仁勋在接受外媒采访时强调"这次不一样"。主要原因在于，需求并非由季节性波动驱动，而是来自整个行业的需求。他乐观地表示，AI 基础设施热潮仍处于早期阶段，半导体行业短期内不会面临低迷，预计未来十年将增长 5 至 10 倍。

Analysts noted that Huang's renewed intervention amid wavering market confidence has helped ease investor anxieties. With the semiconductor industry's sustained growth, Taiwan-based chipmakers such as TSMC (2330), ASE Technology Holding (3711), Nanya Technology (2408), and Winbond (2344) are expected to reap the benefits.
分析师指出，在市场信心摇摆之际，黄仁勋再次出面发声，有助于缓解投资者的焦虑。随着半导体行业的持续增长，台积电（2330）、日月光投控（3711）、南亚科技（2408）和华邦电（2344）等台湾芯片制造商预计将受益。

According to Fortune magazine, when Axios co-founder Mike Allen asked Jensen Huang in an interview if the semiconductor industry's bubble was on the verge of bursting, Huang replied, "No, it won't happen for a while."
据《财富》杂志报道，当 Axios 联合创始人迈克·艾伦在采访中问黄仁勋半导体行业的泡沫是否即将破裂时，黄仁勋回答："不，短期内不会。"

Allen followed up: "So this time it's different?" Huang responded, "Yes, it's different because this boom isn't driven by demand in the usual sense. This time, demand isn't seasonal—it's propelled by the entire industry. In other words, the foundational technology for computing devices is changing." Huang stated that the world needs an entirely new level of AI infrastructure—AI requires it, and AI requires chips. He predicts the semiconductor industry will need to grow 5 to 10 times over the next decade.
艾伦追问：“那么这次有何不同？”黄仁勋回应：“确实不同，因为这一轮增长并非由通常意义上的需求驱动。这次需求并非季节性，而是由整个行业推动的。换句话说，计算设备的基础技术正在发生变革。”黄仁勋指出，世界需要全新的 AI 基础设施——AI 需要它，AI 也需要芯片。他预测，未来十年半导体行业需增长 5 至 10 倍。

Hyperscale cloud providers are making annual capital expenditures in the hundreds of billions of dollars to rapidly deploy AI infrastructure, yet cash flows are already falling short. Even Alphabet, Google's parent company, has seen its free cash flow turn negative for the first time, while major tech firms are expanding corporate bond issuances.
超大规模云服务提供商每年投入数千亿美元的资本支出，以快速部署 AI 基础设施，但现金流已开始捉襟见肘。就连谷歌母公司 Alphabet 的自由现金流也首次转为负值，而各大科技公司正扩大企业债券发行规模。

When asked if he was concerned that customers are taking on debt to buy Nvidia chips, Jensen Huang replied, "I'm not worried." Computing is changing. "The future of computing is an entirely new way of doing things, and we'll need even more computing devices." As AI generates profits and boosts productivity, he said, the industry will need to invest even more in infrastructure. The AI bubble will eventually burst, but since the AI infrastructure boom is still in its early stages, it won't collapse in the short term.
当被问及是否担心客户为购买英伟达芯片而背负债务时，黄仁勋回答：“我并不担心。”计算方式正在改变。“计算的未来将是一种全新的运作模式，我们需要更多计算设备。”他表示，随着人工智能创造利润并提升生产力，行业将需要进一步加大基础设施投资。人工智能泡沫终将破裂，但由于 AI 基础设施热潮仍处于早期阶段，短期内不会崩塌。

He noted that shortages of chips, land, power, and construction labor are hindering the AI industry's growth. "This is actually a good thing," he said, explaining that it delays the point at which supply overtakes demand, and by tempering expectations with environmental realities, it buys enough time to build out the infrastructure.
他指出，芯片、土地、电力和施工劳动力的短缺正在阻碍人工智能行业的发展。“这其实是个好现象，”他解释说，这延缓了供给超过需求的时间点，并且通过用现实环境因素来缓和预期，为基础设施建设争取了足够的时间。

However, according to Caixin, it's only natural for Huang, CEO of a major AI chipmaker, to be optimistic. But the reason he's so confident in the industry's prosperity this time lies in his infamous phrase, "This time it's different." This statement is often seen as a warning sign against overly rosy predictions.
然而，据财新报道，作为一家主要 AI 芯片制造商的 CEO，黄仁勋持乐观态度实属自然。但他此番对行业繁荣如此信心十足的原因，恰恰在于他那个臭名昭著的说法——“这次不一样”。这句话常被视为对过于乐观预测的警示信号。
