🚨DONT IGNORE THE OPTICS BOOM !!

1. Key points from Lumentum ( $LITE) — CEO Michael Hurlston on Earnings :

“Our lead CPO customer’s production plans remain very much on track”  

— “demand signal had strengthened”

 He guided the ultra-high-power laser chip ramp to 2H calendar 2027, ahead of customer scale-up deployments in 2028, and disclosed a first external light source (ELS) module order for 2H27 delivery.

On supply, the surprise was high-power lasers: asked about the demand/supply gap, he said “we are way behind our shipments on high-powered lasers”,  with the imbalance widening rather than easing. 

He also said Lumentum expects to hit its $1.25 billion quarterly revenue target more than a quarter earlier than the schedule laid out at OFC,  on Q4 revenue of $1.01B (+109% y/y)

2. Coherent ( $COHR) CEO — Jim Anderson

 “we have seen absolutely no push-out of CPO demand”  — the opposite, with customers pulling requests forward.”

Closing the call, he framed FY27 as beginning with “exceptional customer demand”  and accelerating growth from new platforms. CPO ships in 2H 2026 and optical circuit switches enter revenue in 1H 2027

The near-term numbers for both are being driven by 1.6T transceivers, lasers and OCS — not CPO itself.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​ $LYTE $LASR
