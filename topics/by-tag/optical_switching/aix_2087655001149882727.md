## 原文

Just listened to the COHR call.  I'm incredulous at the immediate tape tbh.

My verdict, for what it's worth (which is nothing, but here goes): 

Unequivocally bullish, and materially more bullish than the initial earnings release. The after-hours tape massively underreacted.

At approximately 5:31 p.m. ET, COHR was $345.25:
−2.92% versus the $355.64 regular close
+5.08% versus Tuesday’s close

Approximately +1% during the call itself, from around $342

The highest-alpha disclosures imho were:
- FY27 is essentially fully booked.
- Coherent is booked through calendar 2027, with firm product orders reaching into 2028.
- LTAs often extend through 2030, generally carry rising annual volumes, agreed pricing and, in some cases, minimum-demand/take-or-pay protection.
- Current-quarter datacenter growth should exceed 80% year over year.
- Nearly every D&C product line is supply-constrained; management can sell output as fast as it produces it.
- The CPO-delay story was directly disproved: No customer pushouts.
- Customers requesting earlier/more shipments.
- Production wafers already started for December-quarter shipments.
- Scale-out CPO starts in December; scale-up remains on schedule for 2H27.
- NPO provides comparable Coherent content.
- Six-inch InP delivers roughly 4× output at half the wafer cost, with yields already exceeding three-inch production.
- 1.6T is ramping faster than management expected three months ago; 800G remains robust.
- OCS’s >$4B market estimate may be conservative, with revenue already growing and active scale-up engagements.
- The new 1.6T, OCS and CPO mix should be gross-margin accretive, while opex leverage is already ahead of the target model.

My take: What prevents an immediate rerating is not demand. It is the present cash burden: heavy capex, inventory growth, negative free cash flow, dilution and no precise date for exceeding 42% gross margin. Those are reall, but the call materially reduced the risk of the capex, and frankly, how you ramp production to these levels without capex is beyond me - market yet again showing cognitive disonance.

My alpha conclusion: the demand bear case did not survive this call. The remaining bear case is exclusively execution, cash conversion and valuation; and it equally doesn't hold.
