> OCS needs to stay on track for ~$400M of H2 CY26 shipments, with more customer wins behind it.

> Scale - out CPO revenue should start becoming meaningful in the December quarter. Bigger focus for me is NPO in the nearer term + visibility on optical scale-up timelines and whether that opportunity keeps expanding.

> Need confidence around InP substrates and equipment supply

> Curious about the Greensboro / 6-inch experimentation and their plans here. 

> Margins are so strong. If revenue keeps scaling while operating margin moves toward ~40%, earnings power gets very large.

> And I want management’s latest view on China restrictions and whether customers are already shifting toward more Western/U.S. optical supply.

Fundamentally, I expect a very strong print.
