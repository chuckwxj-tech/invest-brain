Forward Guidance Adjustments:

• Q1 2027 Revenue Range: Guided to between $1.225 billion and $1.275 billion. The midpoint of $1.25 billion completely outpaced Wall Street’s pre-existing consensus estimate of $1.16 billion.

• Operating Margins Outlook: Non-GAAP operating margin is projected to accelerate to between 39.5% and 40.5% as operational efficiency improves ahead of schedule.

◦ Production Scaling: Manufacturing pipelines were stabilized by expansions across the company's advanced production facilities in Thailand, positioning Lumentum to reach its long-term financial target models more than a full quarter ahead of its original timeline. 

• Balance Sheet & Liquidity: Lumentum closed out June 2026 holding $2.7 billion in total cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments.
