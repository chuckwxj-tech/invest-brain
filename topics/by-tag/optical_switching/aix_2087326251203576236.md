$LITE Q4’2026 EARNINGS ANALYSIS

1. InP laser demand confirmation

Pump lasers up 80% YoY, still sold out, and they expect shipments to 4x over the next few quarters. Narrow-linewidth lasers up 130%+ YoY too. Even management said the demand surge surprised them.

2. Inability to supply fully & outsource

Still behind on EMLs, no change there. High-power lasers got worse, demand grew faster than they could supply. Had to sign a new substrate deal with AXT to help close the gap.

3. Signs of increase in pricing power

Gross margin crossed 50% for the first time, way earlier than they expected. CEO said there’s still room to run here. CW lasers are also getting a real price premium now.

4. NPO/CPO timelines, possibly 3.2T as well

CPO demand signal increased, with a clearer timeline now, ultra-high-power laser ramp expected 2H 2027 ahead of 2028 deployments. NPO is fully additive, not competing with CPO, even their biggest CPO customer is exploring NPO too. On 3.2T, they expect EMLs to come back in a big way as silicon photonics loses its edge there.

5. Hyperscaler adoption & customer demand

1.6T uptake picking up starting this quarter, running through 2027. They think they’re ahead of competitors here. OCS revenue doubled quarter over quarter, first $100M+ quarter guided next.

6. China competition perspectives

No impact seen so far, and they don’t expect any. Said some Chinese suppliers aren’t even delivering the volumes they claim yet.
