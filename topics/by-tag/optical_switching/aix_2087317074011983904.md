## 原文

Key points from $LITE earnings call today:

Like $AAOI, Lumentum is a supply story compressing into H2 2027 laser ramp (ELS/NPO/CPO) landing on top of 1.6T + OCS systems ramp that's already inflecting.

1. EMLs

- Another big EML quarter led by 100G/lane, with 200G momentum "accelerating rapidly, now accounting for over 25% of total EML revenue." 
- I flagged the 200G mix path as my main watch item in my primer the other day, great to see it already north of a quarter of the mix.
- Still "on track to deliver over 50% EML unit growth" Dec'26 vs Dec'25, even while diverting capacity to CW.
- New: they're selling CW laser chips for 200G/lane to "a multitude of customers" merchant, not just internal.
- Expanding both Japan InP fabs and qualifying CW + EML flows on the newest tools for 200G and 300G lane speeds. 

Vertical integration is obviously nice! Where internal use validates yield, then they sell the same chip merchant. 

$COHR reporting earnings tomorrow with the same 200G EML / 400mW CW story, so the obvious watch point is whether their 6-inch InP gives a cost edge that $LITE has to answer with yield.

2. CPO/NPO

- Lead CPO customer's production plans "remain very much on track, and their demand signal has increased since our last update" 
- They now see the laser ramp in H2 next year ahead of customer scale-up deployments in 2028
- Lumentum also took their FIRST ELS module PO for H2 2027 delivery.
- The rest of the base is prioritizing NPO as an intermediate step - "completely additive for us, significantly increasing the optical TAM," and "even our largest CPO customer is looking at NPO for specific new use cases".

- $AAOI (ELSFP targeted at ~400k/mo by 2028) 
- $SIVE (external InP light sources proliferating across CPO and NPO)
- So ELS architecture got validated by Lumentum aka the biggest merchant laser maker taking real purchase orders.

3. Cloud transceivers / 1.6T 

- Record 800G, initiated 1.6T production shipments as planned, and "our visibility into future cloud transceiver demand is clearer than ever" 
- 1.6T intensifying from fiscal Q1 through 2027, anchored by hyperscaler's custom AI cluster driving a fast 800G -> 1.6T transition.
- "We appear to be first to market in many instances ahead of larger competitors" - a share advantage Lumentum thinks they hold through the cycle.
- Transceiver profitability improving on yield + utilization + higher ASP 1.6T mix.

Overall, $LITE has a 1.6T first mover advantage while $AAOI is becoming the fourth qualified 1.6T supplier at a major hyperscaler.

$AAOI already has >$200M of firm 1.6T orders in hand with qualification finishing in "two, three weeks." Hyperscalers are obviously multi sourcing hard because everyone's capacity constrained.

4. OCS 

- Jumped straight to a guided $100M+ quarter.
- OCS shipments doubled Q3 -> Q4 after they "successfully navigated supply chain constraints to meet steep customer demand" 
- Q1 guidance "includes our first triple digit OCS revenue quarter." 
- "The demand signal for 2027 continues to be incredibly strong."

Lumentum have started adding contract-manufacturer capacity on top of internal factories and are broadening the roadmap to higher/lower port counts including specialized entry offerings.

5. $1.25B guide (+130% YoY)

- CEO: "Adding nearly $250 million incrementally into the guide is pretty impressive" - and he thinks they can "do that more" in future quarters.
- Around half the sequential step is components (scale-out + scale-across).
- Other half is systems (1.6T + accelerating OCS).
- Imo, that balance is what makes Lumentum a durable company. There isn't just one solo SKU carrying the financials.

6. Fresh bits

- Greensboro got no update on the call or in the release.
- The InP fab timeline was a key watchpoint for me, so I'm left with questions given how much capacity language there was around the two Japan fabs. 
- The $AXTI capacity reservation to 2031 tells you where the substrate pull is really going near term though.

- The FCC China transceiver ban is still a net tailwind for $LITE's 1.6T share claim, but would've liked management thoughts on it. Surprised no analyst asked the question.

Just initial thoughts from $LITE earnings call. 

Like $AAOI, I feel like we're coming up to a key inflection point for the laser ramp - ELS, NPO and CPO stacking on top of a 1.6T + OCS systems ramp that's already inflecting.
