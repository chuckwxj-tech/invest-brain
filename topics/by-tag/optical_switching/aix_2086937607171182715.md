JPMorgan on Lumentum ($LITE): management meeting takeaways after F3Q26

JPMorgan says Lumentum’s AI networking opportunity is scaling faster than expected, with OCS, pump lasers, ITLAs, EMLs, and 1.6T transceivers all benefiting from the move toward scale-across architectures. This is not just “more datacenter optics demand” — the real point is that Lumentum may have pricing power, margin mix, and capacity optionality in some of the tightest parts of the AI optical stack.

Key proof points:
• Lumentum has very high share in scale-across components like pump lasers and ITLAs — in some cases 70-80%
• Management is planning output capacity at ~3-4x the current ~$50M/quarter revenue base within 4-6 quarters
• Tight supply is creating a better pricing backdrop, with Lumentum on track for double-digit price increases
• OCS backlog for F1H27 remains ~$400M, despite a slower-than-expected manufacturing ramp after OFC
• Internal OCS capacity is $1.0-1.2B, with contract manufacturing expected to ramp by mid-2026 and come online in early CY27

Why it matters:
The investor debate is whether Lumentum is just riding a cyclical optics squeeze or becoming structurally more important to AI network architecture. JPM’s takeaways lean toward the latter: management sees demand visibility across amplifiers and pluggables, plus a bigger-than-expected TAM as the primary customer’s compute/network design gains traction.

Margin angle:
The best part of the story is mix. Pump lasers and ITLAs carry higher margins, and scarcity is supporting price. Transceivers are still the laggard — margins are only in the 20%+ range versus peers around ~40% — but management sees a path to 30%+ as manufacturing improves.

Capacity / roadmap points:
• OCS roadmap splits into high-radix for training, lower-radix for inference, and smaller-radix for scale-up
• Only one of those OCS use cases is in deployment today
• Caswell is being redirected toward CPO to free San Jose pump-laser capacity
• Takao is being leaned on for EMLs, while Greensboro is planned for 6-inch ramp
• 6-inch migration alone will not meet demand; Lumentum likely needs another fab over time

JPM view:
Overweight. The meeting reinforced JPM’s view that AI scale-across demand, tight supply, mix, and pricing can drive a stronger growth/margin setup — with execution risk still centered on manufacturing throughput and capacity expansion.
