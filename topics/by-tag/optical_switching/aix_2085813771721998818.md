## 原文

So after enjoying some great food last night and taking more time to go over the AAOI ER/call, here are my thoughts. Starting with the ER itself:

Revenue: $191.9M +86% YoY, +27% QoQ
Non-GAAP EPS: $0.06, non-GAAP GM 29.8%, GAAP GM 27.7%, GAAP net loss $22.8M
DC: $107.7M +140.4% YoY, +32.3% QoQ, now 56% of revenue. 100G 38.3%, 200/400G 45%, 800G 11.9%, >10x YoY, guided to grow nearly 5x sequentially in Q3
CATV: record $80.6M, +43.8% YoY with Mediacom naming AOI primary vendor for its DOCSIS 4.0 upgrade. Q3 $100-$110M, over $325M annually.
Cash: $508.8M, capex $565.5M in the quarter, including $280M of prepayments on equipment. ATM has raised $538.8M net to date, and Murry said H2 capex intensity goes higher
Q3 guide: $255-$290M, GM 29-30.5%, EPS $0.11-0.26, +130% YoY at the midpoint

Time for my thoughts. 

We need InP. Lin named it plainly and stated that laser is the biggest bottleneck right now for the transceiver business, with DSP and TIA behind it. 

70mW for 800G, 100mW for 1.6T, 300mW for CPO light source. Power is the easy part, Lin noted the CPO die is about 6 times or even higher in size. Bigger die means fewer per wafer, so wafer math compounds against you at the exact moment power demand triples. Then it gets worse. Gen-1 ELSFP is 1310nm, Gen-2 goes DWDM, where per Lin the wavelength is so tight that the yield loss will be around 40-50% easily compared to 1310. 4x the power 6x the die 1/2 the yield. 

Lin said that in order to meet the demand for the CPO market, the capacity needs to be 8-10x bigger compared to today. That epitaxy comes off MOCVD and Lin confirmed AOI is adding a lot more MOCVD, epi, stepper, all in Houston. The lead time on said equipment is 21-24 months. 21 months minimum. You can't just software patch epi yield and you surely cannot just buy your way to the front of the queue. Anyone who hasn't already ordered the tool is a 2028 supplier. 

Just moved to 4'' substrate volume manufacturing and laser capacity up ~300% by Q3 2027, a second Houston fab roughly 4x the current one. Lin stated most of the Chinese laser entrants are working 70mW and very few can do 100mW, so for 300mW especially DWDM it will take 2-3+ years.

Now, if the US finalizes the plan to ban Chinese transceivers there is one place to go. Wolfe opened the Q&A with exactly this. Murry stated US manufacturing is likely the most important element of appeal to their customers and that announcement heightens that appeal, AOI expects to remain the largest domestic manufacturer of optical transceivers for AI. Lin reaffirmed this stating that AOI might just be the only company committing to really invest heavily in the US and that customers more aggressive will give them more share. 

Now, sold out through Q2 2027, so a ban isn't a 2026 event but rather maybe Q3 next year? 

Which makes it a 2027-2028 windfall sitting outside every sellside model I've seen. Now shifting over to capacity. We know that Q1 exit was nearly 100k units/month against a 150k target for Q2. Q2 exit was approaching 200k. They beat their own plan! 650k/month by EOY and over 930k by end of 2027 (over half of it from Texas). 

Keep in mind that 800G/1.6T run the same line, same process but a different final test. That is why the ramp is a step function and not really an entire rebuild. 

When asked about Q4 Lin literally said more than $500M in revenue. 46% larger than the entire first half and annualizes to a $2.0B exit rate leaving this year. The mid 2027 runrate was reaffirmed and unchanged. 

Now, 1.6T qualification closes within maybe 2-3 weeks, which makes AOI the fourth supplier qualified at the big hyperscaler customer. I know how "fourth supplier" sounds, but it is the opposite of what you think. A hyperscaler only quad sources when no single vendor has enough capacity to sole source them. $200M+ orders in hand and $70-80M of it lands in Q4 with Lin saying next year could be double Q4 or more than double (1.6T revenue in Q1).

So am I still bullish? The answer is YES. I'm so very thankful my beloved AAOI came down into the 70s. $471m months are coming fellas. Hope you're ready.
