## 原文

Interesting observation from Serenity on the $POET / $SIVE connection.

The biggest change isn't that POET is working with Sivers we already knew that. It's the wording.

Previously, the messaging centered around "readiness" by the end of 2026. Now POET explicitly says "production targeted by end of 2026" for its next-generation light source collaboration with Sivers.

That's a meaningful shift. It suggests the focus is moving from development toward manufacturing.

POET is building external laser source (ELS) products for both today's pluggable optics market and tomorrow's co-packaged optics (CPO) market. Those products require high-performance laser technology, and POET says it's co-developing those light sources with Sivers.

If production begins in late 2026 as targeted, the logical next step would be customer shipments ramping through 2027 after qualification. That timeline also lines up with Sivers raising capital to expand InP laser manufacturing capacity.

This doesn't confirm purchase orders or revenue... those still need to come. But it's another breadcrumb pointing toward the same 2027 industry ramp many have been watching.

Between AI infrastructure, CPO, external laser sources, glass substrates, and next-generation networking, more companies are pointing to 2027 as the period when these technologies begin moving from qualification into broader deployment.

Another small but meaningful breadcrumb for the $SIVE thesis.
