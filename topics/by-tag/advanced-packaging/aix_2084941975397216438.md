HBF COULD OPEN A SECOND GROWTH MARKET FOR TC BONDERS: HIGH-BANDWIDTH FLASH MOVES CLOSER TO AI #한미반도체 #HanmiSemiconductor

TIL: *HBF stacks NAND flash to deliver much more capacity than HBM while keeping bandwidth high. Because it is nonvolatile, it can hold large AI datasets and working context at a much lower cost than DRAM alone.

HBF would sit alongside HBM rather than replace it. HBM handles the fastest, latency-sensitive data, while HBF keeps much larger amounts of information close to the GPU.

The equipment market could be just as important. HBF bonding requires less precision than HBM, which should mean cheaper machines but much higher unit demand. Standards work and equipment setup discussions are already underway.

*HBF means High Bandwidth Flash. Think of it as NAND flash packaged more like HBM.
