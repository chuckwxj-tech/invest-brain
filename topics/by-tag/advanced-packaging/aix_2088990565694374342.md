## 原文

As the standalone module disappears, a multi-billion-dollar supply chain is rewired! 

$NVDA's Spectrum-X Ethernet Photonics switches are in full mass production — the first 200G/lane co-packaged optics switch system to ship in volume.

• 400 Tb/s per system, built with TSMC on COUPE packaging
• Per-switch-chip bandwidth doubled to 102.4 Tb/s
• Optical loss cut from ~22 dB to ~4 dB
• Signal integrity up 64x

First customers: $CRWV , Lambda, Oracle. Ramp began May–July.

Now walk the US chain, because this rearranges who gets paid.

The box disappears. For twenty years the optics lived in a pluggable module you bought separately. CPO moves them inside the switch package. Every dollar that used to sit in a third party's module is now contested.

Packaging becomes the bottleneck, not the chip. $TSM does COUPE. NVIDIA said it plainly: the ramp is set by optical engine yield, silicon photonics supply, and advanced packaging capacity — not demand. Whoever holds packaging capacity holds the schedule.

Lasers get more valuable, not less. CPO still needs light, and it needs it from external laser sources with tighter power and reliability specs than a pluggable ever demanded. $COHR and $LITE make those. $AXTI sits under them — indium phosphide is the substrate every high-speed laser is built on, and it just posted record Q2 revenue of $47.6m on data-centre InP demand.

Glass moves up the stack. $GLW has been pushing glass bridge and TGV interconnect precisely for CPO packaging. When optics move into the package, the optical path becomes a packaging material problem.

Test moves upstream too. You cannot discover a bad optical engine after it is co-packaged with a $30k switch ASIC. $AEHR's lead silicon photonics customer already ordered a fully automated FOX-XP system shipping 1H27. Wafer-level burn-in stops being optional.

And the squeeze: every port that goes co-packaged is a port that no longer buys a discrete DSP or retimer at the faceplate. $AVGO and $MRVL own the switch silicon so they capture both sides. $CRDO and $ALAB are levered to the electrical link staying long — CPO shortens it.

Be precise on pace, because most takes will overreach. Three named customers is not the whole market, and the constraint is supply, not orders. The knife is slow. It just started moving.
