## 原文

$NVDA and $AVGO are pushing CPO switches, but the glass FAU supply chain still depends almost entirely on one Chinese supplier.

Crystalwise (4768 TT) says it has begun shipping one-piece glass FAUs to CPO customers, adding another production source.

FAUs align fibers with the optical engine inside a CPO switch and account for 5-10% of its BOM, worth up to $6-7K per switch.

Crystalwise is also shipping glass-core substrates for AI chip packaging. Its combined TGV revenue rose from about $95K in 1Q26 to $430K in 2Q26.
