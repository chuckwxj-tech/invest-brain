## 原文

Lens Technology Announces MoU with $INTC Intel to jointly explore R&D and commercialization in Through Glass Via (TGV) Advanced Packaging. 

The primary collaboration area centers on critical processes including through-hole formation on glass substrates, high-precision laser processing, metal-filled via deposition, and multi-layer interconnect routing. Intel views Lens as a valued potential collaborator and may provide architecture information, Design for Manufacturing (DFM) guidance, benchmark testing, and validation approaches, which are all subject to separate written agreements. 

Any engineering validation, qualification, or volume production would also require further separate agreements.

This MoU strengthens Intel’s advanced packaging ecosystem at a key time for AI and high-performance computing.

Why This Matters for Intel’s Roadmap
TGV technology enables high-density vertical interconnects on glass substrates. 

Glass offers superior flatness, lower warpage, better electrical performance, and improved thermal characteristics compared to traditional organic substrates, which bode as advantages that become critical as chiplets and AI accelerators push power delivery and signal integrity limits.

Intel has been actively developing glass substrate technology as part of its broader advanced packaging strategy (alongside EMIB, Foveros, and Co-EMIB). Partnering with a specialist like Lens Technology (a major precision glass and component supplier) helps Intel accelerate capability in an area where ecosystem depth matters as much as internal process know-how.

Intel’s willingness to share architecture details and DFM guidance is explicitly caveated to separate agreements. This is standard practice to protect proprietary designs while still allowing meaningful collaboration.
