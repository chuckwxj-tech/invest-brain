Quick TLDRs from recent supply chain notes:

- $SNDK Investor Day: 80%+ adjusted gross margins expected through 2030, operating margins ~75%, FCF margins ~50%. LTAs already lock in 2/3 of 2028 wafers. Minimum contracted revenue hits $93B. This is no longer a pure cyclical story.

- Conventional DRAM margins heading toward historic highs. UBS sees $MU gross margins approaching 95% by 2027 — higher than HBM in some scenarios. Nanya, Winbond and other legacy players should see strong leverage if this holds.

- Anthropic still printing absurd growth: 14x+ YoY, 2.4x sequential, Q2 revenue already >$11.5B. Path to $190–200B by 2028 looks intact. Frontier model demand remains unconstrained.

- $NVDA expanding its ecosystem: talks around $3B into SB Energy + large-scale compute financing with major PE firms. Feynman architecture moving to TSMC A16 + SoIC + custom HBM + CPO in H2 2028.

- $MSFT Maia 300 ASIC targeting >300k units in 2027 with path to 1M+. Clear positive for the broader ASIC supply chain ($MRVL, Alchip, etc.).

- $TSM Advanced Packaging: multi-year tightness expected in both memory and ABF substrates. Upstream equipment (vacuum lamination etc.) already at full utilization with 20%+ price increases.

- $AMAT lifting advanced packaging growth guidance to >70% for 2026 (from prior >50%). Customer visibility now stretches into 2030.

- Power infrastructure shifting fast: 48V hitting limits. $NVDA pushing 800VDC MGX racks for H2 2026 — good setup for Delta, Lite-On and related names.

- Industry-wide LTA adoption continues (Nanya, CXMT and others). Probe cards and MLCCs remain constrained (lead times stretching to 36 weeks).

TLDR: The AI buildout is locking multi-year contracted demand across the entire stack — memory, advanced packaging, substrates, power, and components. Bottlenecks look structural.
