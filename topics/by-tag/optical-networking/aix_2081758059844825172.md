Why Recommend LITE (Lumentum Holdings)?

LITE is a leading provider of optical and photonic products—lasers, optical modules, and interconnect solutions—and has become a key supplier for AI data center infrastructure.

Key reasons to consider it:

Critical AI optics play: AI clusters demand higher-bandwidth, lower-latency optical interconnects (shifting from copper to fiber).

$LITE  leads in high-speed EML lasers (especially 200G per lane) used in 800G and 1.6T modules, directly benefiting from surging east-west traffic inside data centers.

Strong growth and technology edge: Recent revenue has grown sharply year-over-year (nearly doubling in some quarters), with improving profitability. 

The company is advancing in co-packaged optics (CPO) and optical circuit switches (OCS), backed by solid order backlogs and recognition from major customers including Nvidia-related deals.
Analyst support: Most Wall Street analysts rate it a Buy or Overweight, citing long-term TAM expansion from the AI infrastructure buildout.

Risks

Valuation already prices in high expectations. Customer concentration, AI capex cycles, competition, and technology shifts can cause sharp pullbacks. Suitable for investors bullish on the optical upgrade cycle in AI data centers who can tolerate volatility.

Bottom line: 

LITE is a pure-play on the “optics” layer of AI infrastructure. If you believe in the long-term shift to high-speed optical connectivity in data centers, it is worth watching.
