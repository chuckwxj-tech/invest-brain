## 原文

𝗠𝗲𝗺𝗼𝗿𝘆 𝗚𝗶𝗮𝗻𝘁𝘀 𝗢𝘂𝘁𝘀𝗼𝘂𝗿𝗰𝗲 𝗖𝗫𝗟 𝗖𝗼𝗻𝘁𝗿𝗼𝗹𝗹𝗲𝗿𝘀 𝗮𝘀 𝗙𝗮𝗯𝗹𝗲𝘀𝘀 𝗦𝗽𝗲𝗰𝗶𝗮𝗹𝗶𝘀𝘁𝘀 𝗚𝗮𝗶𝗻 𝗚𝗿𝗼𝘂𝗻𝗱

> Samsung 005930.KS, SK Hynix 000660.KS, and Micron $MU have each shelved plans to commercialize in-house Compute Express Link (CXL) controller chips, marking a significant shift in how the memory industry approaches this emerging interconnect standard.

> The retreat follows years of parallel internal development at all 3 companies. Each concluded that controller design complexity and time-to-market risk outweigh the benefits of vertical integration at this layer of the AI memory stack.

𝗗𝗶𝗳𝗳𝗲𝗿𝗲𝗻𝘁 𝗘𝘅𝗶𝘁 𝗦𝘁𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲𝗴𝗶𝗲𝘀

> Micron halted in-house CXL controller R&D entirely, adopting an externally sourced CXL 3.0 solution from specialist fabless firm Primemas. SK Hynix terminated its project and redirected those engineering resources toward processing-in-memory (PIM) architecture development.

> Samsung is targeting CXL 3.2 mass production in 2026 but now sources controller silicon externally. All 3 companies continue developing CXL-compatible DRAM and HBM products — only the controller design layer is being handed off.

𝗙𝗮𝗯𝗹𝗲𝘀𝘀 𝗕𝗲𝗻𝗲𝗳𝗶𝗰𝗶𝗮𝗿𝗶𝗲𝘀

> Marvell $MRVL and Astera Labs $ALAB are widely cited beneficiaries of the incumbents’ retreat. Both carry existing hyperscaler customer relationships and silicon optimized for high-bandwidth memory expansion at the PCIe and CXL interface layers.

> CXL memory expansion addresses a real AI infrastructure bottleneck: GPU and accelerator performance is increasingly limited by memory bandwidth. Outsourcing controller design to dedicated fabless specialists may accelerate iteration in one of the AI data center’s more critical layers.
