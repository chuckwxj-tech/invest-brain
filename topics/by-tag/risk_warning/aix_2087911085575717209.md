AI supply chain bottlenecks: 

According to $TSM VP of Advanced Packaging.  

1. Memory 
2. ABF substrates  

ABF substrate supply will be tight over the next few years (TSMC at the 2026 OCP APAC Summit).  

Due to shortages, TSMC are forced to source from various different suppliers despite the resulting risks with differences in mechanical/thermal properties.  

I feel like the ABF shortage is one of your more structural bottlenecks, similar to lasers and InP related stuff like $AAOI / $LITE - now confirmed by TSMC.

Whether equity returns can be high enough though is a different story.

Glass core substrates are still a thing though, especially for CPO. 

TSMC have been validating glass substrates separately with Ibiden and Innolux for CoPoS.  

So that's where names like $GLW, $INTC, Samsung Elec, and $LPK can become beneficiaries.
