## 原文

AI is not just models.
It’s a full stack.

That’s why my #MEXC0808 AI Cart is built across five different layers of the AI economy:

➜25% $META
Meta is no longer just an open source player. In 2026 they launched the proprietary Muse Spark series (now at 1.2) that powers Meta AI, while still releasing open weight models like Muse Glimmer 30B for on device use.
 They have both frontier models and the biggest consumer distribution channel in the world.

➜23% $GOOGL
Google is shipping Gemini at massive scale. The Gemini app crossed 1 billion monthly users, AI Overviews reach over 2.5 billion people, and Google Cloud’s backlog hit $514 billion. They control research, models, cloud and distribution all in one company.

➜20% $AMZN
Amazon is quietly becoming one of the biggest AI chip companies. Their Trainium and Inferentia chips already generate over $25 billion in annual run rate revenue. Anthropic and others are training on hundreds of thousands of Trainium chips. AWS is no longer just cloud it’s custom silicon + infrastructure.

➜17% $MRVL
Marvell sits in the most overlooked part of the AI stack: connectivity and memory infrastructure. Photonic Fabric, CXL memory expansion, PCIe retimers and custom ASICs for hyperscalers. Even Nvidia invested $2 billion in them. Without fast interconnects, the biggest GPUs are just expensive paperweights.

➜15% $MU
Micron’s high bandwidth memory (HBM) is sold out through 2028. AI data centers are expected to consume 70% of all memory chips this year. HBM4 is ramping hard  and Micron just committed $10 billion to next generation memory research. Memory is no longer a commodity it’s the bottleneck.
This is not a random basket.

It’s a deliberate stack:
Models + Distribution → Research + Cloud → Custom Silicon + Scale → Networking + Interconnect → High Bandwidth Memory
Five companies. 

Five different layers. One thesis.
This is my personal #MEXC0808 AI Cart.

Not financial advice. Just how I see the AI economy in 2026.
