## 原文

Samsung’s memory side just offered some clear signals.

zHBM could arrive sooner than many expect. Hybrid bonding is the critical enabler, and the thermal challenges look more manageable than earlier fears suggested. 

HBM5 development continues in parallel. Both technologies will coexist according to actual demand.

The company is also advancing its own CXL solution, CMM D, designed for better synergy with custom HBM. 

MicroLED optics sits explicitly on the roadmap, showing they are treating it seriously. Co-packaged optics remains more of a post-2030 topic.

On the financial side the numbers are striking. This year’s profit alone could exceed the combined total of the previous 20 years by 10-20%. 

On NAND the focus is shifting toward zNAND aimed at on-device AI.

The AI memory cycle remains firmly intact.
