Marvell's AI memory unveil connects to the photonics chokepoint thesis.

$MRVL's portfolio spans CXL pooling, optical shared memory (Photonic Fabric — 32 TB KV-cache offload at 2-3x token throughput), and Bravera SC6 PCIe 6.0 SSD controller.

The optical layer is the key. MRVL builds the controller but needs InP lasers at volume. LITE's CEO just warned laser supply is a more severe bottleneck than memory. AAOI, SIVE, COHR own the photonics supply chain beneath it.

The stock is up 16.6%. The structural read-through is that disaggregated memory needs connectivity, and the connectivity bottleneck runs through laser fabs.

MRVL is the controller chokepoint. The photonics names are the capacity chokepoint beneath it.
