$CAMT will 2x at 10x 2027 sales 

Here’s another positive read through from $KLAC 

Advanced packaging is inflecting hard, and accelerating. KLA raised its adv-packaging revenue growth from high-50s% to >70% Y/Y and said the market itself went from ~20% expected at the start of the year to mid-to-high-30s% now. That’s Camtek’s primary end market re-rating upward mid-year.

The specific drivers named are Camtek’s entire franchise: HBM, CoWoS, SoIC (est. 4x wafer-start growth in CY27), hybrid bonding. KLA also flagged HBM DRAM carries higher process-control intensity than conventional DRAM. Camtek is disproportionately levered to precisely this mix, not to etch/litho.

Finer pitch = content growth, not just volume. KLA’s explicit point that new packaging nodes need higher inspection fidelity means more inspection tooling per unit of output. For an inspection-only name like CAMT, that’s intensity/content growth on top of unit growth.

The up-cycle’s mix favors CAMT. WFE raised to ~$150B CY26 / ~$190B CY27, and process control as a share of WFE keeps rising (12%→15%). The strength is concentrated in leading-edge + HBM/DRAM + packaging — CAMT’s exposure, not the laggard buckets.

2027 visibility is strong (KLA backlog ~$12.5B, engagements into 2H27). That’s the piece your 2-year hold needs
