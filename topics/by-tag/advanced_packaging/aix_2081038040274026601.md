## 原文

Advanced packaging is emerging as AI’s quiet but critical growth engine.

It still represents a small slice of total semiconductor packages, but the high-end segment (2.5D/3D, CoWoS, EMIB, fan-out) powering AI GPUs and accelerators is exploding. 

Nomura/Yole data projects a 31 percent CAGR for high-end advanced packaging units from 2024–2029.

Small base + rapid growth creates significant opportunity. For AI compute, advanced packaging has become a key constraint. 

Even with wafer capacity, system performance depends on high-yield chiplet assembly and tight interconnects.

Intel’s multi-year investments in EMIB, Foveros, glass substrates, and foundry packaging services position it well here.

The units are small today. The growth trajectory is not.
