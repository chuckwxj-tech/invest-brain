# Deutsche Bank met with $MU management at FMS 2026 Flash Memory Summit. According to management, AI is permanently re‑shaping storage into a core component of system architecture...

## 原文

Deutsche Bank met with $MU management at FMS 2026 Flash Memory Summit. According to management, AI is permanently re‑shaping storage into a core component of system architecture, with both DRAM and NAND in supply‑constrained conditions. Memory now accounts for nearly 50% of total system value, and AI is accelerating this multi‑year re‑rating process.

Deutsche Bank notes Micron holds a “luxury advantage”: it can grow without sacrificing profitability, setting the stage for meaningful earnings upgrades and valuation re‑rating.
Strategic Customer Agreements (SCAs) are expected to cover roughly 40% of Micron’s sales volume. Memory’s share of system value has climbed from ~10% three decades ago to almost 50% today, a trend supercharged by AI.
An open industry question remains: will per‑GPU memory demand keep rising as context windows expand, or can software improvements such as KV‑cache orchestration absorb part of incremental memory requirements?

Hyperscalers have historically tilted capital spending heavily toward GPU infrastructure. As workloads shift toward agent‑based AI with far heavier compute invocation, under‑investments on the CPU side are coming into focus.Emerging demand signals worth watching for investors.
With elevated DRAM pricing, bandwidth‑hungry inference workloads with modest memory footprint are shifting toward SRAM. Micron estimates around 5% of systems are running this type of workload today. Even so, SRAM only works as an on‑chip solution with poor scalability and cannot replace HBM.
CXL and HBF were widely discussed at the summit, yet Micron remains skeptical over their real‑world utility and commercial outlook. Per Deutsche Bank, SRAM represents an additional tier in the memory hierarchy and poses no near‑term disruptive threat to HBM or Micron’s core storage business.
Powered by its robust product portfolio, evolving business model and favorable supply‑demand backdrop, Micron is positioned for substantial earnings revisions and valuation reset. Analyst Melissa Weathers emphasized Micron’s “luxury advantage” of profitable growth, with end‑to‑end technical leadership and execution discipline as key differentiators versus peers.
