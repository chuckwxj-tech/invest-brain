【Why I Don’t Invest in Memory Stocks】

I’m not saying memory stocks have no more upside.

But I believe the bottlenecks in AI infrastructure are gradually moving outward from the XPU.

XPU
↓
HBM
↓
PCIe / CXL
↓
Scale-up
↓
Multi-rack
↓
Scale-out
↓
Optics

The HBM shortage will continue.

But that’s increasingly becoming consensus.

Meanwhile, as AI clusters grow larger, connectivity, networking, and optics become increasingly critical — and their addressable markets could expand far beyond current expectations.

That’s why I’d rather invest in companies positioned to solve the next bottlenecks to emerge, rather than the ones everyone can already see.

I believe the biggest asymmetry between future market size and current market capitalization may actually lie further down the stack.
