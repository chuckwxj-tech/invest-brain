• Systems revenue jumped 123% YoY, driven by record cloud transceiver shipments

• Ultra-high-power lasers for co-packaged optics (CPO) are ramping, with meaningful revenue expected exiting 2026
• The OCS ramp remains backed by a multi-year, multi-billion-dollar purchase agreement

And for Q1 FY27, Lumentum is guiding to a 39.5%–40.5% operating margin.

AI data centers are pulling optics deeper into the network — from transceivers and DCI all the way toward CPO and optical switching.
