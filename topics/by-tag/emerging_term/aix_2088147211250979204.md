📉 Glass substrate timelines appear to have slipped again — but the commercialization path is getting clearer.

The latest updates suggest both major Korean players have pushed out their mass-production schedules:

SKC / Absolics
H2 2026 → H1 2027
Samsung Electro-Mechanics
H2 2027 → H1 2028

For Samsung, the issue appears to be customer reliability validation of glass substrate prototypes.
SKC has also reportedly shifted mass production into 2027, while still targeting final customer validation by year-end.

So this is not purely good news.

Timeline delays matter.

But my read is that the glass core substrate story is moving from a “technology concept” toward a much more concrete qualification → production → volume ramp phase.

That distinction matters for the equipment and process suppliers around it.

If these revised schedules hold, names such as:

$LPK
Philoptics (161580)
E&R (8027)

could see their meaningful volume ramps track the commercialization timelines of SKC and Samsung much more closely.

The key thing I’m watching now is no longer whether glass substrates will happen.
It is when customer validation converts into real production orders.
That will determine when the upstream equipment cycle actually starts.
