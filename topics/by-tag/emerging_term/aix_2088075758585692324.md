## 原文

Nvidia’s next-gen Feynman platform is ahead of schedule, targeting mass production in 2H 2028.

Will use TSMC A16 process, 3D chiplets, SoIC packaging, and CPO (co-packaged optics).

Rack bandwidth targeted at 1,000 TB/s (vs. 260 TB/s for Rubin and 520 TB/s for Rubin Ultra), making CPO essential.

TSMC is accelerating advanced packaging capacity:

AP7 (Chiayi) and AP8 (Tainan) construction ahead of schedule.

SoIC, CoWoS-L, and CoPoS all being pulled forward.

SoIC capacity expected to reach 50,000 wpm by end-2027 (vs. 20,000 wpm by end-2026).
