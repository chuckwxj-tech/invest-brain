## 原文

🇯🇵 Rapidus is advancing 600 × 600 mm panel-level packaging – a development that could become highly relevant for SCHMID Group.

Rapidus is developing large-format RDL interposers for next-generation 2.xD and 3D chiplet packaging. The target applications include large AI and HPC packages combining processors, chiplets and HBM.

Key facts:

➡️ Equipment installation at the Rapidus Chiplet Solutions facility in Chitose began in April 2025, with full operations starting in April 2026.

➡️ Rapidus has already produced a prototype 600 mm RDL panel.

➡️ The process targets 2 µm/2 µm line/space structures and an interconnect pitch of around 25 µm.

➡️ Commercial packaging services are planned from around 2028, while an 8-reticle-size interposer is targeted around 2030.

➡️A 600 × 600 mm panel could theoretically accommodate 49 of these large interposers, compared with four on a 300 mm wafer, nine on a 300 × 300 mm panel and 36 on a 510 × 515 mm panel.

This illustrates the potential advantage of PLP: AI packages are becoming increasingly large and expensive to manufacture on circular wafers. Larger rectangular panels offer substantially better area utilization and could reduce costs per package.

However, 49 interposers is a theoretical figure. The commercial case ultimately depends on controlling warpage, defect density, overlay accuracy and yield across the entire 600 mm panel.

An important technical distinction: Rapidus does not necessarily appear to be using a permanent TGV glass-core interposer. The current approach seems to involve organic dielectric and RDL structures formed on a large glass carrier. It is therefore different from concepts in which the glass remains inside the final package.

What could this mean for SCHMID Group?

Several SCHMID systems appear well aligned with the required panel dimensions and process specifications:

◾️ InfinityLine C+: wet processing of panels up to 610 × 610 mm and structures down to 2 µm
◾️ InfinityLine P+: copper plating on panels up to 623 × 623 mm
◾️ InfinityLine L+: planarization/CMP for panels of around 622 × 622 mm
◾️ InfinityLine H+ and PlasmaLine: cleaning, surface treatment and dielectric structuring

The dimensions and targeted resolution are therefore a strong technical fit. However, this does not automatically mean that SCHMID’s equipment has been qualified for the specific Rapidus process.

There is nevertheless an interesting circumstantial link: In December 2025, SCHMID announced the installation of an InfinityLine C+ at an unnamed leading Japanese advanced-packaging customer working on next-generation PLP using glass and other substrates. Later that month, Rapidus disclosed that its 600 mm PLP equipment had been installed and that a first panel had already been produced.

This is not proof that Rapidus is SCHMID’s customer. But the geography, timing, panel dimensions and targeted 2 µm resolution make Rapidus a credible candidate.

One limitation is that Lam Research has reportedly supplied Rapidus with its Kallisto panel-plating system. SCHMID may therefore not address every step of the production flow. Its C+, H+, L+ and PlasmaLine systems could nevertheless cover complementary processes.

The bigger opportunity for $SHMD would not be the initial pilot equipment alone. If Rapidus successfully commercializes the technology from 2028 and ramps high-volume production towards 2030, multiple process clusters and follow-up systems could be required.

Overall, Rapidus provides another strong indication that 600 mm PLP is moving from R&D towards industrialization. SCHMID already offers equipment with the necessary panel dimensions and resolution—but customer qualification, manufacturing yields and actual capacity expansion will determine whether this becomes a meaningful revenue opportunity.
