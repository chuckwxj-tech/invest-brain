## 原文

Odisha is building six semiconductor projects with a combined investment of ₹14,795 crore. RIR Power Electronics has already commenced operations, and more projects are under construction across the state.

This is a proud moment for every Odia.

Our state is putting real work behind silicon carbide, gallium nitride, advanced chip packaging, testing, and specialty semiconductors. These are the technologies used in electric vehicles, renewable energy, telecom, data centers, defense equipment, railways, and modern electronics.

At Info Valley in Bhubaneswar, SiCSem is setting up a silicon carbide compound semiconductor facility with capacity for 60,000 wafers and 96 million packaged units a year. Heterogeneous Integration Packaging Solutions is building advanced 3D chip packaging and embedded glass substrate capability that will support high-performance computing, AI, automotive, defence, and communications.

These projects mean skilled jobs, stronger local supply chains, new opportunities for our engineers and students, and a bigger role for Odisha in India's technology journey.

Odisha has always been known for its resilience, talent, natural resources, culture, and hard work. Now the world will also know Odisha for building the technologies that power the future.

Jai Jagannath. Proud Odia.
