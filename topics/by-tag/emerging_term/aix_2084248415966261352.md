I missed this last week, but glass substrates look a lot less “2028 story” than I thought.

SKC 011790.KS says Absolics has already produced qualified samples in Georgia and sent them to Taiwan for full package reliability testing. That is basically the final technical gate before customer PoC.

LPKF Laser & Electronics https://t.co/vmEb4ndYFn also disclosed another LIDE system order from a specialty glass manufacturer trying to enter advanced packaging.
Still plenty that can go wrong here. Copper filled vias cracking under repeated heat cycles would delay the whole transition.

But this has clearly moved beyond conference slides now.
