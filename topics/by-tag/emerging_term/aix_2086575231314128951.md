## 原文

Semi materials are starting to get more expensive almost everywhere.

GlobalWafers is raising spot wafer prices and negotiating higher 2027 contract prices. Shin-Etsu and SUMCO are raising prices too.

AI servers reportedly use ~3.8x more 12-inch wafers than normal servers, while HBM needs ~3x more wafers per equivalent capacity vs DRAM.

Tungsten/WF6 is getting tight too. Kanto Denka and Central Glass reportedly warned TSMC, Samsung and SK hynix about supply risk. High-purity tungsten is ~70% of WF6 cost, and China controls a big part of the supply chain.

HBM4 reportedly also needs >3x more sputtering-target material than traditional DRAM.

Then you have Formosa Plastics raising semiconductor-grade HF + IPA prices in July, Sumitomo Bakelite/Mitsubishi Chemical raising advanced packaging material prices, and tighter high-purity helium supply.
