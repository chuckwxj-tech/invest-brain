SAMSUNG AND INTEL ARE STARTING TO MEET IN THE MIDDLE — AI CHIP COMPETITION IS MOVING FROM SINGLE CHIPS TO FULL SYSTEMS

TIL: Samsung is pushing a memory + foundry + advanced packaging turnkey model, using HBM as a wedge to win more AI and HPC logic business. Intel is moving the other way, using EMIB, chiplets and CXL to pull memory deeper into its compute platform.

Samsung’s advantage is that it already owns HBM, leading-edge logic manufacturing and packaging under one roof. Its foundry business is also seeing more HBM base-die demand, while 2nm mobile and 4nm AI/HPC products ramp in the second half.

Intel isn’t getting back into conventional DRAM or HBM production. Instead, it hired former SK Hynix CEO Lee Seok-hee to lead advanced packaging, backend manufacturing and system integration — basically betting that the next AI battle is about how compute, memory and packaging fit together.
