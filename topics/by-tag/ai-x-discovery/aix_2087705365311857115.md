$COHR — Strong Earnings, But the Bar Was Extremely High

Coherent delivered another strong quarter, with revenue of $2.05B (+34% YoY) and EPS of $1.74, both above expectations. Even more impressive, Datacenter & Communications revenue surged 59% YoY to $1.615B.

FY27 demand looks exceptionally strong, with management saying the year is essentially booked and purchase orders extending into 2028. CPO/NPO, OCS, multi-rail and thermal solutions could add additional growth over the coming quarters.

So why did $COHR sell off after hours?

The issue isn’t demand — it’s expectations, margins and capital intensity.

Q1 revenue guidance of $2.3B at the midpoint is a major beat, but gross-margin expansion is more gradual. At the same time, Q4 capex jumped to $556M as Coherent invests heavily to expand capacity.

After an 8% rally today and a gain of more than 200% over the past year, investors were clearly looking for near-perfect results.

To me, this looks more like profit-taking and an expectations reset than a deterioration in the AI thesis.

The AI datacenter demand story remains very strong. Now the market wants to see how efficiently Coherent can convert that demand into margins and cash flow.
