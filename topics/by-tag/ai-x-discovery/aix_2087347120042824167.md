## 原文

CoWoS is taken for granted today, but took 15 years of hard work from packaging engineers to develop with little respect.   

For much of its early history, it was often referred to as NWoS (No wafer on substrate) because it was too expensive for anyone to use.   

Now all of a sudden, with AI, and a lot of technological development, CoWoS has emerged as a critical packaging bottleneck to tightly integrate HBM with AI Accelerator Chips. Thus, the AI bottleneck literally flows through the hands of the packaging engineers.

In my post, I describe the fabrication steps of each of the components of CoWoS.   

I also give a much needed reality check from John Lau's ECTC seminar as to why hybrid bonding is not widely used today and the main reason why glass substrates are challenging to design with (and its not SeWaRe).
