## 原文

Just came out of the $LITE earnings call.

The message was clear: demand is still running ahead of supply.

The stock is now up about 2-3%.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

Capacity is still the bottleneck.

The EML supply-demand gap has not improved, while high-power laser demand has accelerated enough that Lumentum is now further behind customer demand despite its ramp staying on plan.

Pricing power remains strong.

Management said some business has been repriced, with more benefit still to come. Even CW lasers are commanding a premium because tighter specifications improve customer yields.

NPO is adding to the opportunity.

Its lead CPO customer remains on track, while near-packaged optics is emerging as an additional market rather than a substitute.

The next ramps are becoming meaningful.

1.6T transceivers are now in production, OCS is heading toward its first $100M+ quarter, and 200G EMLs already exceed 25% of EML revenue.

KEY QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Analysts repeatedly tested whether current financial targets are already too conservative.

Management said it is running ahead on revenue and margins and expects to set new targets at the next OFC.

They also pushed on Chinese competition. Management argued its technology and manufacturing consistency should allow it to sustain premium pricing.

WHAT STILL NEEDS TO PROVE OUT

Capacity expansion.

Lumentum has the demand. The remaining question is how quickly it can turn that demand into shipments and revenue without giving back the margin gains.
