## 原文

Big win for $AXTI holders today — and it came out of someone else's earnings call.

$LITE dropped a monster quarter last night. Revenue +109% YoY to $1.01B. Guide implies +130%. Stock +6% overnight.

Everyone is quoting the headline numbers. Almost nobody made it to minute 49.

That's where it got interesting for $AXTI .

1/ Pump lasers: +80% YoY. "Effectively sold out for the foreseeable future." 4x more coming over the next several quarters. The CEO puts their share at "somewhere between 70% and 80%."

2/ Then Wolfe's analyst asked about indium phosphide substrate.

The CEO: ultra-high-power laser demand "has surprised us." They'd spent the last three months trying to "secure even more substrate supply."

3/ So where did they go?

"We went out and we found additional substrate help from AXT. They are a great partner... we think we are going to need their help just given the surge in demand."

4/ And then the line nobody is quoting.

Asked whether today's supply is enough, he said they feel fine right now — "but given the rate of change in the demand vector that we are seeing, that may not be true a quarter or two from now either."

Translation: he may have to come back for more.

5/ OCS: shipments doubled Q3→Q4, and the Q1 guide includes the first triple-digit OCS revenue quarter.

But on the $400M target he was blunt: "I would not say tracking ahead." Careful before you extrapolate that one.

6/ Modeling CPO? Check your dates.

Lasers ship 2H CY2027. Customer scale-up deployments are CY2028 — not 2027. A lot of people are running a full year early on this.

Now zoom out.

A $64B company just told the market it needs more substrate from a $5B company. That is a 13:1 size gap between the buyer and the constraint.

Everyone is pricing the transceiver and the switch. The chokepoint sits one layer below.

Long $AXTI. All quotes verbatim from the $LITE Q4 FY26 earnings call, 11 Aug 2026. Not investment advice.
