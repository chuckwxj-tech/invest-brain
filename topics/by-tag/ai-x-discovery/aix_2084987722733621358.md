## 原文

Because trading memory equities is not the same as trading the underlying commodity:

- Multiple Compression at Peak Margin: 
Memory stocks historically top out 6 to 12 months before spot contract prices hit their peak. When gross margins breach historical highs, P/B and forward P/E multiples compress aggressively.

- Non-AI Demand Destruction: 
Starving non-AI segments (PC/smartphone DRAM volumes down year-over-year) leaves memory fabs entirely dependent on a single customer class: hyperscaler AI capex.

- LTA Default & Renegotiation History: 
Long-term agreements provide visibility until customer inventory buffers fill. In every past cycle, LTAs were renegotiated or delayed once downstream capacity caught up.

- Capex Supply Wall (2027–2028): Multi-billion-dollar cleanroom expansions currently underway will bring massive supply online right as software efficiency (model quantization, context pruning, CXL/NVMe offloading) lowers memory bandwidth requirements per query.

Which brings me to the Core Question: 

Are you pricing 2026 earnings, or discounting the cyclical margin peak into 2027?
