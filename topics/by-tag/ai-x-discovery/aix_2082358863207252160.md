## 原文

Huawei is targeting 2027 for mass production of semiconductor glass substrates through its domestic supply chain.

The goal is to integrate the technology into advanced AI accelerators and bypass ongoing U.S. export restrictions on traditional lithography equipment.

Key details:  
Partner suppliers in China plan to start localized volume manufacturing lines for Through-Glass Via (TGV) and core glass interposer technologies.

This approach helps improve processor density and thermal limits without needing restricted EUV tools.

The company separately outlined a longer-term trajectory to achieve 1.4-nanometer equivalent density scaling concepts by 2031.

Advantages of glass substrates for AI:

Superior resistance to heat and warpage compared to conventional organic or silicon interposers.

Up to 25% reduction in overall substrate thickness and roughly 30% lower power consumption.

Higher interconnect density for large-scale AI graphics processors and multi-layer stacking.

This represents another step in Huawei’s push for greater self-reliance in advanced packaging amid sanctions.

Worth watching how quickly domestic glass substrate technology matures and whether it delivers on performance at scale.
