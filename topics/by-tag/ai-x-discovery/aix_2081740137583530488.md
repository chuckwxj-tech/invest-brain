## 原文

Samsung's July 8 benchmark is the AI-inference datapoint I would save: a 1TB CXL pool held stable after KV-cache demand exceeded a 512GB DRAM setup.

Public-market map:
1. $MRVL - 260-lane CXL switch, up to 4TB/s; sampling Q3.
2. $MU - Q3 data-center SSD revenue >$5B, more than doubled q/q.
3. $WDC - capacity-storage cross-check; needs deployment/revenue proof.
4. $NVDA - compute dependency, not a pure CXL exposure.

What it proves: memory capacity is becoming a system bottleneck.
What it does not prove: hyperscaler deployment or durable margins.

Break case: CXL stays lab-stage, software complexity wins, or DRAM/SSD economics beat pooling.

Sources: Samsung (Jul 8), Marvell (Mar 17), Micron (Jun 24).
