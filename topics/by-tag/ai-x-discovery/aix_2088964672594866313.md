🇹🇼 MediaTek ($2454.TW)

🚨 MEDIATEK IS BUILDING A SECOND GROWTH ENGINE — FROM SMARTPHONES TO AI DATA-CENTER CHIPS

MediaTek is entering an important transformation phase.

Its traditional smartphone-chip business is facing pressure, but the company is aggressively expanding into AI data-center ASICs, automotive computing, edge AI and advanced connectivity.

The numbers matter:

Q2 2026 Revenue: NT$152.18B
Q2 Revenue Growth: +1.2% YoY
H1 2026 Revenue: NT$301.33B
H1 Revenue Change: −0.8% YoY
2026 AI Data-Center Revenue Target: US$2B+
2027 AI Data-Center Market Opportunity: ~US$80B
2027 Target AI-Chip Market Share: 15–20%
First Custom AI Data-Center Chip Production: Q4 2026
MediaTek has announced a US$5B financing plan to accelerate its AI data-center chip ambitions, with its first custom AI chip scheduled to enter production in Q4 2026. (Reuters)
🤖 THE BIG TRANSFORMATION
The old MediaTek story:
SMARTPHONES → MOBILE SOCs → 5G
The new story:
AI DATA CENTERS + AUTOMOTIVE AI + EDGE AI + CONNECTIVITY
That diversification could materially change MediaTek's long-term earnings profile.

☁️ DATA-CENTER ASICs ARE THE BIGGEST CATALYST

MediaTek expects more than US$2B of data-center ASIC revenue in 2026 and has previously targeted multiple billions of dollars in 2027.

The company is targeting 15–20% of an estimated US$80B AI-chip market in 2027.

That is potentially a completely new business of significant scale. (Reuters)

The strategic equation:

HYPERSCALERS → CUSTOM AI SILICON → ASIC DESIGN → MEDIATEK

This moves MediaTek into a market currently dominated by a small group of specialist AI-chip designers.

🧠 AI IS MOVING TO THE EDGE

MediaTek isn't betting only on data centers.

It is developing AI computing for:

Smartphones → Cars → Homes → Enterprise Devices → Edge Networks

Its latest platforms combine advanced computing with 5G, Wi-Fi 7/8, satellite connectivity and AI capabilities. (MediaTek)

That creates a potentially enormous long-term market as AI moves from centralized cloud infrastructure toward billions of connected devices.

🚗 AUTOMOTIVE IS ANOTHER GROWTH ENGINE

MediaTek is expanding aggressively into automotive computing.

Its Dimensity Auto platform targets:

• AI cockpits
• Infotainment
• Telematics
• Connectivity
• Driver-assistance systems

The company is also working with DENSO on custom ADAS chips.

Management expects significant revenue growth from automotive solutions during 2026. (MediaTek)

📱 SMARTPHONES REMAIN THE CASH ENGINE

The smartphone business is currently under pressure.

Q2 revenue was NT$152.18B, only 1.2% higher YoY, while the global smartphone market declined sharply.

That explains why MediaTek is accelerating investment into new growth areas rather than relying solely on mobile SoCs. (Reuters)

💰 THE OPTION VALUE IS HUGE
The most interesting part of MediaTek's story is the potential asymmetry.
Today:
SMARTPHONES = CORE BUSINESS
Tomorrow:

AI ASICs + AUTOMOTIVE + EDGE AI = NEW GROWTH ENGINES

If the company captures even a meaningful portion of the rapidly expanding custom-AI-chip market, the revenue mix could change dramatically.

⚠️ THE BIGGEST RISK

The biggest risk is execution.

MediaTek is entering a highly competitive AI-chip market where customers demand extremely high performance, software compatibility and reliability.

The smartphone business is also facing cyclical pressure and higher memory costs.

The AI opportunity is enormous, but converting design wins into large-scale recurring revenue will take time.
