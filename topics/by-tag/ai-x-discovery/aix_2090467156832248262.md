Advanced Packaging & PLP Market  🧵

▍FOPLP vs. CoPoS
These technologies target different needs. FOPLP focuses on lowering costs and expanding capacity for PMIC, RF, LEO, and MCU(L/S >10 μm). 

Meanwhile, CoPoS is built for AI GPUs, HPC, and ASICs, supporting larger package sizes and high-density heterogeneous integration(L/S < 5 μm).

▍The Shift to Panel
AI chip interposers are growing massively (2,800 mm² in '22 ➡️ 5,810 mm² in '25 ➡️ 7,885 mm² in '28). 

This drops 300mm wafer output from 21 chips to just 4. In contrast, a square panel can fit 35 chips, delivering an 8.75x efficiency boost over WLP!

▍Glass Technology
Glass substrates (carriers, cores, and interposers) have a bright future in advanced packaging. However, integrating processes like metallization and etching remains tough. 

Stress generated during manufacturing can cause cracks, so most players are still in the R&D or pilot stage rather than mass production.

▍PLP Market Explosion
Yole forecasts the Panel-Level Packaging (PLP) market will surge from $312M in 2025 to $2.96B in 2031 (45.5% CAGR). 

Mass production will scale rapidly between 2029–2030, driven by AI, HPC, and chiplets.

▍Advanced > Traditional
2026 will be a historic tipping point. The advanced packaging market is projected to hit $57.8B, surpassing traditional packaging ($55.5B) for the very first time.
