📈 Weekly Investor Take: Bulls Still Have the Edge

U.S. stocks had one of their strongest weeks in months, with the S&P 500 reaching a new record high as softer labor-market data and strong corporate earnings boosted hopes for a Fed-friendly soft landing.

Tech and AI stocks led the rebound, with semiconductor and software names showing renewed strength. The next major test is CPI inflation data, which could determine whether this rally has room to continue.

🚀 Biggest Bullish Stories

$PLTR — Palantir: Q2 revenue surged 93% YoY to $1.94B, beating expectations, while the company raised guidance across major metrics. U.S. commercial revenue growth is now expected to reach at least 134%, reinforcing the strength of enterprise AI demand.

$MRVL — Marvell: Shares jumped more than 10% after the company unveiled new AI memory and storage technologies targeting major bottlenecks in AI infrastructure, including CXL memory expansion and PCIe Gen6 storage.

$IONQ — IonQ: Q2 revenue reached $80.1M, up 287% YoY, beating expectations. IonQ also raised its 2026 revenue outlook to $280M–$290M, signaling accelerating commercial adoption of quantum computing.

Other strong names included $ALAB, $RDW and $SOUN, all benefiting from improving AI/infrastructure demand or stronger-than-expected earnings.

⚠️ Biggest Bearish Stories

$APP — AppLovin: Revenue grew 53%, but the slight revenue miss and softer-than-expected guidance disappointed investors despite strong cash generation.

$FIG — Figma: Revenue jumped 48%, but higher AI-related spending and a large GAAP operating loss pressured the stock.

$TTD — Trade Desk: Revenue of $715M missed expectations of $752M, while weak Q3 guidance triggered a sharp selloff.

Other stocks under pressure included $BROS, $WDC and $HUBS despite relatively solid results.

🔑 What I’m Watching

The market is still rewarding companies that can show real AI-driven revenue growth, expanding margins and strong cash flow. But high expectations mean even good earnings can trigger a selloff if guidance isn’t strong enough.

For next week, the biggest catalysts are:

CPI → Fed expectations → Treasury yields → AI/tech valuations → earnings

The bull market remains intact, but with indexes at record highs, the next leg higher will require earnings to continue justifying elevated valuations.
