## 原文

Broadcom is losing market share and Google is working with AMD on a TPU project for the v10 generation, according to SemiAnalysis. 

AVGO TPU Downward Revision (2026–2027)

> 2026 Production Cut: Lower TPU output is expected in 2H26 due to challenges ramping CoWoS-S supply. Total Broadcom 2026 CoWoS output is reduced from 250k to 215k wafers.

> TPU v7x (Ironwood): 2026 CoWoS output is reduced by ~32k wafers, lowering unit production by ~500k (from 3.2mn to 2.7mn). Upstream output now peaks in 2Q26 with a slight decline in run-rate in 2H.

> TPU v8i (Sunfish): Output is reduced by ~5k wafers, lowering 2026 unit production by ~80k.

> 2027 Outlook: Lower TPU output is expected compared to previous forecasts of 6mn units of TPU 7 and TPU 8i, driven by greater allocation toward other customers (total Broadcom CoWoS at ~460k wafers).

> Customer Allocations & Fluctuations: This includes 55k wafers (equivalent to 830k units of TPU 8i Sunfish) allocated to Meta's MTIA 400 Iris in 2027, alongside slightly more allocation for OpenAI's (OAI) Jalapeno ASIC. However, Meta's orders remain fluid and prone to shifting or abandonment.

AMD TPU Involvement

> v10 Generation Collaboration: Market chatter suggests Google is working with AMD on a TPU project for the v10 generation, marking AMD's first major involvement in a custom AI ASIC project.

> Strategic Strengths: AMD brings strong IP in advanced packaging, SoIC, and potential CPU IP—which is increasingly relevant as customers push for TPUs with on-board CPU cores for RL workloads.
