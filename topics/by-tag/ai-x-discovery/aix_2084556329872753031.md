CoPoS Moves Toward Commercialization, Taiwanese Suppliers Seize the Opportunity

$TSM and Intel are advancing glass-substrate packaging plans, with validation expected in 2026 and mass production targeted for 2028. Taiwanese equipment suppliers Grand Process Technology (3131), All Ring Tech (6187), and Gallant Precision (6640) are expected to benefit

AI chip packaging is shifting from wafer-level to panel-level production. Intel is working with Lens Technology on glass-substrate packaging, while TSMC continues to validate CoPoS, or Chip-on-Panel-on-Substrate

The next generation of CoPoS combines a glass core with through-glass-via, or TGV, technology, replacing round silicon wafers with large rectangular panels. This could raise material utilization from around 65% to more than 90%, reduce packaging costs by 20% to 30%, improve warpage control, and support AI packages larger than 14 reticle sizes

Glass substrates also offer low thermal expansion, high flatness, and strong high-frequency signal transmission. TSMC, Intel, Samsung, and SK Hynix have all included the technology in their advanced-packaging roadmaps

Grand Process Technology (3131) is expected to benefit from demand for wet-processing equipment used in cleaning, etching, photoresist stripping, surface treatment, and copper via filling

All Ring Tech (6187) should benefit from demand for large-format automation, material handling, inspection, and production logistics, as CoPoS panels are much larger than traditional 12-inch wafers

Gallant Precision (6640) is positioned to benefit from high-precision bonding, lamination, alignment, and handling equipment, which are especially important because glass substrates are fragile and require tighter process control
