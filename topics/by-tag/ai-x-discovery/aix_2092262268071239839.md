Three New Pieces Of AI Infrastructure

1/ IBM unveiled a 2 nm dual architecture processor at Hot Chips 2026 on 24 August.

The design contains 11 high performance cores running above 5.7 GHz.

Each core can execute Arm and IBM Z or LinuxONE instructions concurrently.

The chip also integrates AI inference accelerators for transaction fraud detection.

IBM added an on chip data processing unit for input and output acceleration.

2/ Cisco said on 25 August that it will add Supermicro servers to its Nvidia infrastructure portfolio.

The systems include both liquid cooled and air cooled configurations.

Cisco says the portfolio will support Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 and HGX Rubin NVL8 platforms.

Cisco will combine the servers with Silicon One networking and Nvidia Spectrum X networking.
Commercial availability is scheduled to begin in October 2026.

3/ Intel detailed Diamond Rapids Crescent Island and Wildcat Lake at Hot Chips 2026.

Diamond Rapids supports up to 256 cores with 1.28 GB of last level cache.

It also provides 16 memory channels at 12800 MT/s and 128 lanes of PCIe Gen6 and CXL 3.0.

Crescent Island contains 32 Xe cores and 256 XMX engines with up to 480 GB of LPDDR5X memory.
The inference GPU is designed as a 350 watt air cooled PCIe card.
Wildcat Lake uses Intel 18A and combines two performance cores with four efficiency cores plus an NPU rated at up to 17 TOPS.
