🔥 THE SIGNAL THAT MATTERS

The headline is obvious:

Revenue +150%.

But the truly important number is the ~76% adjusted gross margin.

That's the difference between a commodity hardware supplier and a specialized semiconductor platform.

Astera Labs provides connectivity solutions that allow GPUs, CPUs, memory and accelerators to communicate efficiently inside increasingly complex AI systems.

⚡ HFT DATA DESK VIEW

Watch the GPU → connectivity → memory → rack-scale AI transmission.

The sequence is:

More accelerators → more data movement → higher bandwidth → connectivity bottlenecks → retimers / switches → Astera Labs

As AI clusters become larger, the problem isn't simply computational power.

It is:

How quickly can all the components communicate?

That is where Astera's Aries retimers and Scorpio fabric switches become strategically important.

And the economics are attractive.

Revenue +150%

while maintaining approximately:

76% gross margin

That suggests significant pricing power and product differentiation.

The company is also expanding its platform into PCIe, CXL and UALink-related connectivity, giving it exposure to the next generation of heterogeneous AI systems.

Brutal Truth:

The AI semiconductor stack is becoming increasingly fragmented.

NVIDIA may provide the accelerator.

HBM provides the memory.

TSMC provides manufacturing.

But somewhere between all those components sits a less glamorous problem:

Connectivity.

Every time the AI cluster becomes larger, the amount of data moving between processors and memory increases.

That makes high-speed connectivity increasingly important.

And this is where Astera Labs sits.

The next valuation unlock is:

AI cluster expansion → connectivity content per system → revenue growth → 70%+ gross margins → operating leverage → free cash flow.

But the brutal risk is obvious:

The stock already reflects enormous expectations.

If AI infrastructure spending slows, or hyperscalers develop more connectivity internally, growth expectations could reset rapidly.

For now, however, the signal is powerful:

AI compute is scaling—and the connectivity layer is scaling even faster.

The GPU does the calculation.
The network moves the data.
Astera helps make the AI system talk.
