$LITE ER effects on $SIVE in the early session today. 

A bit long winded thoughts on this report but, I had some time so:

Revenue: $1.006B vs $987.7M exp, beat by $18.6M, up 109% YoY and +24.5% QoQ. 8th straight quarter of growth.

Non-GAAP EPS: $3.23 vs $2.99 exp, blowing through their own prior guide of $2.85-$3.05.

Margins are expanding very fast, GAAP gross margin 49%, GAAP op margin 28%. Non-GAAP gross margin crossed 50% (they didn't expect that until a $2B/qtr run rate), non-GAAP op margin at 36.6%, up 2,150bps YoY.

Headline GAAP net loss of -$7.16B / -$84.65 EPS looks scary but it's 100% a paper charge, they equitized $1.1B (35%) of their converts, triggering a one-time non-cash $7.8B hit. Actual non-GAAP net income was a record $326M.

Guidance for Q1 FY27: $1.225-1.275B revenue (+130% YoY), non-GAAP op margin 39.5-40.5%, EPS $4.05-4.35. They're hitting their $1.25B/qtr target a full quarter early. $2.74B cash + ST investments on the balance sheet.

Two things we don't think the Street is pricing in properly:

1. Share count just jumped from 95.2M to 101.1M diluted (non-GAAP) in one quarter from that convert equitization, that's +6.2% dilution. It's already in the Q1 guide, but a lot of people's spreadsheets are still running last quarter's share count, so "beats" from here might look smaller per-share than the actual dollar profit growth.

2. Everyone's watching the transceiver/OCS headlines but the real bottleneck (and moat) is upstream, components revenue ($649M, +103% YoY) is now outgrowing Systems, and mgmt is guiding a 4x increase in pump laser shipments plus 130%+ growth in narrow-linewidth laser assemblies. 

The actual constraint on how fast they can ride the CPO/NPO wave into 2027-2028 is capacity at their two indium phosphide fabs in Japan, not anything happening at the transceiver level. That's the least talked-about line in the whole print.

Happy with the print and what it means for the photonics sector as a whole, there's much more to come. This is a sector that "can" have constant updates and sustainable growth.
