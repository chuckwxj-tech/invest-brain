## 原文

It makes no sense that $AMD would use Intels EMIB….. 🤔

AMD have recently announced advanced packaging collaborations with TSMC, Powertech, ASE/SPIL….. using their CoWoS, SOIC, EFB packaging technologies and collaborating with them closely. 

Why would AMD hand over their design knowledge to Intel?!?!?! 🤔

AMD using EMIB would require them to provide enough package-level design information to make Intel’s bridge implementation work, die dimensions, bump maps and pitch, I/O placement, bandwidth/latency targets, power-delivery constraints, HBM topology, thermal targets, package/substrate stack-up, and test specifications.

Something AMD DO NOT want to share with their rival Intel whatsoever ☝️

There are more than enough advanced packaging players out there.
