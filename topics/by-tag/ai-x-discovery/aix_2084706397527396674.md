## 原文

GPU utilization in AI inference is increasingly limited by memory access rather than raw compute.

As KV caches grow into hundreds of gigabytes, HBM becomes too expensive to scale further while NVMe introduces too much latency.

Marvell’s Photonic Fabric creates a new memory tier by enabling optical shared memory across AI racks up to 30 meters apart. 

It delivers around 350 ns near-NUMA latency and 7.2 Tbps of bandwidth per module. This lets capacity scale independently of compute while keeping warm caches close to the accelerators.

The approach addresses a core bottleneck in next-generation inference by improving utilization and token efficiency without forcing every byte into scarce HBM.

Optical memory fabrics are emerging as a practical way to reshape how AI infrastructure handles large context and long-running agentic workloads.
