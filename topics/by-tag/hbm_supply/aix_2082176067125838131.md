Fubon Research: Sector Snapshot 

Advanced Packaging & CoWoS Forecast

> TSMC Capacity: Forecasted to reach 180 kwpm by the end of 2027, and 220 kwpm by the end of 2028, with potential upside as TSMC slows its SoIC expansion to prioritize CoWoS.

> ASE Capacity: Revised up to 50 kwpm (and 70k in 4Q28) to support CPU and networking customers.

> NVIDIA Allocation: Remains TSMC’s largest customer with over 50% of allocation (estimated total capacity to hit 1.03 mn in 2027 vs. 629k in 2026), alongside extra capacity from SPIL for Vera CPU production.

> AMD & MediaTek: AMD's allocation is estimated to grow significantly to 300k in 2027 (mostly via ASE), while MediaTek is expected to gain more capacity allocation, potentially raising its custom-ASIC market share.

Chip Output & BOM Cost Adjustments

> NVIDIA Output: Following adjustments to the Rubin Ultra GPU interposer size, NVIDIA's total chip output is estimated to rise to 12.4 mn in 2027 vs. 8.2 mn in 2026.

> Short-Term Headwinds: Rubin’s volume forecast is revised down to around 1 mn due to thermal design changes and production push-outs, which may negatively impact suppliers given Rubin's higher dollar content than Blackwell.

> HBM & Pricing Costs: NVIDIA’s HBM4 cost is expected to increase to US$31–32 per GB (up from US$17–18 for HBM3e in 2026). Rubin GPU prices are estimated to skyrocket to US$78–80K, while other GPU/custom-ASIC project HBM costs could reach US$35–36 per GB in 2027.

NVIDIA's New Rack Architecture Design

> Following the cancellation of the Kyper rack, NVIDIA is working on new rack architectures keeping the same number of compute dies (576 per rack).

> Design Options: Two different designs are currently evaluated—a conservative design using the current Oberon rack layout (four racks connecting via traditional scale-out), and a two-rack design utilizing NPO/CPO for scale-out connections.

Google’s 2028 Target & Supply Chain

> Despite concerns over capex and negative FCF, Google aims to deploy 12–15 mn in-house design TPUs by 2028, entering the V9 generation.

> Achieving this target will likely require supply from Intel alongside TSMC. Intel's EMIT capacity is projected to scale from 10–12k by late 2026 to 24–25k per month by late 2027, backed by aggressive capex toward advanced packaging.

Stock Picks & Recommendations

> Short-Term Caution: NVIDIA's supply chain could temporarily underperform in 2H26 due to lower Rubin volumes and design changes.

Top Picks:

> ASE remains the top pick for the CPU supply chain due to high exposure in Vera and Venice.

> MediaTek is the top pick for TPU, with recent pullbacks viewed as re-entry opportunities.
