## 原文

@jukan05 $MRVL sells custom silicon in exchange for…giving away its stock!

Not really…

It’s like a credit card rewards bonus: get this new card and spend $3000 in 3 months and you get a $300 bonus.

In the case of $MRVL, it incentivizes $GOOGL to buy more Marvell products. 

If $GOOGL goes agog for MRVL products, $GOOGL could make money back in the form of stock price appreciation.

It’s clever. Instead of a honey trap it’s a money trap to lock in sales with a valued customer.

Notice in the filing that it mentions memory a couple of times:

1. Memory Interface Controller:

This manages the movement of data between compute and memory. In $MRVL’s broader portfolio, this includes technologies around HBM and CXL memory. 

Marvell’s custom HBM architecture is designed to improve the connection between AI accelerators and HBM. 

Marvell says its approach can provide 33% greater memory capacity while reducing memory-interface power by 70%. 

2. Near Memory Compute

If you put some compute near the memory, you stop moving so much data in the first place and the memory chips you buy from $SKHY, $MU, or Samsung can be used more efficiently.

$MRVL is #2 in custom silicon behind $AVGO. It has been focusing less on XPU chip design and more on everything else in the data center: 

- leading photonics and more efficient copper connectivity to deal with the copper bottleneck;

- connecting chips to memory and memory to compute to deal with the memory bottleneck; 

- making everything more energy efficient to deal with the energy and cooling problems.

As the Swiss Army knife of data centers and the MacGyver of memory, copper, and energy bottlenecks, $MRVL is going to be a major beneficiary of memory scarcity.

$MRVL can help with XPUs, TPUs, and — thanks to a deal with $NVDA — GPUs.

So $MSFT $AMZN $META $GOOGL all want their help. How about $ORCL and $SPCX next?

And this is just the tip of the iceberg. Remember the Wall Street Journal article last week? Or the Nikkei article before that? The inexorable process of building data centers will offer a multitude of opportunities for $MRVL to increase revenues and become the go-to data-center company.
