## 原文

@EricJhonsa Jensen $NVDA pointed out that every major hyperscaler has underbuilt relative to demand, and that forecasts keep rising because the need for more compute keeps accelerating. There is no comfortable “number two” position — the race for capacity is existential for their AI businesses.

“In this new world of AI, compute is revenues. Without compute, there’s no way to generate tokens. Without tokens, there’s no way to grow revenues. So in this new world of AI, compute equals revenues.”

“Every CSP understands this now, every hyperscaler understands this, that CapEx translates to compute, compute with the right architecture translates to maximizing revenues and compute equals revenues. Without investing capacity today, without investing in compute, there cannot be revenue growth and that I think everybody understands. Compute equals revenues.”

Jensen’s consistent message across recent earnings calls is:
•  In the AI era, compute capacity = the ability to generate tokens.
•  Tokens are now monetized / profitable.
•  Therefore, hyperscalers that do not keep investing in more compute will not be able to grow revenue.
•  This is why CapEx keeps rising and why falling behind in capacity is extremely costly.

 $MU, $COHR, and $LITE are direct beneficiaries of the trend Jensen Huang describes (hyperscalers must keep investing heavily because compute = revenue).
