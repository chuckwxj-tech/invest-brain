39 mid cap companies report between tonight and tomorrow crazy day, these are our picks

$NOVT  (Novanta, $5.7B, Chip Making, Coil 9) — Precision motion and photonics for robotics, medical, and semiconductor. AI is now ~15% of sales and growing fast, with robotics bookings up 50%. Street expects +12.4% revenue and $0.83 EPS — that bar looks easy given the trajectory and the fact that peers FORM and LRCX just confirmed blazing demand. Beat history is 5/7. The bull case is a full-year organic revenue guidance raise from 3-5% to 7-8% on AI mix shift plus H2 margin expansion. The risk is tariffs compressing gross margin by 100bps and drowning the revenue beat. Management tone has been increasingly confident.

$KLIC  (Kulicke & Soffa, $4.9B, Chip Making, Coil 9) — Semiconductor packaging equipment, both wire bonding and thermo-compression bonding (TCB) for AI chip packaging. TCB is sole-source technology. Revenue accelerated +63% sequentially last quarter. The +90% revenue bar sounds scary but it's a soft prior-year comp — easy to clear. Peers AEHR and FORM printed huge beats. Beat history is 5/7. The bull case is TCB order book exceeding $250M or a firm HBM TCB volume order from a top-3 memory customer. The risk is margin compression even if revenue meets — mix could tilt toward lower-margin wire-bonding, and 39% of revenue comes from Chinese OSATs exposed to credit shock. Important caveat: the semi equipment sector has been punishing even good results lately (ASYS, AMKR precedent) — KLIC's numbers could be great and the stock still sells off on sector flow.

$VIAV (Viavi Solutions, $10B, Networking, Coil 8) — Test and measurement plus optical security products. Hyperscaler fiber monitoring surge is driving explosive growth — revenue up 40% in the trailing four quarters. Street expects +52% revenue and $0.30 EPS. That's a high bar, but 6/7 beat history says management guides conservatively, and Spirent HSE contribution is layering in. The bull case is revenue at $430M+ with a Q1 FY2027 guide showing data-center instrument revenue growing sequentially. The risk is that the hyperscaler fiber-monitoring wave is front-loaded and management signals normalization — revenue near the bar with no upside and forward outlook declining. Tone has been confident.

$VECO (Veeco Instruments, $3.3B, Chip Making, Coil 8) — Semiconductor process equipment: laser annealing, ion beam, MOCVD. The InP laser manufacturing business for AI interconnects is the key catalyst — order book reportedly exploding. Street expects only +7.6% revenue growth, which is behind the curve given the soft comps and peer performance. Beat history is 5/7. The bull case is backlog exceeding $650M (up from $555M), FY2026 revenue guidance raised to above $1.2B implying 20%+ growth, and gross margins recovering to 42%+. The risk is a margin disaster — gross margins at 38% or below would mean an EPS miss even on a revenue beat. EBITDA was down 67% last quarter, so the margin story needs to turn here.

$ALNT (Allient, $1.6B, Power Systems, Coil 8) — Precision motion control and power components for industrial, defense, and data center applications. Book-to-bill is 1.14x with a $251M backlog. The beat history is 6/7, which is strong — suggests conservative guidance. Revenue has been flat to slightly up and EPS consensus is $0.62. The bull case is data center cooling and power applications accelerating demand, and the 6/7 history makes another beat the base case. The concern is that margins have declined four consecutive quarters from 13.8% down to 11.8% — if that trend continues, even a revenue meet could produce an EPS miss.

$BKH (Black Hills, $5.5B, Utilities, Coil 7) — Regulated utility in Wyoming and South Dakota. The real story here is the 1.8GW hyperscale data center opportunity in Cheyenne — they've already received a $201M customer deposit from Crusoe/Tallgrass. Revenue is down 36% but that's an accounting mirage from pass-through energy costs; EPS at $0.43 is the real bar. Beat history is weak at 2/7. The bull case is the definitive signing of that 1.8GW contract, which would make the $201M CIAC non-refundable and unlock billions in incremental investment. The risk is the deal collapsing — if the customer walks, the $201M gets returned. META's -9% post-earnings makes the market nervous about AI capex sustainability in general.

$PKOH (Park-Ohio, $0.6B, Power Systems, Coil 7) — Industrial supply chain management and manufactured components. This one is a catalyst play, not a momentum play. The street expects +5% revenue and $0.81 EPS, which isn't an easy bar given only 2/7 beat history. The bull case is singular: an announcement of a definitive agreement to sell Southwest Steel, which drags $0.53/share annually. If that sale is announced, the stock could gap 15-25%. The risk is a revenue and EPS miss with no update on the Southwest Steel strategic review. LBRT at -22% signals broader industrial softness.

$CECO (CECO Environmental, $2.5B, Chip Making, Coil 7) — Industrial air quality and fluid handling, recently acquired Thermon for power super-cycle exposure. Record backlog with book-to-bill at 2.2x. The +55% revenue bar is mostly Thermon's contribution, so it's fair, and EPS at $0.27 looks easier. Beat history is 4/7. The bull case is gross margin above 35%, guidance raise above $1.6B pro-forma revenue, and a second consecutive $400M+ bookings quarter pushing backlog above $1.5B. The risk is margin failure — if gross margin stays in the high-20s and EBITDA keeps declining, the acquisition story falls apart. KLAC at -10.8% shows strong secular themes don't protect against execution misses. Tone has been confident.

$DNOW  (Dnow, $1.7B, Utilities, Coil 7) — Energy and industrial distribution, recently merged with Whitecap. They're dealing with SAP ERP migration disruption and targeting $350M in 2027 EBITDA. The +111% revenue bar is post-merger arithmetic, not real organic growth. EPS at $0.08 doesn't assume heroics, and 5/7 beat history is solid. SLB's +11% peer reaction signals a healthy energy backdrop. The bull case is an adjusted EBITDA margin of 4.5%+ which would crush EPS estimates (likely $0.12+), proving the trough is behind and synergy capture is ahead of schedule. The risk is a second consecutive quarter of ERP-driven disruption — if customer recapture is failing and integration is worse than feared, the $350M EBITDA target starts looking ambitious.
