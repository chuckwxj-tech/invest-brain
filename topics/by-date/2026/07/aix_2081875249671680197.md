$CNDS KEY READ-THROUGHS FROM CADENCE DESIGN SYSTEMS Q2 2026 EARNINGS CALL

Cadence’s Q2 2026 earnings call provided a high-quality, broad-based confirmation that AI infrastructure investment is increasing the number, complexity and strategic importance of semiconductor and system designs rather than merely increasing demand for a narrow group of merchant accelerators. The most important signals were the record $8.1b backlog despite a relatively low renewal year, approximately 24% recurring-revenue growth, normalized recurring growth in the high-teens to approximately 20%, 18% core EDA growth, greater than 40% semiconductor IP growth, another record hardware quarter and 37% System Design and Analysis growth. The breadth indicates that the current cycle is propagating across custom silicon, advanced-node implementation, analog and mixed-signal design, memory interfaces, networking, verification, chiplets, 3DIC, advanced packaging, PCB design and multiphysics simulation. The strongest positive read-throughs are for Cadence, custom-ASIC enablers, memory and interconnect suppliers, advanced-packaging providers and foundries with credible leading-edge ecosystems. The principal negative read-throughs are relative competitive pressure on Synopsys, longer-duration merchant-accelerator share dilution from hyperscaler custom silicon, potential commoditization of the top-layer AI agent interface, Cadence’s H2 margin investment and the explicit export-control assumption embedded in guidance. Management’s statement that no sudden step-function in agentic AI monetization is incorporated into guidance improves the quality of the current growth signal because the raised outlook is being supported by already observable design activity, add-on business and product demand rather than a highly speculative AI revenue forecast.

SOURCE MATERIAL: Cadence Design Systems Q2 2026 earnings call transcript.

EDA AND ENGINEERING SOFTWARE

ADD-ON-DRIVEN BOOKINGS IN A LOW-RENEWAL YEAR CONFIRM REAL CONSUMPTION GROWTH RATHER THAN CONTRACT-CALENDAR TIMING (READ-THROUGH 1)

AFFECTED COMPANIES AND IMPACT: Cadence Design Systems (CDNS: US) — Positive, high magnitude. Synopsys (SNPS: US) — Positive industry-demand read-through, moderate magnitude.

CALL EVIDENCE: Cadence exited Q2 with record backlog of $8.1b. An analyst calculated that H1 bookings appeared to be approximately 55% higher year over year. Management did not explicitly validate that precise calculation, but confirmed that 2026 is one of the lower years in Cadence’s 3-year renewal cycle, that H1 normally draws down backlog, and that the company was nevertheless experiencing “very, very good strength in add-on opportunities.” Recurring revenue grew approximately 24% year over year, including approximately 4 percentage points from Hexagon, with normalized pro forma recurring growth described as high-teens to approximately 20%.

NEAR-TERM TRADING CATALYST: The record backlog and add-on strength increase confidence in H2 revenue visibility and reduce the probability that the Q2 performance was driven primarily by a large renewal cohort, isolated hardware shipments or acquired revenue. The raised FY outlook is therefore supported by stronger underlying consumption than would be indicated by reported revenue alone. The signal is particularly constructive for Cadence because add-ons typically enter revenue through increased license capacity, additional products, expanded workflows or larger enterprise commitments.

LONGER-DURATION FUNDAMENTAL SHIFT: Add-on business is more economically informative than scheduled renewals. A renewal can preserve an existing revenue base without demonstrating rising customer activity. An add-on generally indicates that a customer requires more tools, more licenses, more verification capacity or broader workflow coverage. The result suggests that semiconductor and systems customers are increasing design throughput and complexity within existing contracts. This supports an upward shift in EDA spending as a percentage of semiconductor R&D and increases the likelihood that elevated growth can persist beyond the current renewal calendar.

TRANSMISSION MECHANISM: More chip designs, more design variants and more difficult verification requirements increase tool concurrency and capacity requirements. Customers add digital implementation, analog simulation, signoff, verification, IP, emulation and packaging products to existing relationships. These additions expand annual contract value and backlog without waiting for the next full enterprise renewal. The same design-intensity conditions should be directionally favorable for Synopsys, although the relative allocation of the growth between the 2 leading vendors remains a separate competitive question.

INVESTMENT IMPLICATION: Cadence’s backlog performance should be interpreted as evidence of real end-market design consumption rather than a temporary billing or renewal anomaly. This is one of the highest-quality positive signals in the call. It also provides an important positive sector read-through for EDA demand, even where Cadence may be capturing a disproportionate share of the incremental activity.

AGENTIC AI IS EXPANDING THE DESIGN SPACE AND INCREASING DEMAND FOR CORE EDA ENGINES (READ-THROUGH 2)

AFFECTED COMPANIES AND IMPACT: Cadence Design Systems (CDNS: US) — Positive, high magnitude over the long term and moderate magnitude in the near term. Synopsys (SNPS: US) — Positive, moderate-to-high industry impact, subject to relative product execution.

CALL EVIDENCE: Management stated, “Agentic AI is a demand accelerator for Cadence as autonomous agents expand the design exploration space and call our underlying physically accurate engines more often.” ChipStack had more than 20 customer engagements and was already deployed in production across multiple chip designs. ViraStack had more than 25 engagements and reportedly delivered 2x-10x productivity improvements. A joint NVIDIA demonstration produced greater than 40x faster RTL validation, reducing a typical 5-week verification cycle to less than 1 day. AuraStack was described as delivering up to 15x higher productivity and 2x faster time to market in PCB and advanced-packaging design.

NEAR-TERM TRADING CATALYST: Early add-on business from agentic workflows is already contributing to customer expansions, but management explicitly stated that it is “still not assuming a sudden step function” in guidance. The near-term earnings contribution should therefore be expected to emerge through incremental workflow licenses and additional use of underlying EDA tools rather than an immediate, separately visible agentic AI revenue stream. This reduces the risk that current guidance depends on an aggressive adoption assumption.

LONGER-DURATION FUNDAMENTAL SHIFT: The call challenges the labor-displacement interpretation of AI in semiconductor design. Management’s operating assumption is that customers are not seeking to perform the same amount of design work with fewer engineers; they are attempting to address complexity that is increasing faster than engineering headcount can scale. Agents therefore allow engineering teams to explore more architectures, verify more alternatives and optimize across more parameters. The likely outcome is higher design throughput and more EDA-engine consumption rather than lower software demand.

TRANSMISSION MECHANISM: Cadence described a commercial model in which customers continue purchasing the underlying EDA software and separately purchase teams of agent licenses that orchestrate engineering workflows. Revenue can expand through 2 channels: direct sales of new agentic workflow products and incremental capacity for simulation, verification, implementation and signoff engines. The second channel is strategically more important because it reinforces the existing recurring-software base. Agents that generate more RTL, explore more floorplans, test more analog configurations or optimize more package alternatives create additional calls to deterministic tools that establish timing, power, area, signal-integrity and manufacturability ground truth.

COMPETITIVE SIGNIFICANCE: The agent layer does not eliminate the requirement for production-grade EDA. Advanced-node designs remain dependent on silicon-correlated physics engines, foundry-certified methodologies, proprietary design data and reliable signoff. This supports both major EDA vendors at the industry level. Cadence’s advantage will depend on whether its full-flow breadth and integration allow it to capture a disproportionate share of the resulting usage.

INVESTMENT IMPLICATION: Agentic AI should be underwritten primarily as an EDA demand multiplier rather than as a stand-alone software category with immediate hypergrowth. The more defensible earnings opportunity is increased consumption of existing high-value engines, with agent-license revenue representing additional upside. This framework is more durable than a thesis based solely on proprietary chatbot or copilot pricing.

CADENCE’S SHARE GAINS CREATE A MATERIAL RELATIVE NEGATIVE FOR SYNOPSYS ACROSS DIGITAL, SIGNOFF, ANALOG, IP AND HARDWARE (READ-THROUGH 3)

AFFECTED COMPANIES AND IMPACT: Cadence Design Systems (CDNS: US) — Positive, high magnitude. Synopsys (SNPS: US) — Negative relative impact, moderate-to-high magnitude. Rambus (RMBS: US) — Negative relative impact in selected memory and interface IP categories, moderate magnitude.

CALL EVIDENCE: Core EDA grew 18% year over year. Cadence cited expanded Tempus and Certus signoff adoption at hyperscalers, leading semiconductor companies, startups, frontier AI companies and a large ASIC silicon vendor. Management disclosed a significant Spectre competitive win at a leading semiconductor supplier, several Spectre FX production wins, 12 new hardware logos and a hardware competitive win at a major AI infrastructure provider. Semiconductor IP grew greater than 40%, with management stating that most of the increase was organic and that improved power, performance and area characteristics were enabling wins in opportunities where Cadence would not have competed approximately 2 years earlier. Management also said its competitive position “has never been better.”

NEAR-TERM TRADING CATALYST: Competitive wins should support further recurring add-ons, improved renewal economics and stronger bookings. EDA share gains tend to become visible progressively rather than in a single quarter because customers deploy tools across individual programs before incorporating them into larger enterprise agreements. The Q2 bookings and recurring-revenue strength suggest that this conversion is already occurring.

LONGER-DURATION FUNDAMENTAL SHIFT: EDA share is unusually durable because a successful tool is embedded in design flows, scripts, libraries, verification environments, foundry methodologies and engineering practices. Winning implementation or signoff on a leading-edge program can create a multi-year position and improve the probability of selling adjacent products. Signoff is especially strategic because the signoff vendor becomes the trusted authority for whether a design meets timing, power and manufacturing requirements. Strength in signoff can pull through implementation, agentic optimization and verification.

TRANSMISSION MECHANISM: Cadence is broadening from traditional strengths in analog and place-and-route into deeper digital signoff, verification hardware, advanced packaging, semiconductor IP and system analysis. Each additional position increases wallet share and reduces the number of workflow boundaries where a competitor can control the customer relationship. The improved Intel and Samsung positions are especially relevant because Cadence acknowledged that it had historically been weaker in those ecosystems.

IMPLICATION FOR SYNOPSYS: The negative read-through is relative rather than an assertion of absolute revenue contraction. The overall market is expanding rapidly enough for Synopsys to continue growing. However, Cadence’s evidence of share gains across multiple contested categories increases the risk that Synopsys grows below the expanding market in selected accounts or must invest more aggressively in pricing, product development and customer support to defend positions. The risk is greatest where Cadence has gained signoff credibility, improved leading-node IP quality or secured broader strategic relationships.

IMPLICATION FOR RAMBUS: Cadence’s emphasis on memory IP, chip-to-chip IP and interface IP creates competitive pressure for specialist IP vendors. Expanding demand for HBM, CXL, PCIe, UCIe and next-generation memory interfaces enlarges the market, but Cadence’s improved PPA and broader foundry enablement may allow it to internalize more of the value within full-flow customer relationships.

INVESTMENT IMPLICATION: Cadence should not be viewed solely as a beneficiary of industry growth. The call indicates that above-market growth is also being driven by wallet-share gains. Relative positioning between Cadence and Synopsys is therefore becoming as important as the aggregate EDA demand environment.

OPEN-MODEL CHOICE AND CUSTOMER-BUILT AGENTS MAY CAP THE PROPRIETARY AGENT-LAYER TAKE RATE (READ-THROUGH 4)

AFFECTED COMPANIES AND IMPACT: Cadence Design Systems (CDNS: US) — Negative to the most aggressive agent-revenue expectations, moderate magnitude; positive to the core-engine moat. Synopsys (SNPS: US) — Similar mixed impact, moderate magnitude.

CALL EVIDENCE: Management stated that customers are demanding choice in LLMs and may maintain their own agents, just as they maintain proprietary design flows. The CEO said, “What is likely to happen is at the agentic layer, there will be a lot of choices for LLMs.” Management argued that differentiation resides in knowledge graphs, engineering mental models, reinforcement loops, deep API access, deterministic EDA engines and specialized compute rather than in the underlying horizontal language model alone.

NEAR-TERM TRADING CATALYST: The company is approximately 6 months into commercial engagement with its agentic products, and early add-on business is encouraging, but pricing, attach rates, renewal terms and agent-specific revenue have not been quantified. The absence of a sudden step-function assumption in guidance is prudent, but it also means that near-term agent monetization should not be capitalized as though a dominant proprietary platform has already been established.

LONGER-DURATION FUNDAMENTAL SHIFT: The agent layer is likely to become multi-model and partially customer-controlled. Customers may use Cadence agents, internally developed agents or third-party orchestration while continuing to rely on Cadence or Synopsys engines for verification, implementation and signoff. This would preserve the core EDA franchise but reduce the exclusivity and pricing power of the top-layer agent product.

TRANSMISSION MECHANISM: If the LLM and orchestration layers commoditize, customers can substitute models without replacing the underlying engineering tools. Economic value would concentrate in proprietary design data, validated workflows, physics-based engines, foundry certification and APIs. Cadence could still benefit through increased tool consumption, but the incremental agent-license revenue per customer may be lower than implied by a fully closed, vertically integrated agent platform.

COMPETITIVE SIGNIFICANCE: Cadence’s 3-layer architecture is a defense against model commoditization because it does not require the company to own the dominant LLM. The risk is that customers internalize more of the orchestration layer and treat agent software as a lower-value interface to the EDA engines. The opportunity is that Cadence remains the required system of record and execution environment regardless of the selected model.

INVESTMENT IMPLICATION: The highest-confidence agentic AI value should be assigned to increased EDA-engine demand. A more conservative value should be assigned to proprietary agent-layer revenue until attach rates, contract values and customer retention are disclosed. This distinction is important for both Cadence and Synopsys valuation frameworks.

CUSTOM SILICON, COMPUTE AND NETWORKING

HYPERSCALER CUSTOM SILICON PROGRAM COUNTS ARE ACCELERATING, BENEFITING ASIC ENABLERS WHILE DILUTING LONG-RUN MERCHANT GPU SHARE (READ-THROUGH 5)

AFFECTED COMPANIES AND IMPACT: Broadcom (AVGO: US) — Positive, high magnitude. Marvell Technology (MRVL: US) — Positive, high magnitude. Alphabet (GOOGL: US) — Positive, moderate-to-high long-duration impact. https://t.co/SpqvHNV5fi (AMZN: US) — Positive, moderate-to-high long-duration impact. Microsoft (MSFT: US) — Positive, moderate-to-high long-duration impact. Meta Platforms (META: US) — Positive, moderate-to-high long-duration impact. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSM: Taiwan) — Positive, high magnitude. NVIDIA (NVDA: US) — Negative to long-duration share, moderate magnitude, but not a near-term demand-collapse signal.

CALL EVIDENCE: Management stated that hyperscaler commitment to silicon is “much higher than like 12 months ago” and that the number of designs being undertaken by each hyperscaler is “impressive.” The CEO said large customers expect to continue using standard products from leading semiconductor suppliers such as NVIDIA while also developing their own custom silicon and multiple versions optimized for different memory-access and networking requirements. Management also cited renewed startup activity in AI, networking and CPUs.

NEAR-TERM TRADING CATALYST: Increasing program counts benefit EDA vendors and custom-silicon partners before chips enter production because architecture, implementation, verification, IP selection and tapeout spending occur during development. Broadcom and Marvell were not identified by Cadence as specific partners on the call; their positive attribution is a public-market inference based on their established custom-silicon and connectivity business models. More hyperscaler designs increase the available project pipeline, potential non-recurring engineering revenue and subsequent production opportunities.

LONGER-DURATION FUNDAMENTAL SHIFT: Hyperscalers are moving from purchasing largely standardized compute infrastructure toward owning a diversified portfolio of workload-specific silicon. This is likely to create separate accelerators for training, inference, recommendation, networking, storage, security and memory orchestration. Custom silicon increases hyperscaler control over system cost, power efficiency, software integration and supply-chain strategy.

TRANSMISSION MECHANISM FOR ASIC ENABLERS: Each additional silicon program requires architecture support, IP integration, physical design, packaging, verification and manufacturing coordination. Custom-ASIC vendors can monetize design services, intellectual property, chip revenue and, in some cases, networking or interconnect content. Program proliferation is more important than the success of any single chip because it expands the total number of addressable engagements.

TRANSMISSION MECHANISM FOR HYPERSCALERS: Proprietary silicon can lower total cost of ownership for sufficiently large internal workloads, reduce dependence on a single merchant supplier and optimize performance for proprietary software stacks. The economic benefit is realized through lower infrastructure cost per unit of compute and improved workload efficiency, although development risk and execution complexity increase.

TRANSMISSION MECHANISM FOR NVIDIA: The call does not support a near-term collapse in NVIDIA demand. Management explicitly stated that hyperscalers expect to use both merchant products and custom silicon. The longer-duration negative is a lower merchant-GPU share of incremental workloads than would occur in a fully standardized market. Custom accelerators can also increase buyer leverage and constrain pricing power in workload categories where an internal substitute becomes credible.

INVESTMENT IMPLICATION: The most actionable conclusion is a positive custom-silicon program-cycle read-through for Broadcom, Marvell, TSMC and the hyperscalers themselves. The corresponding NVIDIA risk is strategic and multi-year rather than an immediate earnings shortfall. AI infrastructure growth can remain strong while the composition gradually becomes more heterogeneous and less exclusively merchant-GPU based.
