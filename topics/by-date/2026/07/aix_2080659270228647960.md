## 原文

This is the most important chart in memory right now (Save this)

Servers and graphics were 30% of SK hynix's DRAM revenue a decade ago

Today it's 69%

Memory is so scarce this industry is not just raising prices, it's redesigning the architecture

Everyone still treats memory as a price cycle like the last 25 years

But what you need to realize is that the buyer has changed, and so to has the "cycle"

In Q4 2013 SK hynix's DRAM dollars came from PCs (29%) and phones (34%). By Q1 2026 it's servers (47%) and graphics incl HBM (22%), and the inflection started right at ChatGPT's launch in late 2022

It's a structural demand shift, not a spot price spike

But the mix shift is only part of why this matters

AI inference generates so much working data (the KV caches behind every chatbot response) that HBM and DDR physically can't hold it all. Demand outgrew what memory could supply

So the industry's response is to invent an entirely new tier of memory: CXL

Samsung starts mass producing CXL 3.2 modules this year, SK hynix's new IMTE architecture improves inference efficiency 35.7% and Micron is accelerating its own CXL roadmap

And the buyers are already here: Nvidia's Vera Rubin ships with CXL support, Intel and AMD are adding it, Microsoft is piloting CXL cloud servers

That's a brand new product category layered ON TOP of HBM demand, not replacing it

This 35% pullback in memory stocks was a gift to those who are still not allocated (or those who want to buy more)
