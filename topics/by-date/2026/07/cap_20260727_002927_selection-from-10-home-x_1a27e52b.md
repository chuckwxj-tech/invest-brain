Qualcomm Notifies Customers of "Double-Digit" Price Increases Amid Supply Chain Cost Pressures  
Effective from Shipments Starting September 1... Proliferation of Parts and Rising Costs Make Pass-Through Inevitable  
高通通知客户将"两位数"涨价，因供应链成本压力
自 9 月 1 日起出货生效...零部件数量激增与成本上涨，转嫁成为必然

U.S.-based Qualcomm has notified its customer companies of double-digit percentage price increases, citing rising component costs and supply chain pressures.  
美国高通已通知其客户公司将进行两位数百分比的涨价，原因在于零部件成本上涨和供应链压力。

Bloomberg reported on the 24th local time that Qualcomm had sent a letter to its customer companies outlining its policy on product price hikes.  
彭博社当地时间 24 日报道称，高通已向其客户公司发送信件，概述了其产品涨价政策。

These price increases will be abruptly applied to products shipped after September 1.  
这些价格上涨将突然适用于 9 月 1 日之后发货的产品。

Through the letter, Qualcomm explained that it has reached the limit of absorbing supplier cost increases at the company level and, despite implementing efforts to secure new supply chains for alternative parts, price adjustments could not be avoided.  
在信中，高通解释说，公司层面已无法承受供应商成本上涨的压力，尽管努力确保替代零部件的供应链，但价格调整仍不可避免。

Currently, the tech industry as a whole is experiencing a comprehensive parts shortage across everything from smartphones to supercomputers and automotive semiconductors. In particular, the explosive recent surge in AI data center construction is straining supply chains for memory semiconductors and core processors, while even relatively common general-purpose parts are seeing deepening supply-demand imbalances.  
目前，整个科技行业正经历从智能手机到超级计算机再到汽车半导体的全面零部件短缺。特别是近期 AI 数据中心建设的爆发式增长，正在给存储半导体和核心处理器的供应链带来压力，而即使是相对常见的通用零部件，其供需失衡也在加剧。

Bloomberg commented, "Even Qualcomm—one of the core customer companies of Taiwan's TSMC, the world's largest foundry (semiconductor contract manufacturing) firm—has been unable to withstand the cost burdens and has implemented price hikes, raising attention to whether this will lead to price increases in finished products from major smartphone manufacturers going forward."
彭博社评论称，“即便是全球最大晶圆代工企业台湾台积电的核心客户之一高通，也无法承受成本压力而提价，这是否会导致未来各大智能手机制造商成品价格上涨，引发关注。”
