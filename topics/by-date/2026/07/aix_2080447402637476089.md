Tier 3: Advanced Packaging Equipment

This tier has the cleanest rent capture characteristics and the best listed accessibility.

BE Semiconductor, BESI ($BESIY). Approximately 42% of the die attach market and the global leader in hybrid bonding, the technology positioned to succeed current approaches. Already covered extensively in prior KB work. The hybrid bonding leg is less exposed to EMIB-T competition than bridge-architecture suppliers, since EMIB-T competes more directly with CoWoS-L style bridge approaches.

ASMPT (https://t.co/f0gcmunC81, Hong Kong). Market leader in thermo-compression bonding, the incumbent tool for chip-to-wafer applications at TSMC. Roughly 25% of revenue from advanced packaging. Competes with BESI at the bonding tier.

Disco Corporation (6146.T, Tokyo). Approximately 20% of the back-end equipment market, dominant in wafer thinning, grinding, and dicing. Every advanced package passes through these steps regardless of architecture, which makes Disco architecture-agnostic in a way that bridge or bonder suppliers are not. Structurally one of the strongest positions in the tier.

SUSS MicroTec (https://t.co/1N3tR9oHYj, Frankfurt). Temporary bonding and debonding plus UV scanning. Roughly 70% of revenue from advanced packaging, the highest concentration of any listed equipment maker. Already covered in prior KB work.

Camtek ($CAMT, Nasdaq) and Onto Innovation (ONTO, NYSE). Inspection and metrology for advanced packaging. Both US-listed. Onto reportedly holds a higher share of TSMC advanced packaging capacity than Camtek. Camtek derives roughly 60% of revenue from advanced packaging.

Towa (6315.T, Tokyo). Molding and encapsulation equipment. Already covered in prior KB work.

Kulicke and Soffa ($KLIC, Nasdaq). Die and wire bonding, with fluxless TCB entry. The most accessible US-listed bonding name, though positioned lower in the value stack than BESI or ASMPT.

Applied Materials ($AMAT, Nasdaq) and Tokyo Electron (8035.T) both participate in hybrid bonding process steps, though packaging is a modest share of consolidated revenue for each. Shibaura Mechatronics (6590.T) and privately held EV Group round out the bonding tool chain.
