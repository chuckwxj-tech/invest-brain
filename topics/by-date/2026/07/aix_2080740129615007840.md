Just in: Nvidia signs $1.5 billion agreement with Amkor to expand U.S. AI chip packaging capacity.

In response to the explosive growth in demand for artificial intelligence infrastructure and to strengthen the domestic semiconductor supply chain in the United States, chip giant Nvidia and packaging and testing giant Amkor Technology have reached a multi-year strategic cooperation agreement worth US$1.5 billion.

Under the agreement, Nvidia will provide Amkor with an upfront payment to support the expansion of its advanced packaging and test capabilities in the United States, particularly in Arizona.

The two parties will jointly develop advanced packaging technologies for next-generation AI and accelerated computing platforms, focusing on key areas such as high-density interconnection and heterogeneous integration, aiming to integrate different types of chips into a single package to improve the performance and energy efficiency of AI processors.

Currently, advanced packaging has become a key bottleneck for AI chip performance and delivery. in In the context of CoWoS and other production capacity being in short supply, this cooperation is another strategic move by Nvidia to ensure the shipment of its AI processors after locking in the supply of key materials such as memory and glass substrates.

Boosted by the news, Amkor's stock price rose by about 12% to 17% in after-hours trading. This cooperation is one of a series of recent moves by Amkor to consolidate its position in the U.S. semiconductor supply chain. Previously, the company had built advanced packaging and testing facilities in Arizona and signed a ten-year U.S.

packaging cooperation agreement with TSMC in June. It is also working on Provide chip packaging services. The company's CEO Kevin Engel said that the strategic cooperation with NVIDIA highlights the core position of advanced packaging in the future of AI and accelerates the company's long-term technology roadmap.
