## 原文

$KLAC beat earnings and raised its outlook. Why did the stock drop 8% after earnings?

Revenue growth accelerated to +15.2% YoY, up from +11.5% last quarter, and came in above consensus.

Guidance was also ahead of analyst expectations. Management expects Q1 FY2027 revenue of $4.0B ± $200M, above the $3.92B estimate.

However, margins stopped expanding, even though profitability remains very high. Gross margin was 61.0%, down 0.6 percentage points YoY, while operating margin was 42.0%, down 0.1 percentage points YoY.

Diluted shares declined 1.1% YoY, which is positive for investors.

The key question is whether these results justify a high valuation of 36.4x forward P/E.

The market appears to think the answer is no, and the stock fell 8.4% after earnings.

Looking deeper, Semiconductor Process Control revenue increased 13.2% YoY, while PCB and Component Inspection grew 56.5% YoY.

Advanced packaging remains a major growth engine. Management expects $1.1B of advanced-packaging process-control revenue in 2026, implying roughly +70% YoY growth.

Demand is being driven by CoWoS, hybrid bonding, SOIC, die-to-wafer bonding, HBM, EUV adoption in DRAM, and rising complexity in leading-edge logic.

Backlog is expected to reach $12.5B.

Management also expects second-half 2026 revenue to grow +20% versus the first half.

KLA Services also delivered strong results, with revenue up 17% YoY to $820M. Around 80% of service revenue is contract-based, giving the company a more predictable recurring revenue base.

The gross margin decline was mainly explained by a much higher memory mix, which created a 100 basis point headwind. Management expects this pressure to persist through the end of 2027.

KLA remains a clear beneficiary of the current semiconductor manufacturing cycle and rising process complexity. AI infrastructure also requires more inspection, more process control, and more advanced packaging.

Overall, this was a solid quarter with a stronger outlook.
