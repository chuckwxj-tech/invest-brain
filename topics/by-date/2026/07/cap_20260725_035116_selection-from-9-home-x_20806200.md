Storage just stole the show.
存储功能简直抢尽了风头。

Between KV cache offload and SSD prices going vertical, storage went from afterthought to headliner. Vik Malyala walks through 
@Supermicro
's storage stack.
由于 KV 缓存功能的淘汰以及 SSD 价格的飞涨，存储问题已经从原本被忽视的环节，变成了备受关注的重点。Vik Malyala 详细介绍了 @Supermicro 的存储解决方案。

"Storage, whether it be for KV cache offload or just the incredible rise in cost of SSDs, is the primary thing to focus on for a lot of users."
对于许多用户来说，存储问题至关重要——无论是为了将数据从内存中缓存出来，还是因为固态硬盘的成本不断上升。

"Initially it started with the form factors. You have U.2s that were popular, or still are popular for that matter. Then you have E1.S and E3.S drives, mainly because of the density that we can bring in a 1U or 2U form factor. And we have come up with so-called Petascale solutions, which you can get a very good balance between the amount of storage, the storage bandwidth as IO bandwidth."
最初，这一切都是从存储设备的物理形态开始的。比如，U.2 规格的存储设备曾经很受欢迎，现在依然如此。此外，还有 E1.S 和 E3.S 规格的存储设备，它们之所以受欢迎，主要是因为它们能在 1U 或 2U 的物理空间内提供极高的存储密度。我们还推出了所谓的“拍字节级存储解决方案”，这种方案能够在存储容量、存储带宽以及 I/O 带宽之间实现理想的平衡。

"Hardware is only one part of it, but what we are doing is working with the likes of VAST, WEKA, DDN, Qumulo, OSNEXUS and every one of these software vendors. We are able to bring different types of software defined storage into customer environments so that you don't necessarily get stuck with traditional storage. So super exciting times for that."
“硬件只是其中的一部分而已。我们所做的，是与 VAST、WEKA、DDN、Qumulo、OSNEXUS 等软件供应商合作。这样，我们就能将各种类型的软件定义存储方案引入客户的系统中，从而避免客户不得不继续使用传统的存储方式。这真是令人兴奋的时刻。”
