INTEL IS MOVING TOWARD MEMORY WHILE SAMSUNG PUSHES BACK IN FOUNDRY — AI IS BLURRING THE OLD CHIP INDUSTRY LINES

TIL: Intel is strengthening the compute-memory connection through EMIB, chiplets and CXL, while Samsung is using tight HBM supply to pitch customers a HBM + foundry + advanced packaging turnkey stack.

Intel’s move isn’t a return to making DRAM or HBM wafers. It’s about owning more of the system around memory, and hiring former SK Hynix CEO Lee Seok-hee to lead advanced packaging, backend manufacturing and system integration fits directly into that strategy.

Samsung is coming from the opposite direction. With HBM demand rising and its foundry business nearing breakeven, it wants AI customers to source memory and logic together. Basically, Intel and Samsung are starting from different places but increasingly competing for the same thing — more of the full AI system.
