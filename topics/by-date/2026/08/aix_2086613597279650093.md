## 原文

This is one I’m especially excited about.

I first started paying close attention to $ALAB in October 2025, when Astera Labs announced the acquisition of aiXscale Photonics.

My post at the time was titled:

“$ALAB: I smell Silicon Photonics / CPO.”

https://t.co/8d6k8peJpK

What caught my attention was aiXscale’s fiber-to-chip coupling and photonic packaging technology.

I saw the acquisition as a sign that Astera was looking beyond electrical connectivity and toward Optical I/O and scale-up networking.

At the time, very few people in my X community were looking at $ALAB from a photonics angle.

Now Gavin is talking about scale-up networking in a memory-constrained world, CXL, switches, and the intersection with optics.

Really looking forward to hearing Jitendra Mohan’s thoughts on all of this at @TomorrowXSummit.
