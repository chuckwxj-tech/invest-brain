# TSMC is reportedly in talks to acquire two AUO factories in the Central Taiwan Science Park for advanced packaging expansion. Market sources say the company is evaluating AUO’s ...

## 原文

TSMC is reportedly in talks to acquire two AUO factories in the Central Taiwan Science Park for advanced packaging expansion.

Market sources say the company is evaluating AUO’s L7 (7.5-generation) and L5C (5-generation) fabs, which sit adjacent to TSMC’s existing CTSP facilities. 

The potential transaction is estimated at more than NT$30 billion and would rank among the highest prices paid for older-generation panel plants in Taiwan in recent years.

The cooperation is described as following the earlier “Innolux model,” in which TSMC partners with a panel maker to accelerate advanced packaging. 

The focus would be fan-out panel-level packaging and especially CoPoS, TSMC’s planned next-generation 2.5D platform that uses large rectangular glass substrates instead of circular silicon interposers. 

The approach aims to improve area utilization, cost and warpage control for very large AI packages.

TSMC’s first CoPoS line at its AP7 facility in Chiayi is already complete and in equipment and materials validation, starting with 310 × 310 mm panels. Commercial maturity is expected to take roughly a year. 

Panel makers bring existing expertise in large-substrate handling, equipment coordination and production-line management that can shorten the learning curve for high-volume manufacturing.

Older panel fabs also offer scarce large cleanroom space and substantial power allocations at a time when industrial land and electricity are constrained in Taiwan. Advanced packaging players have been competing for such assets.

If completed, the deal would give TSMC additional nearby capacity for CoPoS while allowing AUO to convert under-utilized panel assets into higher-value semiconductor packaging activity.
