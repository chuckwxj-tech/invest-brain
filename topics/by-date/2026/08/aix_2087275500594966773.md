$LITE Q4 2026 earnings: Explosive AI Growth Eclipsed by a Massive Debt Equitization Charge

Lumentum has crossed a historic milestone, delivering over $1 billion in quarterly revenue (+109% YoY) and guiding to $1.25 billion for Q1 FY27, hitting its target model quarters ahead of schedule. The underlying operations are accelerating violently, driven by AI data center demand for 1.6T transceivers and EMLs, which pushed non-GAAP operating margins to a staggering 36.6%. However, investors must stomach a severe GAAP reality: the company recorded a $7.8 billion non-cash loss on debt extinguishment due to the equitization of convertible notes, resulting in a $7.2 billion GAAP net loss and massive share dilution.
