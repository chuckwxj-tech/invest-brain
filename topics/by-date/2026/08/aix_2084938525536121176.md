HANMI SEMICONDUCTOR BUILT ITS LEAD BEFORE THE HBM BOOM: NOW IT IS EXPANDING BEYOND TC BONDERS

*Hanmi Semi is the global leader in HBM stacking equipment.

TIL: Hanmi Semiconductor held more than 70% of the HBM TC-bonder market last year after entering the field with SK Hynix back in 2017. Q2 revenue reached a record ₩251.1 billion ($176.2 million), and the company is now moving into hybrid bonding, 2.5D packaging, GDDR and high-bandwidth flash equipment.

Hanmi is investing ₩57.8 billion ($40.6 million) in a dedicated hybrid-bonder factory scheduled to start operating in the first half of next year. It also plans to launch a wider TC bonder as customers move toward more advanced HBM stacks.

Chairman Kwak Dong-shin has personally bought nearly ₩69.5 billion ($48.8 million) of shares since 2023, while the company invested ₩50 billion ($35.1 million) in SpaceX and is preparing a US subsidiary. The bet is no longer just on HBM. It is on becoming a broader AI packaging-equipment supplier.
