## 原文

According to Taiwanese media reports, AI demand has set off a race to raise prices and a scramble for volume in MLCCs.
据台湾媒体报道，AI 需求在 MLCC 市场掀起了涨价潮和抢产能竞赛。

Word in the industry is that some customers are offering two to three times the going price to major passive component makers such as Yageo (2327) and Murata Manufacturing just to lock down enough supply.
业界传言，一些客户正以现行价格的两到三倍向国巨（2327）和村田制作所等主要被动元件厂商下单，只为锁定足够的供货量。

Yageo, for its part, acknowledged that "MLCC demand really is very strong."
国巨方面则坦言，“MLCC 需求确实非常强劲。”

Another notable shift is customers racing to lock in long term contracts.
另一个值得注意的变化是，客户正争相锁定长期合约。

Yageo said AI related customers are increasingly moving to secure passive component supply for the next six months, and in some cases several years out, through long term contracts in order to reduce supply chain risk.
国巨表示，AI 相关客户正越来越多地转向通过长期合约来确保未来六个月、部分情况下甚至长达数年的被动元件供应，以降低供应链风险。

Shipments of high specification applications such as AI servers and data centers are ramping quickly, so demand for high capacity, high reliability products is surging. Large customers generally receive higher priority in capacity allocation, while the volumes available to small and midsize customers keep shrinking. Customers with thin inventories of their own, worried that a component shortfall will disrupt shipments of their finished products, have little choice but to bid higher to secure the limited supply on offer.
高规格应用（如 AI 服务器和数据中心）的发货量正在迅速攀升，因此对高容量、高可靠性产品的需求激增。大客户通常在产能分配中获得更高优先级，而中小企业可获得的供货量持续缩减。那些自身库存单薄的客户，担心元器件短缺会扰乱其成品的出货，别无选择只能抬高报价以锁定市场上有限的供应。
