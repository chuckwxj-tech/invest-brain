## 原文

I’m honestly surprised this post has reached 270K views. After reading it, many investors and engineers reached out to me. Thank you. I hope I can meet many of you soon.

Some people also told me that the article was difficult to fully understand without a strong technical background.

So here is a short summary and interpretation for them.

1. Due to supply constraints and other factors, some customers are currently considering reducing HBM4 stack height from 12 high to 8 high.

2. There is growing discussion that bandwidth may be a bigger bottleneck than memory density.

3. There are also clear efforts to compensate for limited density through system architecture. NVIDIA’s NPO architecture, SK hynix’s CPO research, and the Marvell and SK hynix CXL collaboration are examples of this direction.

4. This absolutely does not mean that HBM is going away. The architecture will continue to evolve in a way that balances DRAM supply and demand while maximizing value for both customers and memory vendors.

5. My core investment thesis is that the investment logic is moving in this sequence:

Compute → Memory → Optics
