Pluggable vs CPO, Optical Communication Big Three Fully Summarized
  可插拔 vs CPO，光通信三大技术全面解析

1. A breakdown of the differences between Pluggable and CPO that many people are curious about.
- Pluggable, which is $AAOI's main focus, involves plugging finger-sized modules into the front of the switch. Since electrical signals have to travel a long distance across the board, there's a lot of signal loss and heat generation.
很多人好奇的 Pluggable 与 CPO 之间的区别解析。
- Pluggable 是 $AAOI 的主要研究方向，将手指大小的模块插入交换机正面。由于电信号需要在电路板上长距离传输，因此会产生大量信号损耗和热量。

- On the other hand, CPO attaches the optical engine right next to the switch chip. $LITE supplies the external laser module (ELS) here that shoots out the light. With the signal distance being so short, power efficiency is off the charts.
- 另一方面，CPO 将光学引擎直接放置在交换芯片旁边。 $LITE 在此提供发射光线的外部激光模块（ELS）。由于信号传输距离极短，能效表现极其出色。

2. So, did $AAOI skip the first generation because it lacks CPO technology?
"Not at all—$AAOI's laser technology is at the top of the industry."
2. 那么， $AAOI 是因为缺乏 CPO 技术而跳过了第一代吗？
一点也不会——$AAOI 的激光技术处于行业顶尖水平。

- They've already run all the tests with big tech companies. The issue was just laser factory capacity. They went all-in on lasers for their own 800G/1.6T finished modules, which are raking in money right now, so they ceded the first generation. They're ramping up expansion at their Texas factory right now, so once it's complete, they plan to fully enter the second-generation CPO market.
- 他们早就和大型科技公司完成了所有测试。问题只在于激光器工厂的产能。他们把全部精力都押注在自家 800G/1.6T 成品模块的激光器上，这些产品目前正在大把赚钱，所以第一代市场就拱手让人了。他们现在正在德州工厂全力扩产，等扩产完成后，计划全面进入第二代 CPO 市场。

3. The key reason Lumentum, Coherent, and $AAOI are tied together.
- It's because they're among the few companies in the world that own their own indium phosphide (InP) laser fabs and can design and manufacture chips in-house. Whether it's selling finished products like $AAOI or CPO laser components like $LITE, they all share the common thread of being core beneficiaries responsible for the internal speeds of AI data centers.
3. Lumentum、Coherent 和 $AAOI 之所以绑在一起，关键原因在于此。
- 这是因为它们都是全球少数几家拥有自家磷化铟（InP）激光器工厂、能够自主设计和制造芯片的公司。无论是像 $AAOI 那样销售成品，还是像 $LITE 那样提供 CPO 激光器组件，它们都有一个共同点——都是负责 AI 数据中心内部传输速率的核心受益者。

 In summary, $AAOI is grabbing short-term earnings with 800G finished products, and after the Texas fab is complete, it'll level up as a second-generation CPO laser supplier. Whether Nvidia adopts CPO or sticks with Pluggable, the story of the optical communication big three holding their own InP fabs is one that has to keep going alongside the expansion of AI data centers.
  综上所述， $AAOI 正通过 800G 成品抢占短期收益，待得州晶圆厂建成后，将升级为第二代 CPO 激光器供应商。无论 Nvidia 采用 CPO 还是坚持 Pluggable 方案，光通信三巨头自持 InP 晶圆厂的故事，都必将随着 AI 数据中心的扩张而持续推进。
