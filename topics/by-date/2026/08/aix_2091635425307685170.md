## 原文

@optionscjp You’re not just getting one thing. You’re getting exposure to:

Custom silicon:  they’re designing the actual accelerators for hyperscalers. Multiple programs already shipping, more ramping, and management keeps talking about a $10B+ custom run-rate target by FY29. That’s real volume, not just design wins on a slide.

Interconnect: this is the quiet monster right now. Optical DSPs (they’re roughly 60-65% share), TIAs, drivers, DCI modules… basically the chips that sit inside every high-speed optical link moving data between GPUs, switches, and racks. As models get more mixture-of-experts and reasoning-heavy, traffic explodes and this stuff flies. Guidance for interconnect is now +70%+ in FY27.

Photonics:  they already had a strong base from the Inphi days, and then they went and bought Celestial AI for the Photonic Fabric platform. That’s the scale-up optical interconnect play (co-packaged optics, near-packaged, chiplet-level optical I/O). Copper is hitting hard physical limits inside the rack; optics is the next step and they’re positioned early with actual silicon and hyperscaler engagement.

Add in the Ethernet switching and the newer PCIe/CXL stuff from XConn and you’ve basically got a company that touches custom compute, the wires between the compute, the optics that will replace a lot of those wires, and the switches that stitch it all together.

Data center is already ~75% of revenue and still accelerating. Feels like one of the cleaner pure-plays on “AI infrastructure beyond just the GPU itself.”

Not financial advice, just how I’m looking at it.
