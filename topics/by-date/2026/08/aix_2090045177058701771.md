## 原文

LG Display (@lgdisplayoled )
just unveiled FLiPP (FMM-Less innovative Pixel Patterning), a breakthrough that could solve OLED’s biggest manufacturing bottleneck.

For over a decade, OLED manufacturing has relied on Fine Metal Masks (FMM) essentially micro-stencils that are costly, limit panel sizes due to metal sagging, and restrict glass substrate efficiency.

FLiPP replaces mechanical masks with UV photolithography patterning.

LG claims following performance leap:
 🔆 +60% Brightness (1.6x)
 ⏳ +140% Lifespan (2.4x)
 ⚡ -13% Power Consumption
 📐 +55% Aperture Ratio

-8.5th-Gen Full Glass Processing: 

Enables single whole-piece 8.5G mother glass production without dividing substrates thus boosting utilization efficiency by up to 64%.

-Total Scalability: 

Eliminates size/resolution constraints, scaling seamlessly from 1-inch micro-displays (AR/VR/wearables) up to 100-inch ultra-large TVs.

A massive moment for RGB OLED production economics and display longevity across consumer tech.📱💻📺

Might bring some reprieve for struggling Display makers being squeezed due to Chipflation
