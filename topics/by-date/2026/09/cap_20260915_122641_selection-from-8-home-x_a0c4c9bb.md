YMTC Begins Full Scale Equipment Hookup at Wuhan Fab 3, Accelerating Toward Year End Start of Operations  

Yangtze Memory Technologies Co. (YMTC), China's largest NAND flash maker, has started connecting production equipment at its third fab in Wuhan (FAB3). With preparations for bringing the fab online entering the final stage, initial operation by the end of the year is coming into view.  

According to Chinese procurement networks on the 14th, China Electronics System Engineering No. 2 Construction Co. (CESE No. 2), a state owned contractor under China Electronics Corporation (CEC), recently began selecting a construction firm for "Gas Section 3" of the "Wuhan YMTC FAB3 Secondary Piping Zone A, Package B Process Equipment Piping General Contracting Project."  

The work is secondary piping, which connects the utility supply network already built into the fab, including gas and water, to individual pieces of production equipment. Gas piping delivers the specialty gases used in semiconductor manufacturing steps such as etching and deposition to the production tools.  

This is read as YMTC expanding the work needed to bring already installed production equipment into operation. Bidders were required to have a track record on projects affiliated with YMTC or Chinese DRAM maker CXMT, or on a 12 inch semiconductor fab of equivalent scale.
这被解读为长江存储正在扩大将已安装的生产设备投入运行所需的工作。投标人必须拥有与长江存储或中国 DRAM 制造商长鑫存储相关项目的业绩记录，或拥有同等规模的 12 英寸半导体晶圆厂项目业绩。

YMTC's Wuhan Fab 3 is reportedly set to begin initial operation at the end of this year. At full capacity, output is understood to be around 100,000 wafers per month on a 12 inch basis. After initial production, capacity is expected to be expanded in stages through further equipment move in and yield stabilization.
据报道，长江存储的武汉 Fab 3 将于今年年底开始初步运营。满产时，按 12 英寸晶圆计算，产量据信约为每月 10 万片。初期生产后，预计将通过进一步搬入设备和稳定良率来分阶段扩大产能。

The Fab 3 expansion draws attention because it coincides with YMTC's rapid market share gains. According to Counterpoint Research, YMTC accounted for 14% of global NAND bit shipments in the second quarter of this year, overtaking Kioxia to become the world's No. 3. Samsung Electronics ranked first at 25% and SK hynix second at 22%.
Fab 3 扩建之所以引人关注，是因为它正值长江存储市场份额快速上升之际。根据 Counterpoint Research 的数据，今年第二季度，长江存储占全球 NAND 位元出货量的 14%，超过铠侠，成为全球第三。三星电子以 25%排名第一，SK 海力士以 22%排名第二。

Once YMTC brings its new Wuhan capacity fully online, supply competition in the NAND market is expected to intensify further. In particular, since YMTC has been securing new capacity by raising the share of Chinese made equipment despite US export controls on advanced semiconductor equipment, attention is on whether it will close the gap with incumbent NAND makers such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix at a faster pace.
一旦长江存储（YMTC）在武汉的新产能全面投产，NAND 市场的供应竞争预计将进一步加剧。尤其是，尽管美国对先进半导体设备实施出口管制，YMTC 仍通过提高国产设备占比来获取新产能，因此外界关注它是否会以更快的速度缩小与三星电子、SK 海力士等现有 NAND 制造商之间的差距。

An industry source said, "YMTC's capacity expansion is unlikely to lead to a NAND oversupply right away," but added, "Because it is growing output and share on the back of China's domestic market, it could become a medium to long term competitive burden for the existing NAND makers."
一位行业消息人士表示：“YMTC 的产能扩张不太可能立即导致 NAND 供应过剩”，但补充道，“由于它依托中国国内市场扩大产量和份额，这可能会成为现有 NAND 制造商中长期面临的竞争负担。”
